<p>
Unlike other types of lumber that are made from various types of wood, bamboo lumber is made from the bamboo plant, which is actually a type of grass and incredibly durable, it’s one of the most sustainable building materials around. The bamboo material is usually compressed into sheets that can be made into pieces of lumber, bamboo strips are laid up lengthwise, having all the layers running the length direction.
</p>
<p>
<br />
</p>
<p>
Bamboo lumber offers a beautiful, unique appearance, adds durability over other popular options, is a popular choice for various applications, for example, interior application, building support, furniture making, though this type of lumber can be somewhat expensive. Bamboo is a renewable resource, which means this type of lumber tends to be fairly eco-friendly.
</p>
