<p>
Bamboo tile is small cut from outdoor strand woven bamboo block, same material of decking production, one of best, strongest, sustainable material for exterior and outdoor. Strand woven bamboo derived directly from the pole of the Moso bamboo plant which has a tensile strength superior to cold rolled steel and a compressive strength higher than concrete. Tile has 2 parts, top is strand woven bamboo, bottom is plastic platform.
</p>
<p>
<br />
</p>
<p>
Bamboo tile has wide applications, for garden, park, bathroom, which need a material with long lasting, no moldy, antibacterial, antimicrobial and anti-fungal.
</p>
<p>
<br />
</p>
<p>
Strand Woven Bamboo Tile Manufacturing Process:
</p>
<p>
<br />
</p>
<p>
Step 1: Dried bamboo poles are shredded into fine strips, dissecting and opening the cell structure of the plant.
</p>
<p>
<br />
</p>
<p>
Step 2: The bamboo strips are immersed in non-toxic, water based glue, which fills and encapsulates the exposed cells.
</p>
<p>
<br />
</p>
<p>
Step 3: A precisely measured quantity of bamboo strips is fused together under the high pressure of a 2500-ton cold press, producing blocks similar in size and shape to railroad ties. The cold press uses no heat and no toxic or harmful chemicals.
</p>
<p>
<br />
</p>
<p>
Step 4: The solid block is left to stabilize in a resting position for 30 days before being processed into flooring material.
</p>
<p>
<br />
</p>
<p>
Step 5: Slice the woven block into flooring planks, sand the planks to be smooth, mill the grooves on the surface, put the oil on.
</p>
<p>
<br />
</p>
<p>
Step 6: Assemble the bamboo with the plastic platform.
</p>
