Horizontal grain is flat-pressed style, bamboo strips are placed into three layers on top of one another, horizontal displays the natural nodes or knuckles of the bamboo. These nodes are randomly visible on the surface, adding to the natural beauty of the floor. Horizontal features more of the unique properties of the bamboo grass, with more visible nodes (the characteristic joint in the bamboo cane).

<a href="http://www.bambooflooringchina.com/products/product-type-BambooFlooring.html" target="_blank"><strong>Engineered bamboo flooring</strong></a> is a type of flooring that made up of a bamboo top layer laminated to multiple layers of plywood, 3 layers in total, face layer is bamboo (horizontal or vertical), core and bottom layers are wood. <p>
Engineered Bamboo Flooring Manufacturing:
</p>
<p>
<br />
</p>
<p>
Step 1: Bamboo trees are harvested and cut into strips, physical treatment like boiling, drying, carbonizing, cutting, sorting. Beautiful and quality bamboo strips are well-prepared.
</p>
<p>
<br />
</p>
<p>
Step 2: Glue bamboo strips with healthy, non-toxic, low VOC adhesive, laminate strips flatly into single layer of bamboo sheet in 800 tons pressure.
</p>
<p>
<br />
</p>
<p>
Step 3: Condition wood and plywood into low moisture content, prevent bending and cupping after laminating with bamboo wear layer. This conditioning takes 20-30 days to dry down the moisture of wood.
</p>
<p>
<br />
</p>
<p>
Step 4: Press bamboo with wood in 12 layers of presser with hot steam, cut big board into flooring plank, sand the board to be smooth, mill standard tongue+groove.
</p>
<p>
<br />
</p>
<p>
Step 5: Lacquer the flooring with six coats of the highest quality UV cured durable urethane, anti-scratch, wear resistant. Sort out bamboo flooring with color tone and pack individually.
</p>
