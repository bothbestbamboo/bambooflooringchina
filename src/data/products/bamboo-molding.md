Bamboo molding is available in styles both classic and strand woven, classic means horizontal and vertical which are standard and common profile and style for bamboo flooring. Strand woven is brand new style and growing most fast in the market. To match bamboo flooring either classic or strand woven, we produce the molding and trims in the same grain and color, this is to ensure the client can have flooring with perfect match with molding.

We offer just about every type of molding and trim you can imagine, all designed to complement your existing wood floors as well as your current home style and decor. Bamboo moldings are used to cover any uneven cuts along the bottom of a wall, to be the transition between wall with floor.

Our bamboo moldings and trim add a professional, finished look to your home. All of our moldings and trim pieces match the bamboo flooring we sell. Bamboo moldings are available for all bamboo floors, both classic (horizontal, vertical) and strand woven, natural and caramel color.
