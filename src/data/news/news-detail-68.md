<p><p><span style="font-family: Verdana;">
Nowadays, there are too many floors for people to select for home or office decoration, for example, it is common to see solid wood flooring, solid wood composite flooring, strengthening composite flooring and <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com">bamboo flooring</a>. All these flooring have their own advantages and disadvantages, therefore when making purchase decision, people have to weigh their merits and demerits, this is the exact article about comparison between bamboo flooring and solid wood flooring, which is useful for you to make wise decision.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;">
Firstly, about stability
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
Bamboo flooring has its advantage on stability because it is not easy to be shrank and expand, however, please pay attention on the durability of bamboo flooring, if you want to lengthen its working life, you’d better avoid these two aspects, on the one hand, bamboo flooring should not be under the blazing sun for quite a long time or under too much moist environment, on the other hand, it is easy to be damaged by worms, especially in south area.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;">
Secondly, about comfort degree
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
I am sure lots of people choose bamboo flooring for decoration because of its remarkable comfort degree. When paving bamboo flooring at room, people will feel that it is cool in hot summer and so warm in cold winter, do you know the reason? Bamboo raw material has low heat conductivity coefficient so it can not release cold air or warm air by itself. No mater the seasons, people can walk on bamboo flooring even without wearing shoes and socks, it is more comfortable.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;">
Thirdly, about color and lustre
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
From the aspect of artistic outlook, bamboo flooring is more monotonous because the bamboo raw material itself doesn’t have abundant color and lustre, it is not suitable for diversified decoration style at the beginning, however, this problem has ceased to exist because of dyeing technology used in bamboo flooring manufacture. As for solid wood flooring, which is mainly made from natural wood, there is no such problem on color and lustre. If you are the people who don’t like chromatic aberration, bamboo flooring should be your wise choice.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;">
Finally, about environmental protection factor
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
Believe it or not, in the long term, bamboo flooring would be the development tendency in home or office decoration industry because of its environmentally friendly advantage.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;">
As is known to all, the growth cycle of bamboo is too much less than that of trees, in other words, bamboo can be considered as sustainable resource because only in a few years, bamboo can grow up and then used as material. As for solid wood flooring, its raw material is the trees which have grown at least thirty years, so it will be less and less, more and more precious in the future.
</span></p>
