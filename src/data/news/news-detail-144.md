<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Horizontal bamboo parquet: the bamboo stalks have been arranged in length on their widest edges, in order to naturally create the blade by compression of several layers (3 alternating layers in general) superimposed. <p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Vertical bamboo flooring: it consists of a single layer of bamboo rods placed on their thinnest edges, to create the blade by their broad side, leaving finer and more delicate ribs to appear.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">

<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>

<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Other factors that affect bamboo flooring cost</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">1- Types of work</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">To get a new floor, you have two choices: refurbish your existing floor or install new materials. If you opt for a refurbishment, certain techniques can achieve very satisfactory results (pushing and refinishing of the floor, cleaning of the marble, etc.) at a lower cost.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">2- Area to be covered</span></strong></span>

<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Renovating your floor does not necessarily mean changing all the floors in your home. You may want to change only the parquet flooring in the living room or the tiled flooring of the bathroom. This will affect the amount of materials needed and the length of time the installation takes place. Thus, the larger the area to be renovated, the more the price will increase proportionately!</span>
</span></p>

Indeed, there are several types of materials such as hardwood, ceramic, marble, vinyl or carpet, whose prices are very variable within each of the categories.</span>
