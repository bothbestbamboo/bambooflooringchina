<p><p><span style="font-family: Verdana;">
Although there are different types of hardwood flooring available in market, in this age of going green bamboo flooring is definitely major player in hardwood flooring. However not many people are concerned about maintenance and taking care of their already installed bamboo flooring. In case if you want to know about the best ways of taking care of your <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com">bamboo floor</a> then just keep on reading.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<strong>Taking Care of Your Bamboo Floor</strong>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
Compared to other types of flooring cleaning and maintaining bamboo flooring is relatively easy.&nbsp; Bamboo being durable, string and relatively harder compared to other hardwood material bamboo is good water resistant providing you much flexibility in maintaining its look and color. Still, you need to undertake some regular maintenance and cleaning tasks that will help you keep you bamboo floor looking new and astonishing.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
Cleaning Dust, Dirt and Grit: The only thing that can damage look and feel of your bamboo floor is tiny dust particles spread on floor through shoes or by other means. When we press or dig down these tiny particles they usually, intervene with the natural finish and smoothness of the flooring. Dents, divots and scratches are commonly caused by such particles due to which your floor might get faded look. Regular sweeping, mopping or vacuuming your floor will nullify this possibility. Although you can perform these tasks periodically if the floor is experiencing more foot traffic then it is recommended that cleaning or mopping your floor on daily basis is good. However avoiding vacuum cleaner with beater bars, sweeps with hard bristles and avoiding use of too much water will make sure you bamboo flowing looks clean and stays durable.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
Cleaning Strand Woven Bamboo Flooring: Although you can use any hardwood floor cleaner to clean bamboo floor it is recommended that you prefer using non-alkaline and non-abrasive cleaning material. You are aware that bamboo is much water resistant but even bamboo is susceptible to warping if enough moisture is left onboard bamboo floors. Therefore, following all the necessary directions with utmost care when you are using cleaning agents that need to be added in the water is much necessary. Never use steel wool, rather prefer using sponge or clean rag for cleaning bamboo floors. After finished make sure to mop the excess moisture and water from the floor using dry towel or any other material.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
Bamboo Floor Cleaning Solutions: With increasing popularity of bamboo flooring various companies have came up with cleaning agents specially formulated for bamboo floors. You can use any of such products or by mixing vinegar in to water; you can safely clean your bamboo floor.
</span></p>
