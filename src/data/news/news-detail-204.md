<p><p><font face="宋体">
</font><font face="Verdana"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">According to the incomplete statistics of China Forestry Products Industry Association, in 2020, the total annual sales volume of flooring products of enterprises of a certain scale in China will be about 903 million square meters, a year-on-year increase of 1.35%. Among them, the wood and <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com">bamboo flooring</a> is about 412 million square meters, the stone wood plastic (shijing) floor is about 417 million square meters, and the wood plastic floor is about 74 million square meters.
</span></font></p>
<font face="Verdana">
</font>
<p><br>
<font face="Verdana">
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">1. Wood and Bamboo Flooring
</span></font></p>
<font face="Verdana">
</font>
<p><br>
<font face="Verdana">
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">In 2020, the total sales of wood and bamboo flooring in China is about 411.7 million square meters, a year-on-year decrease of about 3.04%, including the sales of laminate flooring of 199 million square meters, a year-on-year decrease of 7.96%; the sales of solid wood composite floors of 138 million square meters, a year-on-year increase of 8.66%; the sales of solid wood floors of 4100 10,000 square meters, a year-on-year decrease of 12.02%; bamboo flooring sales of 28.6 million square meters, a year-on-year decrease of 5.30%; other floor sales of 5.1 million square meters, a year-on-year increase of 10.87%.
</span></font></p>
<font face="Verdana">
</font>
<p><br>
<font face="Verdana">
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">2. Stone wood plastic floor (Shijing floor)
</span></font></p>
<font face="Verdana">
</font>
<p><br>
<font face="Verdana">
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The total sales volume of stone-wood-plastic floor (Shijing floor) is about 417 million square meters, a year-on-year increase of 5.30%. The export volume of stone crystal flooring accounts for about 95.00% of the total.
</span></font></p>
<font face="Verdana">
</font>
<p><br>
<font face="Verdana">
<span style="font-size: 12pt;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">3.&nbsp;wood plastic floor
</span></span></font></p>
<font face="Verdana">
</font>
<p><br>
<font face="Verdana">
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The sales volume of wood-plastic floors was about 74 million square meters, a year-on-year increase of 5.71%. Exports of wood-plastic flooring account for about 60% of the total. Outdoor wood-plastic flooring accounts for about 95% of the total.
</span></font></p>
<font face="Verdana">
</font>
<p><br>
<font face="Verdana">
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">In 2020, the wood and bamboo flooring industry presents the following characteristics: </span></font></p>
<font face="Verdana">
</font>
<p><span style="font-size: 12pt;"><br>
<font face="Verdana">
</font></span></p>
<font face="Verdana">
</font>
<p><span style="font-size: 12pt;"><font face="Verdana">1. The domestic market is affected by the epidemic, and the sales of wood and bamboo flooring have declined; </font></span></p>
<font face="Verdana">
</font>
<p><span style="font-size: 12pt;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;"><br>
<font face="Verdana">
</font></span></span></p>
<font face="Verdana">
</font>
<p><span style="font-size: 12pt;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;"><font face="Verdana">2. The differentiation of domestic sales channels has become more and more significant, and the sales volume of engineering channels has continued to grow, driving the sales growth of parquet; retail channels The decrease in sales led to a decrease in the sales of solid wood flooring and laminate flooring. </font></span></span></p>
<font face="Verdana">
</font>

<p><font face="宋体"><span style="font-size: 12pt;"><font face="Verdana">3. The epidemic has little impact on the export volume of flooring, and the sales volume of Shijing flooring and wood-plastic flooring increased slightly.</font></span></font></p></p>
