<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">In this article, we want to introduce you to the fashionable and up-to-date designs for flooring this year. The use of various materials of various colors enables customers to choose their unique design at an affordable price. &nbsp;</span></span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Every year at specialized exhibitions, manufacturers’ present new collections of decors, and designers analyze and highlight the most relevant shades, patterns, styles, etc. Floor coverings are not exceptions! After all, sex is a very important part of interior design. From what you choose the floor, will depend on the comfort, the general appearance of the room, its visual size and much more.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Most laminate buyers, when choosing, pay attention only to the cost and basic characteristics. But to get aesthetic pleasure from buying and enjoying the result, we want to draw your attention to the current designs of laminate and other floor coverings.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">1. New - a well-forgotten old</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Wooden floor has always been, and more than one decade will be one of the most topical coatings. It was not able to supersede even new items, such as flootex and </span><span style="font-size: 12pt;"><a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com">bamboo floor</a></span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">.</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;"> However, preference is given to wide boards with a pronounced rough texture. Rustic, with its knots, tangential cut and wavy figure of the rings - is an ideal example of such a floor.</span></span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Here are such uneven, undulating boards with a pronounced texture today at the height of fashion. They will perfectly fit into a cozy home interior and meet the general trend of using natural materials</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Rustic is one of the varieties of a wooden floor, differs by a rough texture and looks somewhat rustic</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">A vivid example of how aristocratic the wooden floor may look. Especially in combination with such laconic furniture and restrained, poorly painted interior</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">In this case, light wood was chosen for the floor, but the texture itself remained as brightly expressed</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">2. We paint the floor fashionably</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Designers claim that despite the wide popularity of laminate and linoleum, the painted floor is gaining popularity again. Variants of patterns and shades are available in the market and it will not be difficult and costly to update the floor.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The main trends: vintage shabby, artificially aged floor, combination of colored bright floor with neutral walls, as well as Moroccan and Greek patterns that are ideal for ethnic interiors.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">3. Inserts from glass blocks</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">In the past, you could only admire the transparent floor in the shopping center, and now you can order such coverage home. Inserts from glass blocks, often with patterns, are widely used in home interior. An excellent option, in order to highlight the zone in the room or simply emphasize and pay attention to the beauty of your sexes.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">4. Actual tile</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Vinyl tiles with a wide selection of shades and patterns give a unique opportunity for designers to experiment with interior design. The most popular are the tiles under the wooden, leather and stone floor. And also it is worth noting geometric large patterns of 2-3 colors, which can be found in catalogs or fantasy itself.&nbsp;</span></span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">5. Parquet - fashionable classics</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Parquet is considered one of the most expensive varieties of coatings. It has several ways of laying. Basically it depends on the design idea.&nbsp;</span></span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">However, now the most popular way of laying-laying herringbone (an attempt to reproduce the palace interiors of Versailles and Trianon). Such a floor will look beautiful, rich and respectable. And those who are bored, or do not like the French fir-tree, can try asymmetrical parquet, an unusual and modern option.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">6. Combination of materials</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">In multi-functional rooms using two different types of coatings is an excellent opportunity to identify individual zones. For example, a kitchen area to lay out with tiles, and a guest room - linoleum or laminate. However, with this laying, there may be slight difficulties, but experienced masters will always cope.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Main trends: a combination of natural stone and wood, ceramic tiles and parquet.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">7. Digital printing on the floor</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The actual way of decoration for today. It is applied to any coating and imitates any of your wishes. It is possible to successfully imitate wood, stone, tile and so on.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Thus, summing up, we can say, all the fashion is aimed at using environmental materials, original accents, and natural shades with particular details. We will always be happy to help you and decide on a fashionable design for your gender.</span></span></p>
