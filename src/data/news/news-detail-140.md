<p><p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Recently, <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com">bamboo flooring</a> are increasingly used for the installation of floors in rooms of various purposes. Basically, this is all due to the fact that the bamboo covering is a safe natural material, which in its basic properties is very close to wood.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Like traditional types of wooden floors, bamboo is very versatile and, in fact, does not differ much on the floor from a wooden parquet floor with a well-finished surface or from a three-layered parquet board.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">There is a huge variety of solid bamboo flooring of various types and colors from different manufacturers all over the world. In the Ukrainian market, this type of sex is represented, mainly, by Chinese manufacturers.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Numerous distribution companies and dealers offer their customers the widest choice of bamboo of various sizes, colors and types of surface finish. Basically, such coatings are delivered in the form of boards with a trimmed front surface. They can be easily installed using either an adhesive or nailing method or a floating kit.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo flooring is the ideal solution for those who want to have rich and elegant wooden floors, but cannot afford it because of their high cost. Bamboo cannot be attributed to the category of wood materials. Actually, bamboo, like a plant, is characterized as a type of grass, but the floors from it have the same beauty and warmth as the wooden floors. At the same time, bamboo floors have a much longer service life and a higher resistance to moisture.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The durability of solid bamboo floors is their huge advantage over other types of flooring. Bamboo has a high resistance to abrasion and to pressing into it hard and sharp objects. With proper care, bamboo floors will serve you for a long time.</span></span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 16px;"><br>
</span>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Fashion and trends in the modern world of flooring have many directions, most of which are oriented towards the use of natural wood. Wooden floors attract many consumers with their endless charm and beauty. They are especially popular among those people who appreciate natural materials in everything, as well as those who want to avoid the appearance of allergens, usually arising from the use of carpet flooring.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Warmer and much more durable, compared to others, wooden floors are now widely used in public spaces and in residential areas. Today bamboo is an economically viable alternative on the way to achieving the kind, beauty and durability that are inherent in wooden floors.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>

</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">A few recommendations on the use of bamboo floors</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Depending on the design of the board, </span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">bamboo floors</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;"> can be longitudinal (with a longitudinal direction of the fibers) and transverse. Floors from the longitudinal bamboo always look more elegant. Bamboo fibers on this floor are less noticeable. At the same time, the use of transverse bamboo will give your floors a characteristic rough-contrast texture of the surface saturated with fine texture details. This type of bamboo floor is more suitable for smaller rooms.</span></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">If you want to give your room elegance, it is recommended to use a carbonized longitudinal coffee bamboo with a touch of cherry or walnut.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">If you prefer the natural look of the floor, you will want to use unfinished bamboo parquet, which, as a rule, has a light color and will look great in a dimly lit or small room, creating the effect of airiness, space and increasing the width of the room.</span></span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 16px;"><br>
</span>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">When laying the floor of bamboo, keep in mind that the direction of the fibers in the bamboo strip is important. Make sure that to lay the floor in the room you have prepared in the required number of slats of the same type, or transverse or longitudinal. Combination of the strips of transverse and longitudinal bamboo is highly discouraged.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Try searching for a product line from a reputable manufacturer that has a solid history and offers a long-term guarantee. This is usually a sign of durability, which goes hand in hand with hardness. But make sure the company has been in business for a while, otherwise the warranty cannot say that much in the end.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Remember that the charred version is not just a colorful product. It is rich, dark colored as a result of the production stage, which consists of boiling the strips. This is why it gives a sweeter end result.</span> </span></span>
</p></p>
