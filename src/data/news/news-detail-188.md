<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Features of Bamboo flooring</span>

<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo flooring has become the choice of many people due to its physical similarities to hardwoods. The bamboo floors are highly recommended due to its strength, cost effective, eco-friendly and its natural resistance to insects and moisture. <p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">The Manufacturing process of bamboo flooring</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">As the vast variety of bamboo plants are grown and available too, different kinds of floor are manufactured according to the need and requirement of the people. These bamboo plants are cut to similar lengths and are stained and varnished or sometimes people prefer using it in its natural look.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo flooring is made by cutting, slicing the mature bamboo pole into strips. The outer rough area of the bamboo plant which has nodes on it, are removed to make its surface smooth. To give different colour or making its colour darker or lighter is completely dependent on the mixture in which it is boiled. The bamboo plant is also boiled in the solution of boric acid or lime to remove the starch and sugar in it. It also undergoes a carbonizing process to give different shade to the flooring.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">The manufactured bamboo flooring is made available in planks with vertical or horizontal orientation. It is also being laminated to give the impact of natural shine which takes you in the close proximity of nature. The idea of putting bamboo flooring has numerous advantages over its disadvantages. The lamination gives uniform impact in the flooring.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">There are the different types of bamboo flooring which will cover down:</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Strand woven bamboo- It is harder than wood flooring. It is woven together which is totally compressed under heat along with pressure in order to form the plank flooring. The beautiful designs and patterns can be created to make it appealing. It is durable and can be used for a longer period of time.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Horizontal bamboo- In this type, bamboo strips are glued together in horizontal form for plank flooring. In this type the grains of the bamboo are evident and it will show wider on the surface of the floor. It is cut into small, thin strips. The layers of lacquer are added to the surface to the surface to protect and finish the floor. The flooring is wider and the grain pattern is more evident.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Vertical bamboo- In this type, bamboo strips are glued together in vertical form for plank flooring. In this type of flooring the bamboo grain is thinner and it is less evident on the floor surface.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Maintenance of the bamboo flooring</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">It is quite easy to maintain its natural shine for a longer period of time. It does not even demand high repairing and maintenance costs. In fact, it is much easier to clean and mop. It is completely safe for the kids and the people who are suffering due to any kind of allergies. It is recommended to use a dust mop to remove the dirt or grime. Even a mop with hard bristles can also be used to clean the dirt from its crevices.</span>
</span></p>
