<p><font face="Verdana"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">When stepping into a room graced with tiger strand woven bamboo flooring, ones gaze is immediately drawn to the bold streaks and warm nuances reminiscent of the majestic creature roaming the wild forests. This type of flooring brings the allure of the natural world into the sanctuary of your home while providing an eco-friendly alternative to traditional hardwood floors. </span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Harvesting with Care
</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The journey begins in the forests where bamboo, a highly sustainable material, thrives. Grown and harvested responsibly, bamboo reaches maturity rapidly, making it an environmentally sound choice. The specific bamboo culms, chosen for their maturity and strength, are harvested to begin their transformation.
</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The Strand Woven Process
</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Tiger strand woven bamboo is the result of an innovative process where the bamboo is not only cut into strips but also shredded into finer fibers. These fibers are then intricately woven together, creating a dense block of material under extreme pressure and heat, hence the term "strand woven." This process enhances the durability and density, making the floor resistant to wear and tear, indentation, and even the effects of moisture.
</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Tiger Coloration: The Art of Contrast
</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">What sets the tiger variant apart is the artful blending of natural and carbonized bamboo fibers. The carbonization process entails the application of heat to the bamboo, darkening its natural color to a rich caramel hue through a caramelization of the sugars within. When these darker strands intermingle with the lighter, natural bamboo fibers, a striking contrast is born – the distinctive tiger stripe.
</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Milling for Precision
</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Once the block of woven bamboo has cured and hardened, its milled into planks. Precision is key during this phase, ensuring that each plank fits seamlessly with another. Modern milling techniques are used to form the tongue-and-groove or click-lock systems that will later aid in a smooth installation process.
</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Finishing Touches: Durability Meets Aesthetics
</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Post-milling, the tiger-striped planks are sanded for smoothness and then finished with top-quality sealants. These finishes not only protect the flooring from stains and scratches but also enhance the bamboo’s natural aesthetics. Typically, a UV-cured lacquer is applied, which cures the finish instantly and provides an extra layer of protection.
</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Installation: From Forest to Floor
</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">With its versatile design, tiger strand woven bamboo flooring can be installed over various subfloors and is compatible with underfloor heating systems. The advanced milling techniques allow for floating installation, traditional glue-down, or even nail-down methods, offering flexibility to meet diverse needs.
</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Walking on the Wild Side: The Final Masterpiece
</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The final product is a testament to ingenuity and homage to the wild beauty of one of nature’s most magnificent creatures. Tiger strand woven bamboo flooring presents a luxurious yet sustainable choice for homeowners seeking to make a statement with their floors.
