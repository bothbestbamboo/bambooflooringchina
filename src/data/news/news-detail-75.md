<p><p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Click bamboo flooring is a type of engineered bamboo. Usually <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com/products/product-type-BambooFlooring.html">engineered bamboo flooring</a> appears like it is wholly made up of solid pieces of bamboo but it really isn’t. There is actually quite a little bamboo in each piece because during manufacturing the plant stalk is usually sliced into very thin sheets and adhered to a backing material. On the top of the plank is a wear layer that acts to protect the bamboo veneer and at the bottom is also another water proof layer for protection. Therefore the actual bamboo is sandwiched between these two surfaces thereby becoming impervious to stains and moisture.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">How click flooring works</span></strong>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;"> This is one of the easiest ways of installing bamboo flooring and you can even do it on your own. </span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Click bamboo flooring</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;"> has been designed to snap together thereby creating a floating floor without having to use either glue, staples or nails. You will find it exciting to install as well as easy to have replaced when a piece is damaged.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Click Bamboo Flooring Advantages</span></strong>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;"> This kind of floor is usually durable, strong and easy to maintain and looks a lot like natural bamboo but without the drawbacks of it. The tiles are available in a click together pattern that can be installed by even an amateur. If an individual tile gets damaged it can be removed and replaced with another one quite easily. It can also be installed safely in environments that are wet.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Click Bamboo Flooring Disadvantages</span></strong>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;"> It is usually very hard to refinish an engineered bamboo flooring. The wear layer is very durable but when it degrades the floor will just have to be replaced.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Click Bamboo Flooring Applications</span></strong>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;"> Engineered bamboo when properly installed can be suitable for almost any location. If you are installing it in a high traffic environment you will have to purchase the tiles with a thicker wear layer so as to avoid quick degradation.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-size: 12pt; font-family: Verdana;">Even though it is not 100% bamboo, it happens to be stronger than solid wood. It is normally made up of different types of wood with the top layer having bamboo strips and boasts of a lesser expansion rate in comparison to non-engineered bamboo flooring. Due to this, it is a very good option for people located in very humid weather or very dry weather which are quite harmful for pure natural bamboo. Their prices usually start at around $3 for each square foot.</span>
</p></p>
