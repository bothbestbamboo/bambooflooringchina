<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Hardwood floors are a classic choice for the home, due to its attractive appearance, durability, and variety of grains and colors. Bamboo is another popular choice because it has a simple and elegant grain that complements modern furniture. The laminates offer great strength and cheaper prices. Buyers must take into account significant differences in the construction of these types of floors before buying.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">When you are thinking about a floor remodeling project, and you desire to have a look of hardwood floor, you have the choice between real wood, laminate, and bamboo. <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com">Bamboo flooring</a> offers a look similar to hardwood, but there are several differences between bamboo and hardwood that could influence your final purchase decision. Make a side by side comparison of bamboo and real wood to see which material would work best for your project.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Advantages of Wooden Floors</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Hardwoods are available in many colors, from pale pine to dark ebony, in many different table widths and lengths and granulation styles that can provide interest and texture to the decor. Easy to maintain with simple sweeping and scrubbing site, they are more hypoallergenic than other types of floors. Wood floors can be varnished and repaired to look like new, adding value to your home in the long term. The wooden floors also provide good sound insulation.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Hardwood floors Disadvantages</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Wood flooring can be detrimental to the environment not to be extracted from sustainable forests. Because gouges and scratches easily, the wooden floor may not be the best option where children play inside. Renewal of the periodic finish will be necessary, messy, long and expensive. Hardwood floors are also initially expensive and should be installed by an experienced contractor.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Advantages Laminated floor</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Wood lamination is a process of creating a wood look with additional wear capabilities. The floor has a high fiber density center covered with a photographic design of high-resolution wood grain. At the top of these layers is a transparent layer, a resin that lasts for years. Laminate does not fade in sunlight just like normal wood. Its price is three to five times less than hardwood and can also be installed by doing things themselves easily and quickly.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Laminate floor Disadvantages</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Wood floors increase the value of homes with a rich appearance, which stratified floors do not provide. Laminates cannot be recycled easily, which causes environmental concerns. Do not isolate sound, as well as hardwood. You cannot touch up laminate flooring to give it a fresh, new look, and excess water can damage it.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Advantages bamboo floor</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The bamboo floor has a similar appearance to that of wooden planks and comes in light and dark tones. Installed with glue, nails or as a "floating" floor, which is said to be more difficult than hardwoods such as maple or oak. Bamboo grows back quickly, and it is a sustainable product.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Environment</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo allows you to remodel your home by using an ecological soil material. Real wood is made by cutting down trees and modeling wood on floorboards. Bamboo is a grass and regrows much faster than hardwood trees. Growing bamboo for the soil material can help accelerate the growth of bamboo, such as ornaments of old bamboo grass to allow the new lawn to grow.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Hardness</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo floors tend to be more difficult than most kinds of real wood floors. Bamboo flooring is made by bamboo grass-pressure treatment with adhesive. The advantage of bamboo is more than real wood is that it makes </span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">bamboo flooring</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;"> more durable than real wood, which means that bamboo floors will last longer.</span></span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Color</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Real wood comes in a variety of light and dark colors, with several shades in between. You can get real wood that has a red tinge to it or wood that has a greenish appearance. Bamboo comes in a light yellowish color or a dark brown color. Some engineering bamboo floors offer shades of color, but real bamboo only comes in two colors.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo floor Disadvantages</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The processing and maturation of bamboo can affect its longevity and durability.
