<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Examining the Legacy of Bruce Flooring</span></span>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Bruce Flooring is a component of AHF Products, a business that has been around for more than a century. Bruce, a US-based company, has come to represent hardwood flooring manufactured in the US. A large amount of their hardwood products are made in their manufacturing facilities, which are mostly situated in Tennessee and Pennsylvania. In addition to guaranteeing quality, this dedication to domestic production promotes sustainable practices and local communities.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Bruce has added bamboo, laminate, and engineered wood flooring to its list of products over the years. The brand has expanded its product line, but it has remained true to its founding values of affordability, style, and durability.</span><br>

Bruce Flooring Product Types</span></span></div>

<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
1. Flooring made of hardwood</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">

<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">

Engineered Hardwood: Bruce‘s engineered hardwood is composed of many layers of wood that are glued together to provide the appearance of solid wood with more versatility. <div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
2. Flooring made of laminate</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Bruce‘s laminate flooring is intended for homeowners seeking a gorgeous yet affordable hardwood substitute. Additionally, laminate flooring is renowned for being low maintenance and scratch resistant, which makes it perfect for busy homes.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
3. Flooring made of bamboo</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Bruce provides bamboo flooring for people looking for an environmentally friendly flooring choice. Bamboo is a fast-growing, renewable resource that is a great option for homeowners that care about the environment. Bruce‘s bamboo floors are designed to last and are available in a variety of finishes to match both traditional and modern settings.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
4. Wood flooring that has been engineered</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Bruce offers a specialised line of engineered wood flooring in addition to hardwood options. This kind of flooring combines improved stability and resistance with the organic beauty of wood. Where moisture or temperature changes would make real hardwood impracticable, engineered wood is a fantastic alternative.</span><br>
