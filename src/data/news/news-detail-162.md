<p><p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Choosing the right floor for your home includes accuracy and attention to all the details. By choosing an appropriate floor covering, your house will have an increased value, and the cover will add style and warmth. Hardwood wood for flooring is always the best choice for homeowners, since this type of wood has a long period of life, an apartment with such a floor covering can be sold a little more expensive and another floor made of wood brings the character of space.&nbsp;</span></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">However, hardwood has some disadvantages, especially in parts, dents and scratches, and the wood itself is vulnerable to moisture. Some people are also concerned about the environment, and in particular the green forest.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">If you are looking for a floor covering that brings splendor to your home, in this case consider the value of bamboo. Bamboo is not really a tree, it is grass. Stems of bamboo ripen much faster than trees. The grass has little impact on the environment, so do not worry about it. It is necessary to say, than bamboo has a greater maturity, the weaker it will serve as a cover.&nbsp;</span></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">If the strength of the floor is the main factor, then talk with the manufacturer about the age of bamboo used in the parquet board and where it was brought from. Bamboo, usually originates from China, although there are quality stems that grow in the US and Europe.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Installation begins with acclimatization of parquet</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;"><a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com">Bamboo flooring</a> is affordable in many colors and can be painted in a shade of your choice. Just remember that before installing the parquet floor of bamboo, the panels will need to be acclimatized to the room temperature in the same way as the hardwood flooring. After the parquet board from the bamboo is delivered to the house, do not forget to leave the boards in the room where they will be installed, for 3 days in boxes and unpacked. This will allow the parquet board to adjust to humidity and prevent deformation of the parquet floor after installation.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Other factors that can be attributed to saving are that bamboo flooring can be installed without professionals - with your own hands, and you will well save on the expensive services of firms installing the floors. Parquet panels are stacked using a sound and heat insulating substrate, and the panels are installed on the comb-groove lock system, which blocks everything as a mosaic. Spill glue or perform difficult work is not required. To care for these floors, you need to regularly vacuum the floor and this is enough, although the coating can be cleaned with special cleaners designed to care for bamboo parquet. To protect the floors and ensure their durability, lay a large carpet in a place with high traffic.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">What are some of its notable features?</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">The basis of bamboo parquet is a substance created by compressing bamboo fibers into a specific shape, in subsequent stages this form receives a certain color and texture.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Parquet from Bamboo flooring is formed in such a way that it turns out very easy. This means that the installation of such a parquet in the house will be a fairly simple process. Because the bamboo fiber is compressed during production, the parquet is durable and flexible. This parquet is resistant to moisture and scratches.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Different colors and texture of bamboo flooring is achieved due to varying degrees of carbonation. Carbonized bamboo flooring, in fact, is subjected to heat treatment in order to darken its texture and give a certain strength. The longer it heats, the darker the color will turn out and the stronger it will be.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Since, in fact, bamboo is a grass, not a tree, then when choosing Bamboo flooring, it is important to pay attention to the quality and the manufacturer (you should know that all bamboo flooring is made only in China). The </span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">main advantage of bamboo flooring</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;"> is that with its low price interiors with bamboo floors look rather rich and presentable.</span></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Care of the parquet is very important, as well as for any floor of natural wood.</span></span></p>
