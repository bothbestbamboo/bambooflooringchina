<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;">Bamboo parquet: characteristic features.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;">Before going into the different types of bamboo flooring offered by the market, let us see together what are the characteristics that have made this material so popular! First of all, you have to know that bamboo is an eco-sustainable material: the bamboo plant, in fact, has much faster growth and maturation time than the traditional wood used for parquet.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;">To be exact, it can be used already after 3 or 5 years, against the 20 needed for a normal tree to reach maturity. <span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;">For this reason, we advise you always to contact only companies with certified products and materials.&nbsp;</span>

</span>

<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;">Another point in favor of bamboo flooring is its hardness and strength: compared to oak wood it is 30% harder.</span>

<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;">Bamboo is also ideal for renovations since it is distributed already painted and ready for installation: in this way, you will avoid running risks associated with the evaporation of sanding and painting which instead still remain valid for classic parquet.</span>

<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;"><br>
</span>

<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;">With all the flooring options available to us today, finding the right floor for you can be a challenge, so much so that it is easy to overlook options that would be perfect for us, such as bamboo floorings. </span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;">You probably would never think of it, but bamboo is one of the most durable floorings and furniture options. It lasts as long as many types of parquet, sometimes even more. This depends on the quality of the bamboo used; the low-quality one will not last as long as the high-quality one. Therefore make sure that the bamboo used is of the best quality available.</span>

<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;"><br>

</span>

<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;">A bamboo flooring is exceptionally easy to maintain. Daily cleaning will only require a simple rag without the need for special treatments, which is perfectly suited to busy people. Over time, if the floor shows scratches, nicks or bleaching, you can feel comfortable because the bamboo floor can be easily finished. Simply smooth the floor and reapply a top coat and voila! Like New!</span>

</span></p>
