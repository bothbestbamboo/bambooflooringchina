<p><p><span style="font-size: 16pt;"><font face="Verdana"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Hardwood Floors: If you‘re on a budget, you might as well choose solid wood floors.
</span></font></span></p>
<font face="Verdana"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">
</span></font>
<p><br>
<font face="Verdana"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">
</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;"><a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com/products/product-type-BambooFlooring.html">Solid wood flooring</a> is processed from logs, so it has the advantages of natural texture, environmental protection and health. In addition, solid wood flooring also has the advantages of warm in winter and cool in summer, good touch, good elasticity, good sound insulation, sound absorption, insulation performance, and refurbishment.
</span></font></p>
<font face="Verdana"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">
</span></font>
<p><br>
<font face="Verdana"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">
</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">However, solid wood flooring retains the characteristics of wood to the greatest extent, so it inevitably has shortcomings such as non-wear resistance, easy deformation, fear of acid and alkali. Taking all aspects into consideration, solid wood floors are suitable for laying places such as bedrooms, living rooms, study rooms, etc. of ordinary residences.
</span></font></p>
<font face="Verdana"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">
</span></font>
<p><br>
<font face="Verdana"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">
</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Laminate Flooring: If you have a limited budget, it is recommended that you choose Laminate Flooring, which is one of the most popular wood floors on the market.
</span></font></p>
<font face="Verdana"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">
</span></font>
<p><br>
<font face="Verdana"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">
</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">The laminate floor is composed of a wear-resistant layer, a decorative layer, a high-density substrate layer, and a balance (moisture-proof) layer. Laminate flooring is not only affordable, rich in colors, but also has the advantages of good wear resistance, good acid and alkali resistance, easy maintenance, good anti-skid performance, wear resistance, antibacterial and so on. However, the laminate floor has a bad foot feel and can be repaired. Laminate flooring is suitable for ground decoration in ordinary houses and public places.
</span></font></p>
<font face="Verdana"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">
</span></font>
<p><br>
<font face="Verdana"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">
</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo Flooring
</span></font></p>
<font face="Verdana"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">
</span></font>
<p><br>
<font face="Verdana"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">
</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">The floor made of bamboo can reduce the use of wood and protect the environment.
</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo floors are fresh and elegant, with comfortable feet and good wear resistance, but Chinese families are not very accepting of bamboo floors. It may be related to the fact that the bamboo floor is afraid of scratches and is prone to delamination in the sun.
</span></font></p>
<font face="Verdana"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">
