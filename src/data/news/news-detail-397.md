<span style="font-size: 10.5pt;">
A Closer Look at Wellmade Flooring</span></span>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 16px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;"><span style="font-size: 10.5pt;">
Wellmade Performance Flooring is based in Wilsonville, Oregon, with manufacturing operations in China. The company was founded in the early 2000s and has since grown into a recognized leader in engineered and strand-woven </span><a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com/"><span style="font-size: 10.5pt;">bamboo flooring</span></a><span style="font-size: 10.5pt;">. <br>
<span style="font-size: 10.5pt;">
What sets Wellmade apart is its commitment to combining technology and sustainability. Their products are FloorScore? certified for indoor air quality, and they manufacture bamboo flooring using low-VOC adhesives, often meeting or exceeding CARB Phase 2 standards.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10.5pt;">
Their patented HDPC? (High-Density Plastic Composite) core technology is another standout innovation, providing enhanced water resistance and dimensional stability — a feature especially attractive to homeowners in moisture-prone regions.</span><br>
