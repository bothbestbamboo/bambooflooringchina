<p><p><span style="font-family: Verdana;">
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">No matter what plants you want to plant, its growing environment is the important factor you have to consider. Take the <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com">bamboo</a> planting for example, only with the suitable climate and geographic position, bamboo which has better ornamental value can be planted very well.
</span></span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana; font-size: 12pt;">
In the following article, we will mainly discuss growing environment of different bamboos, which would be helpful if you want to plant them.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Firstly, variety of bamboo</span></strong>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">
As is known to all, different bamboos should be planted in different environment, for example, if it is sympodial bamboo, it should be planted in the south region because growing temperature is over minus five degrees in full year. On the contrary, if bamboo has poor performance of low temperature resistant, it should not be planted in the north region, otherwise it is easy to be frozen to death, which is so serious.
</span></span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana; font-size: 12pt;">
Based on such characteristic, please consider your living environment at first and then choose right variety of bamboo to plant.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-weight: bold; font-size: 12pt;">Secondly, </span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">different bamboos have different biology characteristics</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">, so that if its growing environment is suitable, it will grow very well. In real life, most people like to plant </span><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">moso bamboo</span></strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;"> because of its tall and big shape, along with too much green bamboo leaves on it, however, when being asked which environment is suitable for moso bamboo growing, can you give right answer? In fact, moso bamboo grows very well in the environment which has bigger humidness, so that if there is too much fog in the morning, it is so perfect growing environment for moso bamboo planting. What’s more, with large scale bamboo forest, it will become great tourist attractions because people would enjoy such environment.
</span></span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana; font-size: 12pt;">
Some bamboos grow very well in shady and cool environment, and Chimonobambusa qundrangularis is one of such varieties. On the other hands, some bamboos grow better in sunny environment, and Phyllostachys aurea is the exact variety. Believe it or not, if planting Phyllostachys aurea in humid environment, there is no arhat shape at bamboo joint.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana; font-size: 12pt;">
Lots of people have wrong standpoint that bamboo are not afraid of being soaked in water because most bamboos would grow very well in humid environment. When planting bamboo in large scale, you should be more careful about such condition, if bamboo leaves roll up, it is important to drain away water in bamboo leaves instantly, otherwise bamboo will die due to rotten bamboo root.
</span></p>
