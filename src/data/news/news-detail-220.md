<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo board is a high-quality furniture board, comparable to precious solid wood, with more uniform material and superior physical and mechanical properties. Why did you say that? Since the bamboo board is dried in small pieces and the solid board is dried as a whole, the water treatment of the bamboo board is more uniform and the shrinkage is smaller than that of the wood board. Bamboo is one of the hardness of all plants in the world. Furniture products made of bamboo boards are more waterproof and crack-resistant than solid boards. Bamboo plywood are aligned with straight fibers and are not easily deformed. The arrangement of wood fibers is complex and easily twisted and deformed. <span style="font-size: 12pt;">Advantages of bamboo plywood for making furniture

<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">3. Bamboo board has better stability and moisture resistance than solid board. Bamboo is a coarse fiber plant with high density and little expansion space. <p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">4.
