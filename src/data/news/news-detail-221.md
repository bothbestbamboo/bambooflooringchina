<p><p><font face="Verdana"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">More and more people like to lay bamboo floors at home, mainly because bamboo floors are more practical and can be matched with various decorative styles without being obtrusive. When buying a <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com/">bamboo floor</a>, it is necessary to maintain it. How to maintain the bamboo floor? The cleaning work in daily maintenance is a special key. It can be better maintained when the cleaning work is done well. </span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Avoid damaging the floor surface: The lacquer surface of the bamboo floor is not only the decorative layer of the floor, but also the protective layer of the floor. Therefore, scratches with sharp objects, impact with hard objects, friction with metals, etc. should be avoided, and chemicals should not be stored indoors. In addition, indoor furniture should be handled with care when moving, and the feet of the furniture should be padded with rubber skins. In public places, the main passages should be covered with carpets, etc.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Avoid sun exposure and rain: In some houses, sunlight or rain can directly enter the local area of ??the room through the window, which will cause great harm to the bamboo floor. Sunlight will accelerate the aging of paint and glue (bamboo outdoor floors are specially treated, not listed here), and will also cause shrinkage and cracking of the floor. After the rain is wet, the bamboo absorbs water and causes swelling and deformation, and in severe cases, the floor will become moldy (it doesn‘t matter if it is wiped dry immediately).
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Keep the indoor ventilated and dry environment: Keep the indoor ventilation regularly, which can not only volatilize the chemical substances in the floor as much as possible, and discharge them to the outdoors, but also exchange the indoor humid air with the outdoor. Especially when no one lives indoors for a long time, indoor ventilation is more important. Commonly used methods are: opening windows or doors frequently to allow air convection, or using air conditioning systems and ventilation systems to create a dry and clean indoor environment.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Correct cleaning and care: During daily use, the floor should be cleaned regularly to keep the floor clean and hygienic. When cleaning, you can first use a clean broom to sweep away the dust and debris, and then manually wipe it with a rag that has been wrung out of water. Mop the ground. The cut cannot be washed with water, nor can it be cleaned with a damp rag or mop. Usually, if there is water-containing material spilled on the ground, it should be wiped with a dry cloth immediately.</span></font></p></p>
