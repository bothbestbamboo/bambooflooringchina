<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 11pt;">In today‘s era of environmental protection and health, the environmental protection level of panels has become the focus of consumers‘ attention during the decoration process. Among them, ENF-grade panels stand out with their excellent environmental protection performance and have become a popular choice in the market. So, what exactly is ENF-grade board?</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
1. Definition of ENF-grade panels</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
ENF grade is the highest standard in the environmental protection level of panels in my country. According to the "Grading of Formaldehyde Emission of Artificial panels and Their Products" standard, the formaldehyde emission of ENF-grade panels is ≤0.025mg/m3. This standard is far lower than the common E0-grade (formaldehyde emission ≤0.050mg/m3) and E1-grade (formaldehyde emission ≤0.124mg/m3) panels on the market.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
2. Characteristics of ENF-grade panels</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
1. Extreme environmental protection</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
The biggest feature of ENF-grade panels is their extremely low formaldehyde emission. Formaldehyde is a chemical substance that is harmful to the human body. Long-term exposure may cause health problems such as respiratory diseases and cancer. ENF-grade panels are strictly controlled in the production process to control the formaldehyde emission at an extremely low level, providing consumers with a safe and healthy indoor environment.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
2. High-quality raw materials</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
In order to meet the environmental protection standards of ENF level, manufacturers usually use high-quality raw materials. For example, adhesives without formaldehyde addition and environmentally friendly wood raw materials are used. These high-quality raw materials not only ensure the environmental performance of the board, but also improve the physical properties and service life of the board.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
3. Strict production process</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
The production process of ENF-grade panels needs to be strictly controlled. From the selection of raw materials, the optimization of processing technology to the strict control of quality inspection, every link must ensure that the formaldehyde emission of the board meets the ENF-level standard. For example, advanced hot pressing technology and environmentally friendly coating technology are used to reduce the generation and release of formaldehyde.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
III. Types of ENF-grade panels</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
1. Solid wood particle board</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Solid wood particle board is a man-made board made of wood or other wood cellulose materials, which is glued together under heat and pressure after applying adhesive. ENF-grade solid wood particle board usually uses adhesives without formaldehyde addition, which is environmentally friendly, stable and durable. It is suitable for furniture making, interior decoration and other fields.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
2. Medium-density fiberboard</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Medium-density fiberboard is made of wood fiber or other plant fiber as raw materials, prepared by fiber, applied with synthetic resin, and pressed into board under heating and pressure conditions. ENF-grade medium-density fiberboard also uses environmentally friendly adhesives and production processes, and has the advantages of high flatness, moderate strength and easy processing. It is widely used in furniture, cabinets, floors and other fields.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
3. Multi-layer solid wood board</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Multi-layer solid wood board is made of three or more layers of single board or thin board wood board glued and hot pressed. ENF-grade multi-layer solid wood board usually uses adhesives without formaldehyde addition and high-quality wood raw materials, which is environmentally friendly, stable and beautiful. It is suitable for high-end furniture, interior decoration and other fields.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
IV. Advantages of ENF-grade panels</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
1. Protect family health</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
The extremely low formaldehyde emission of ENF-grade panels can effectively reduce indoor air pollution and protect family health. Especially for sensitive groups such as children, pregnant women, and the elderly, using ENF-grade panels can reduce their risk of being harmed by formaldehyde.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
2. Improve the quality of decoration</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
ENF-grade panels not only have excellent environmental performance, but also have a high level in appearance and quality. It can provide high-quality material guarantees for decoration projects and improve the quality and grade of decoration.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
3. Comply with environmental protection trends</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
With the continuous improvement of people‘s awareness of environmental protection, environmentally friendly materials are becoming more and more popular in the market. As one of the panels with the highest environmental protection level, ENF-grade panels are in line with the current environmental protection trend and have broad market prospects.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
V. How to identify ENF-grade panels</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
1. Check the test report</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Formal ENF-grade board manufacturers will provide test reports issued by authoritative organizations. Consumers can check the test reports to confirm whether the environmental protection level of the board meets the ENF-level standards.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
2. Observe the appearance of the board</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
The appearance of ENF-grade panels is usually flat and smooth, without obvious defects and odors. Consumers can make a preliminary judgment on its quality and environmental protection performance by observing the appearance of the board.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
3. Choose a well-known brand</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Choosing ENF-grade panels from well-known brands can ensure the quality and environmental performance of the products. Well-known brands usually have strict quality control systems and good after-sales services, which can provide more protection for consumers.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Summary: ENF-grade panels are the highest standard in the environmental protection level of panels in my country. They are characterized by extreme environmental protection, high-quality raw materials, and strict production processes. It has a wide variety, including solid wood particle panels, medium-density fiberpanels, multi-layer solid wood panels, etc. ENF-grade panels have obvious advantages, which can protect the health of the family, improve the quality of decoration, and comply with environmental protection trends.
