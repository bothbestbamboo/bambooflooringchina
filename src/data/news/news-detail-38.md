<p><p><span style="font-family: Verdana;">
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo is a large tubular grass with a hard outer shell, making it suitable to use as an alternative for woods, thereby saving many forest lands from destruction. Bamboo is extensively used as flooring material in many East Asian countries.
</span></span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;"><a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com">
Bamboo flooring</a> sales have shot up over the years and the reasons are quite obvious; they are attractive, versatile, affordable and above all environment friendly.
</span></span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">
Basic Forms of Flooring
</span></span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">
Bamboo flooring comes in two basic forms: solid planks and engineered planks.
</span></span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">
Solid, tongue and groove bamboo is handled and installed similar to the other regular hardwood flooring. The planks are secured down with nails or staples with or without mastic.&nbsp; As we all know, nailing&nbsp; and stapling can be a time consuming process, So is the installation of solid flooring normally takes twice as long as engineered planks to install.
</span></span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">
The good news is, </span><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">solid bamboo flooring</span></strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">&nbsp; produces a permanent and rock solid floor; they also come prefinished and one can transport all your things&nbsp; as soon as you finish flooring instead of having to wait until the finishing’s polish dries.
</span></span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">
There are three different kinds of solid bamboo flooring:
</span></span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">
1)&nbsp;Vertical grain solid strip</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">
2)&nbsp;Flat grain solid strip</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">
3)&nbsp;Strand bamboo
</span></span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana; font-size: 12pt;">
Vertical grain solid strip is composed of thin bamboo strips stacked on the edges and glued together. These floors give a mottled look and are ideal for hiding dirt as well as providing an interesting look to the room
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana; font-size: 12pt;">
Flat grain is the most common method of preparing Solid wood. In this process, the thin, flat strips of bamboo are stacked one atop the other and then laminated together.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">
Strand bamboo is obtained from super thin&nbsp;&nbsp; shredded bamboo fibers that are mixed together with resins and pressed into rock hard planks.
</span></span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana; font-size: 12pt;">
Costs
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana; font-size: 12pt;">
A solid bamboo plank costs less than engineered planks. Prices are often based on the thickness of the planks; thicker the plank, greater will be the price.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana; font-size: 12pt;">
Warranties are excellent indicators of the quality of wood. The best wood comes with a longer warranty as compared to low cost wood.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana; font-size: 12pt;">
Advantages of Solid Bamboo
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Solid bamboo finish gives one the most natural look amongst other types of bamboo flooring</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">. The surface of the board will have quite rich features and some interesting patterns enhancing the ‘look’ of the room to another level.
</span></span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">
Scratches and dents tend to occur on the woods after some time on the floors. With solid bamboo planks, one can sand down and refinish the surface at frequent intervals, making it look new and shiny again.</span>
</span></p></p>
