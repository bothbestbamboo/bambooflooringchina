<p><p><span style="font-family: Verdana;">
In past few years, bamboo flooring has received a tremendous applause that lead to increased sales of this flooring choice. With changing choices and increasing preference bamboo flooring is made available in different colors and in different varieties making it one of the prime choices when flooring options are concerned. One other reason that might have increased consumers leaning towards <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com">bamboo flooring</a> is its harvesting in natural and in environmental responsible manner. Not only people cautious to preserve their environment prefer bamboo hardwood for their flooring but also people who want to bring aesthetic appeal and luxury to their homes.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<strong>Analyzing the Success of Bamboo Flooring</strong>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
Gone are the days when people used to think that bamboo is not a viable material for flooring. With increased durability, resistance to moisture and insects and feel bamboo is certainly the best hardwood flooring material now. Not only bamboo feels like wood but it also looks and cuts like wood. Additionally, bamboo is said to be denser that hardwood even denser than maple hardwood, which makes it exceptionally good for flooring.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
The best thing is bamboo being type of grass can be harvested in environment friendly manner. Apart from that, being fastest growing plants it can be harvested to full-grown flanks within 4 to 5 years. On the contrary, hardwood trees usually need 30 to 40 years before they can be milled for making flooring flanks. When bamboo regenerates after cutting from the same plant you need to plant trees for getting other hardwood.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<strong>Options Available in Bamboo Flooring</strong>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
As of now, two varieties that are in most demand are engineered planks and solid bamboo planks used commonly for flooring. Usually engineered planks are installed using floating floor method wherein the bamboo floor planks are attached together on the thin foal rubber, which is typically used as underlayment for flooring. Due to this kind of installation in no way bamboo floor from engineered planks is attached to the subfloor speeding up the installation process in cost effective manner.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
When it comes to solid or grooved bamboo flooring then it is installed like any other traditional hardwood flooring. With or without mastic solid bamboo planks either are stapled or nailed that fastens them with the underlying floor. Although this type of installation is costly and time consuming it surely produces permanent floor that is more rock solid and sturdy. The best thing is engineered and solid bamboo flooring planks come in prefinished manner so immediately after the installation of flooring you can start using it or placing your furniture.
</span></p>
