<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Ever since the Bamboo flooring has come into existence, it has acquired a lot of attention as an alternative to the hardwood flooring. It is now been used in residential as well as in commercial properties. A bamboo flooring is a man-made creation with the use of a bamboo plant. This plant is a kind of a grass that originated from China. <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com">Bamboo flooring</a> being a natural product can create a graceful as well as a recent appearance to your apartment or commercial space.&nbsp;</span></span>

<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo Flooring Constructions</span></strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">There are many ways to change bamboo grass plant into bamboo flooring planks that can be all set for an installation. Products that are created through engineering process has a detailed appearances. You will be able to choose the right type of bamboo for your venture if you are able to identify the options that are available in the market.&nbsp;</span></span>

<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">In the Eastern Asia, the traditional flooring was prepared by carving the stems in the form of thin sheets, then fixing it to a hardwood beams. In certain rural zones, this type of flooring is still been used. The modified form of flooring mainly has two common categories – Solid bamboo flooring in which solid parts of bamboo are compressed and joined together to form floor board. While engineering bamboo flooring contains comparatively thinner external layer that are joined to a wood or a high-density fiberboard surface layer.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Types of profiles in Bamboo Flooring</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">A bamboo flooring has mainly two types of profiles or fitting method:&nbsp;</span></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Tongue and Groove – This is a Traditional bamboo flooring fitting method where the tongue is installed firmly into the groove in such a way that a slight opening is left among the flooring planks.&nbsp;</span></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Click Fitting Method – A click fitting method make use a uniclic lock-up system. Here the sheets can be easily installed together in a simple click and lock way.&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Colors and finishes of Bamboo Flooring.&nbsp;</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo flooring may be available in different finishes. Flooring finish is mainly the way the surface appears and displays an impression. The bamboo flooring colours is decided either by blemishing or carbonizing at the time of production. The bamboo colour is light yellow are the natural ones while a carbonized bamboo flooring is formed by burning in-order to make a light and dark brown colour. The method carried out in protecting the bamboo flooring from day to day damage is known as finish. Bamboo flooring of all kind come’s pre-finished thus can be used once it’s been installed.&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></span>
</span></p>
