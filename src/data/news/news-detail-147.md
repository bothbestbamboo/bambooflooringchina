<p><p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">We evaluate together all the main advantages and defects of the </span><span style="font-size: 12pt;"><a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com/products/product-type-BambooFlooring.html">bamboo flooring</a></span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">. Bamboo flooring has become popular in recent years due to the many similarities with wooden floors. The bamboo version is elegant, gives an exotic look to the environments and is relatively inexpensive, which makes it even more attractive.</span></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Let‘s try and evaluate all the pros and cons of this type of flooring to make the right choice before installation and choose the one that best suits your lifestyle.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">So what are the main reasons to install bamboo flooring?</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:16px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Duration in time</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The bamboo flooring just like the one in wood, can last many years. As in any field, quality is a key feature to establish durability, durability and reliability.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">In particular, the non-carbonated bamboo is particularly resistant and can be compared to American oak parquet, but with more gear.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:16px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Elegance</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The bamboo floor fits perfectly into linear environments, with modern aesthetics. Whoever chooses the bamboo parquet is attentive to the trends, but also demands a high quality.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:16px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Availability in many variants</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">One of the advantages of bamboo flooring is the wide choice available on the market because it is available in many styles and colors.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Keep in mind that dark-colored bamboo floors have often been thermally treated.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:16px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Eco-sustainability&nbsp;</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo by many is considered a very good alternative to traditional wood flooring thanks to its much faster growth and reproduction capacity. In fact a bamboo plant reaches full maturity between five and seven years.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:16px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Natural materials</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The use of natural materials is depopulating in construction and bamboo flooring can be the icing on the cake in a project initiated by ecologically aware people.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:16px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Easy maintenance</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The bamboo parquet is easily cleaned with a broom and a little neutral soap, no special treatment required. A great pro for those who do not have much time to clean up!</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:16px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Possibility of finishing</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo floors can become discolored or dull with time, but there is good news: they can be finished according to your needs because it comes with several layers of protection that are applied during installation.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">If necessary the surface sanding by professionals can give new life to the bamboo floor.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:16px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Water resistance</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Compared to traditional wood floors, bamboo is more resistant to water and damage caused by it, such as stains and deformations. More durable however does not mean that it does not suffer damage, so it is best to pay attention.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:16px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Low cost</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Despite its exotic charm, bamboo parquet is usually less expensive than wooden parquet. What else could be better option than choosing a flooring that looks awesome and costs a small amount of money!</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">What are the limitations of bamboo flooring?</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p align="center">
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><img width="600" height="400" title="What are the benefits of installing bamboo flooring?" align="" alt="What are the benefits of installing bamboo flooring?" src="/kindeditor/attached/image/20171005/20171005170550_8198.jpg"></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:16px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Scratch sensitivity</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo compared to wooden parquet tends to scratch more easily. With an intensive use of an average family, it is impossible to maintain a smooth surface because pets, furniture, or heel shoes can damage the floor. Also beware of the sand!</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">As mentioned above under the heading "Possibility of finishing", however, it is easy to remedy the damage.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:16px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Damage caused by water</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">As mentioned in the Pro list, the bamboo floor is water resistant enough, but this does not exclude that, being a natural organic material, it does not cause damage caused by too much moisture, causing deformation, discoloration or mold.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:16px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Sensitive to moisture</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">In very humid environments, the parquet boards in bamboo are likely to expand beyond normal, or to shrink in too dry environments.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:16px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Risk of too soft materials</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">If the bamboo used was taken too early and did not have time to mature the risk is to be with a floor too soft and, consequently, not lasting.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Generally uncoated and stranded bamboo floors are not affected by this problem, while dark bamboo can represent a weaker structure.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:16px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Less versatile style</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Though the bamboo flooring is unique in its kind and has a great appeal, it can be a limit in styles of homes very different from contemporary and modern. However, it is also true that most of the designers and flooring experts confirm that that the wooden bamboo decking is one of the finest form of flooring one can use in their homes.</span> </span></span>
</p></p>
