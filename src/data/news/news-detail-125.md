<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;">You might love the idea of intensely campaigned green movement which talks about implementing sustainable resources for the construction and decoration of your home. Before taking any decision, one must explore the various aspects of it. </span><span style="font-size:14px;">Carry out a research if needed. But depending solely on what the retailers suggest is the last thing you should do apart from following the trend blindly. Hardwood flooring is associated with having sound knowledge regarding quality aftercare for its reliable durability. Bamboo is native to Southeast Asia, so it is evident that the bamboo flooring products thrive from that region of the landmass. Bamboo is always the best alternative for genuine hardwood floors as they are physically similar. </span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;">The plus points of hand scraped bamboo flooring are its strength and durability. It is eco-friendly, pest and moisture resistant. If you are keen on its possession, you should know how they are made. A bamboo flooring is typically made by slicing mature bamboo stems as flat as possible. Then the pieces are hand scraped and are nailed down to beams of stems of other bigger bamboos. The processing of bamboo involves a lot of chemical compounds in a required amount. The chemical cured boards then put under ultraviolet radiations. </span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;">The variations worth mentioning are vertical or horizontal grain oriented, locking bamboo and strand woven bamboo. The hand scraped bamboo flooring has a unique ageless splendor for its chisel shaped look. The fashion and the craftsmanship of the skilled laborers are evident in its every look. To put a grainy feel and exclusivity to the pattern, hand scraping is done. Actually, hand scraped bamboo flooring designs have the propensity to reflect the design and give a distinct appearance. </span><span style="font-size:14px;">The model gives a noble classic wood look to the surface of the house it has been installed. The utilization of manual labor for the wood differs. Some retailers merely get their installers chafe the floor after installation of the floorboards. Recently, pre-finished boards for floors have stood up to be the best choice because it eases the pace of installation. The cracks of pre-finished boards are also little or invisible. The scraped look and bamboo flooring provide an oomph to your homely abode. </span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;">The advantage</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;">It is easy to maintain hand scraped bamboo flooring as it is resistant to moisture, stains, and warping compared to other hardwood materials. Since it is the trend now in the construction world, it will be easy for its repair and other such services. The bamboo flooring designs reject the ideas of cookie cutter process. Every piece is unique and possesses individuality. Still, the price is similar or slightly on the higher side in compared to the hardwood floorings. Bamboo enhances the look of space. It creates a niche among every other kind of wood planks available. It is different, distinct and adds ineffable quality to your home. Everything under the sun is entitled to a certain amount of depreciation, and hand scraped bamboo flooring is no exception. It can get scratched, marred and discolored over the time, but sanding and re-application of polishes and finishing coats can resurrect it.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;">The Disadvantage</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;">The processing chemicals of bamboo planks using heat, pressure, resin adhesives and other chemicals make it emit a harmful amount of VOC. It is impossible to keep bamboo flooring in perfect condition when it is used on a regular basis. There is many conditions which can create devastating (apparently) appearance on it. The friction of shoes, claws, furniture legs is just to be as few. Dust and sand particles can also do the same to it. If the house which is to be floored by hand scraped bamboo flooring is in the humid area, the factor automatically decreases the longevity of it. Also, in arid areas, the planks can shrink. In both weather conditions, it is possible for the planks to develop cracks. Dark bamboo planks are soft because the carbonization weakens the structure of bamboo. There are some sustainable issues regarding bamboos. The chemical toxicity, replacement of bamboo fields for commercial usage are to name a few.</span>
