<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 11pt;">Armstrong Flooring is a well-known brand in the flooring industry that is associated with excellence, creativity, and longevity. <span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Armstrong Flooring‘s Story</span></span>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Thomas Armstrong started the company in 1860 as a modest cork-cutting enterprise in Pennsylvania. Armstrong became one of the most well-known brands in the flooring industry over the years. Their dedication to innovation has greatly influenced flooring trends and technology. Even though the company has expanded into other product categories, its flooring segment continues to be a vital component of its success.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
The core values of the brand are performance, aesthetic appeal, and environmental responsibility through the use of premium materials. Armstrong Flooring‘s sustained success in the market is evidence of its capacity to develop and adjust while keeping the demands of its clients in mind.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Examining the Product Types of Armstrong Flooring</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Although Armstrong has several flooring choices, its bamboo, hardwood, and engineered wood floors are especially well-liked. Because each variety meets various functional needs and design preferences, they are adaptable options for a range of applications.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Bamboo Floors</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Bamboo flooring has drawn a lot of interest due to its distinctive appearance and sustainability. Armstrong‘s <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com">bamboo flooring</a> is an environmentally beneficial substitute for conventional hardwoods because it is made from bamboo plants that are renewable. Bamboo flooring is a great option for high-traffic areas because of its reputation for strength and resilience to wear.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
From light natural colours to deep, rich hues, Armstrong bamboo flooring comes in a range of treatments and tones. Because of its strand-woven design, which guarantees increased strength, it is appropriate for homes with kids or dogs. Additionally, whether you‘re a professional contractor or a do-it-yourself enthusiast, Armstrong‘s simple locking mechanisms make the installation process easier.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Hardwood floors</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Armstrong‘s collection of hardwood floors is an ode to classic style. These flooring, which are made from high-quality wood species like oak, maple, hickory, and others, add warmth and natural beauty to any area. Armstrong hardwood products are versatile for a range of subfloor conditions since they come in both solid and engineered formats.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Armstrong‘s exclusive protective coatings are applied to the hardwood floors to improve their resilience to moisture, stains, and scratches. Customers can tailor the look to fit their interior design by selecting from a range of finishes, such as matte, semi-gloss, and high-gloss. A common option for homeowners wishing to give their houses more personality and value is Armstrong hardwood flooring.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Wood Flooring Engineered</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Engineered wood flooring offers improved stability and durability along with the beauty of real wood. Compared to conventional solid wood flooring, Armstrong‘s engineered wood floors are more resilient to moisture and temperature changes because they are made of layers of actual wood that have been glued together. They are therefore a great choice for spaces like kitchens and basements where humidity levels can fluctuate.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Armstrong provides engineered wood flooring in a variety of designs, ranging from exotic woods like acacia to traditional oak. Its adaptability is increased by the fact that engineered wood can be installed as a glue-down or floating floor. Armstrong‘s engineered wood floors are also made to last, featuring a wear layer that can be repeatedly sanded and refinished.</span><br>
