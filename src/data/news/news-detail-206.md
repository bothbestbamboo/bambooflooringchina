<p><p><font face="Verdana"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Wooden floor is divided into: solid wood floor, laminate floor, solid wood composite floor, <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com">bamboo flooring</a>.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">1. Solid wood floor
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Solid wood floor is a ground decoration material formed after drying and processing of wood. It has the characteristics of natural pattern, comfortable foot feeling and safe use. It is an ideal material for floor decoration such as bedrooms, living rooms and study rooms. The decorative style of solid wood returns to simplicity and the texture is natural. Today, when the forest coverage rate is declining, and environmental protection is vigorously advocated, solid wood flooring is even more precious.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The sound produced by the solid wood floor is not necessarily the floor itself, but may also be caused by the high moisture content of the wood keel and deformation. The choice of solid wood floor is to pursue its real and natural feeling, and the solid wood floor can be repaired and painted many times. The average moisture content of solid wood floors is best controlled at about 12%, and it is not good to be too dry or too wet.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">2. Laminate flooring
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The surface of laminate flooring is determined by the shape of the upper platen during the hot pressing process. The shape of the board is different, the surface is different, and its characteristics are also different. Laminate flooring is favored by more and more consumers due to its many advantages such as wear resistance, beautiful appearance, environmental protection, moisture resistance, flame retardant, moth resistance, convenient installation, easy cleaning and care, economical and practical, etc. With the continuous improvement of the quality of laminate flooring , The products are more artistic, personalized, more real, beautiful, lighter, more environmentally friendly, and the surface treatment technology is more advanced and diversified, which can better meet the needs of different markets.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">3. Engineered wood parquet
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Solid wood multi-layer floor, also known as solid wood engineered floor, is based on solid wood floor, through technological processing, the wood is decomposed and then combined into a new generation of solid wood floor, so that the world‘s precious and rare tree species can be fully and reasonably utilized, A small amount of precious tree species produces a large number of precious wood floors, so that the green forest resources can be used scientifically.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The solid wood multi-layer floor is made of multi-layer solid wood sheets, which are combined in a criss-cross manner, using adhesive, and hot-pressed into a board and then processed. The internal stress of the product has been balanced to a certain extent, which solves the problem of single-layer solid wood The floor is prone to moisture deformation, shrinkage and cracking, and is more suitable for geothermal floors. The thickness of the solid wood multi-layer surface board is generally 0.3mm-2mm, of which the three-layer solid wood can be up to 4mm.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">4. Bamboo wood floor
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo floor is divided into multi-layer glued bamboo floor and single-layer side-split bamboo floor. Bamboo floor has a natural and fresh appearance, delicate and smooth texture, moisture-proof, moisture-proof, anti-corrosion, strong toughness, elasticity and hard surface.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo wood engineered floor is a composite regeneration product of bamboo and wood. Its panels and bottom plates are made of bamboo, and the core layers are mostly fir, camphor and other woods. Bamboo wood composite floor has the advantages of bamboo floor. This floor core material uses wood as raw material, with good stability, strong and durable, good foot feel, warm in winter and cool in summer.</span></font></p></p>
