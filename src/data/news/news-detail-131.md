<p><p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Nowadays, bamboo is the way to go. After all, the flexibility, cost-effectiveness and widespread availability are potent factors that have given a massive spurt to furniture, fittings and floorings made of this material. Not to miss out the eco-friendliness and environmental improvement that bamboo provides, which is why </span><span style="font-size: 12pt;"><a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com/products/bamboo-veneer.html">bamboo veneer</a></span><span style="font-size: 12pt;"> has become a very popular product in the market, at least in comparison to many of its counterparts like hardwood. The natural colour and ease with which modern day designs can be made of bamboo veneer is gaining momentum by the day.</span></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">So people are quite interested about knowing how this tall and round bamboo can be processed and the soft and thin veneer extracted from the same. Here’s taking a look at the manufacturing process of bamboo veneer to understand what it is all about.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Cutting of the trees</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">So once the bamboo gets harvested and the plantation is good enough, the bamboo needs to be cut into a proper length (around 2800mm) that facilitates optimum production of bamboo veneer.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Splitting into thin strips</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Essentially, the bamboo trunks (those measuring around 2800mm) has to be split from its original form and made into very small and fine strips, for the round slices are rendered unfit for production.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Slicing into specific sizes</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Splitting makes the bamboo strips very rough. They cannot be used in such form under any cost. So, the best way to use it would be a slicing up a bit to make it a little smooth (not much!).</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Boiling bamboo strips</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Why do you think boiling is important? Actually, boiling helps the bamboo plant to get rid of its excess sugars, starch and protein content, which attracts insects to a great extent. Once the plant is free from any pests or insects, it’s easy to extract veneer and thereby use the same.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Carbonization</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Think of carbonized bamboo veneer and you will know what impact carbonizing can have, which makes it a must. For starters, carbonizing is a process wherein the bamboo strips are put inside the carbonizing tank for close to 4-6 hours and that too in 200 or 300 centigrade.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Drying the strips</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Once the boiling phase is over, the moisture content in the bamboo is too high. Drying makes the moisture level come down. After that, the bamboo veneer’s quality becomes stable and no bending happens.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Slicing (again)</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">So the bamboo strips need to be sliced into very thin and smooth parts. Only after this is done, the plant will be ready for the next step.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Sorting out strips</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Basically, bamboo is a very natural material so colour varies considerably. Most manufacturers know that the strips need to be sorted out carefully as per the colour for maintaining evenness. It might not be 100% even, but to the best of capacity.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Pressing strips together to get the block</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Perhaps many aren’t aware of this, but pressing is ideal to extract bamboo veneer. In the process, the presser used is close to 800 tons and is a hot presser. This ensures that the bamboo block that comes out is of a better quality.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Soaking the block</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Blocks of bamboo cannot be sliced into thin veneer directly. This is largely because of the hardness. It is for this reason that the block needs to be placed inside a nig water tank and for 24 hours.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Slicing the block into veneer</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">So once the bamboo block has been soaked in the water and the time is complete, the slicing process begins to extract veneer. Usually, the slicing is quiet fast. After that, the veneer that comes out becomes a small roll and not flat.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Drying bamboo veneer</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Since the block has been soaked in water for almost a day, the moisture content is very high, Drying is a must and the veneer taken should be of less moisture. This will make the bamboo veneer flat, once dried.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Checking on quality</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">There can be slight defects on the veneer and requires fixing at the earliest. </span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Clipping veneer</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The tolerance in length and width is common in production, but must be cut before packaging to suit the requirements of customers.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Packing</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Once over, packing is done on the basis of customer orders.</span></span>
</p></p>
