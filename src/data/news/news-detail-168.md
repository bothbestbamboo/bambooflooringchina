</span></p>

</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Why you should choose bamboo flooring?&nbsp;</span></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">In short, bamboo flooring is a natural surface coating material which is produced from Bamboo. It is a great option among other traditional wood floors. Let us understand the advantages and disadvantages of this type of flooring, with the hope that you will find this information useful.</span>

<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo is a durable product:&nbsp;</span></span>

</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">High-quality bamboo flooring</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;"> will last as long as traditional wood flooring. <span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>

<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">- Bamboo is elegant:&nbsp;</span></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">For those who are attracted by a clean and modern aesthetic, bamboo flooring lends itself perfectly to this style. Although similar to wood, the differences can be subtle.</span>

<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">- Colors and many styles:</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">One of the advantages of bamboo flooring is that it is available in a variety of styles and colors. Note: It is important to know what the different colors represent because the dark colors usually result from a heat treatment that has softened the bamboo.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">- Bamboo is good for the environment:</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">As bamboo is a fast-growing grass; it reaches full maturity in five to seven years; bamboo is considered by many to be much more durable than traditional wood. Because the use of natural materials is currently at the heart of many people‘s concerns in building a house, bamboo flooring is an ideal choice for many eco-friendly people.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">- Bamboo is easy to maintain:</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo flooring requires little maintenance because it can be easily cleaned with mild soap; no special treatment is needed.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">- Repairing of Bamboo floor is possible:</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">As bamboo flooring can dent, scratch or fade over time, it is good to know that they can be repaired if needed. Generally, the installation of bamboo flooring involves many topcoats so that bamboo can protect itself. If necessary, simply sand the surface and then reapply a top coat to give a boost to your floor.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">- A bamboo parquet resists water:</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">In comparison with traditional wood flooring, bamboo comes out early as being more resistant to water damage and stains.</span>

<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">-&nbsp; Bamboo flooring is not expensive:</span></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Although bamboo is an exotic wood, it can be less expensive than a traditional wood floor.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">- Bamboo flooring is subject to scratches:</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">A poor quality bamboo floor can get scratched easily, it is difficult or impossible to keep a completely smooth bamboo floor. Furniture, animals, and even heeled shoes can damage the surface of the floor over time, even the tiny particles present in everyday life.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">- A bamboo floor gets moisture easily:</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Relying on the size of your place, bamboo flooring may get worse in very humid or dry environments. </span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">- A bamboo floor can be toxic:</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Some bamboo flooring from China may contain high levels of toxic chemicals, such as glues and formaldehyde-based finishes. Make sure the bamboo flooring you want to buy is certified and high quality based. Although bamboo is presented as a very environmentally friendly option, sometimes forest practices can be irresponsible and harmful to the environment.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
