What, then, distinguishes "solid wood composite flooring" from "pure solid wood floor heating flooring"? In contrast to Jiusheng pure solid wood floor heating flooring, which is made from an entire piece of wood and pure natural glue, solid wood composite flooring is clearly stratified from a structural perspective, with a layer of glue applied to the two surfaces between each layer to hold them together.</span><br>

<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
What makes 0 glue so eco-friendly, then? The national standard now only specifies environmental protection levels for composite products. Since pure solid wood is a natural material and the national standard does not require formaldehyde detection, it is acknowledged as the healthiest flooring category. To spread the word, the most strict environmental protection standard in the world, ENF level, is currently the highest level of environmental protection for domestic composite products. Even so-called ENF-level solid wood composite flooring, also known as formaldehyde-free flooring, has the unstated risk of producing too much formaldehyde in hot summer temperatures and floor heating settings. Consequently, using 0 glue takes environmental protection a step further by removing formaldehyde products from their source.</span><br>
