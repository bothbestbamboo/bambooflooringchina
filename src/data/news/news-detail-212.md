<p><p><font face="Verdana"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">In fact, with the gradual rise of healthy home life, the energy saving and environmental protection of building materials products are also more and more concerned by consumers. The future floor market will also develop around the word environmental protection, and bamboo flooring will also become the trend of products. So what is the reason for the embarrassing situation that bamboo flooring is prosperous in domestic production, but consumption is not strong?
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Industry embarrassment: the market is full of fragrance outside the wall, lacking the right to speak and pricing
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo flooring has not been in the Chinese market for a long time. It started in the 1980s. The technology has progressed rapidly, the development momentum is strong, and the technology is becoming more and more mature. Large enterprises are equipped with tenoning machines imported from Germany. The imported paint line adopts paints and adhesives imported from Europe, with high processing precision and stable product quality.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">More popular with international consumers. In the past few years, the large bamboo floor production enterprises have mainly exported, and the export volume has increased year by year, and exported to Europe, the United States, Canada and Southeast Asian countries, accounting for more than 80% of the industry. It is more popular in cities in the northeastern region of China, and its sales volume is rising rapidly. Beijing and other cities also show a steady upward trend, so its market prospects are considerable.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">As the domestic bamboo flooring industry has always been export-oriented, enterprises have always relied on the international market, and thus lacked attention to the domestic market, which not only led to the fragrance of bamboo flooring inside the wall, but also caused the market pricing power of China‘s bamboo flooring industry to be developed by Europe and the United States. State control. In addition, although China is the country with the largest bamboo planting area, the production machinery and core accessories are imported from abroad.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">China is only an assembly workshop and raw material production base in the international market, which leads to the lack of international voice in the domestic bamboo flooring industry, and the market is attached to the international market, so the development of the industry is subject to the international market. For example, Anji, as a domestic bamboo town, does not have a company or brand with international influence. Basically, it is OEM for others.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Product embarrassment: sandwich biscuits lack appeal and lose the core competitiveness of the category

<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo itself is a relatively environmentally friendly material, and its bamboo is also a relatively rich resource in my country. The domestic bamboo forest area reaches more than 5 million hectares. The area and output of bamboo resources rank first in the world, and the performance is the best in the world. , About 90% of the bamboo forests with the highest utilization value are distributed in the southern provinces of my country. However, my country has been facing the contradiction of the shortage of wood for a long time. The use of bamboo instead of wood has provided a good development opportunity for the development, utilization and processing of rich bamboo materials.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">However, because domestic bamboo flooring products are between the laminate flooring and solid wood flooring markets, the low-end market is squeezed by laminate flooring and ceramic tiles, and the high-end market is suppressed by solid wood flooring and solid wood composite flooring. In the first floor, bamboo flooring is used as a product from foreign trade to domestic sales.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Not only lack of market promotion, but also the core value of the category, so that the market cannot form a trend. </span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The embarrassing situation of not going up and down has led domestic bamboo flooring companies to blindly follow mature categories to market when operating in the domestic market, and operate the market by means of price manipulation. As a result, investment in brand building and market promotion has been neglected.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Market embarrassment: there are many brands without leading companies, no consumption atmosphere and no guidance
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The area and output of domestic bamboo resources rank first in the world, and about 90% of the bamboo with the best performance and the highest utilization value in the world is distributed in China.
