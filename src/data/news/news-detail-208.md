<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">1. What is Bamboo Wood Flooring?
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo wood floor, also a kind of composite floor, is mainly a floor made of bamboo and wood. After more than 20 procedures such as inspection of original bamboo, cutting, punching, cutting and planing, it is made. The panels and bottom plates of the bamboo floor are made of bamboo, and the core material in the middle is made of wood such as fir, camphor and so on. There are two kinds of bamboo floors on the market, one is a more natural color, the color of this kind of bamboo floor is even and has the natural texture of bamboo, and the other is that the bamboo floor is artificially painted, it can choose various kinds. The same color, but the texture of the bamboo is not very clear. Bamboo floors are mainly used for floor decoration and wall decoration of decoration.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">2. What are the advantages of bamboo and wood flooring?
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">1. The appearance of bamboo and wood flooring is relatively natural, and the texture is also very delicate. Not only does it have advantages in appearance, but its performance is also very excellent. The surface is very hard, so it is durable. The core of the bamboo floor is wood as the raw material, so it has excellent stability and is not easy to deform.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">2. Bamboo floor has a very excellent feature, that is, "warm in winter and cool in summer", the main factor is that the board surface and back board are made of bamboo.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">3. The installation of bamboo and wood floors is very simple, because it can be directly paved without installing wooden keels, which reduces labor costs and costs. Moreover, the material price of bamboo flooring will be lower than that of solid wood flooring.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">3. What are the disadvantages of bamboo and wood flooring?
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">1. Bamboo and wood floors are made of adhesives. Sometimes the adhesives are not glued well and fall off easily, which will easily lead to delamination.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">2. Bamboo and wood flooring is not waterproof because the core material is wood, and it is easy to expand after encountering water, and its shrinkage performance is poor. If it is in a humid environment for a long time, it is easy to mold.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">3. Bamboo and wood floors cannot be used for floor heating, because bamboo and wood floors will change according to changes in temperature.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Fourth, how to choose bamboo flooring?
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">1. Moisture content: The humidity in each area is different, so when purchasing wooden floors, you need to choose bamboo floors according to the humidity of the area, because bamboo floors are easily tarnished or deformed due to the influence of time, so you need to pay attention. Whether the moisture content of the bamboo floor is suitable for the local area.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">2. Bonding technology: Bamboo floor is a composite floor made of bonding, so we need to pay attention to whether the bonding process meets the standard when purchasing, otherwise it will be easy to delaminate later.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">3. Appearance: Bamboo floor is easy to deform and expand when it encounters water or humid air, so we need to pay attention to whether each surface of the bamboo floor is covered with paint to avoid moisture.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo flooring is a relatively cost-effective material in terms of decoration and the product itself, but it is easy to deform when it is wet, so we need to take waterproof and moisture-proof measures first to avoid deformation and other phenomena during later use.</span></font></p></p>
