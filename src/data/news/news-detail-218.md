</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">What is the production process of outdoor bamboo and wood flooring manufacturers
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">1. Cut off the skewed end formed by the root of the original bamboo to form a flat mouth.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">2. Using the port as the positioning surface, cut a certain length of bamboo tube from the root to the tip.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">3. When encountering parts with large curvature, they should be twisted into short bamboo tubes. The crack part of the original bamboo should be cut according to the length of the crack, so as not to affect the use of bamboo in other parts.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">4. The cutting allowance should be reasonably reserved when cutting. Generally, if the length of the finished flooring product is 910mm, the cut-off length should be 950mm. If the length of the finished floor is 610mm, the cut-off length should be 650mm.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">In fact, before the production of outdoor bamboo and wood flooring manufacturers, for the selection of raw materials, the cylindrical bamboo materials are processed into bamboo pieces of equal width, etc., and then follow-up production is carried out according to the general wood processing method. This is completely different from bamboo flattening or bamboo curtain weaving of bamboo plywood. Select fresh, four-year-old or older original bamboo to be cut into a bamboo tube according to a preset length by a cutting machine, commonly known as cutting.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">In addition, the original bamboo used for the production of outdoor bamboo floors is usually 7m to 9m long, the eyebrow circumference is more than 300mm, and the weight is 25kg/root to 35kg/root. The thickness of the bamboo wall of the original bamboo is . Sharp, curved and shipping. The degree of damage in the process varies. Therefore, when cutting, attention should be paid to the selection of materials to improve the utilization rate of bamboo and reduce production costs.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The bamboo tube cut by the cutter is sawed with a coaxial double saw blade opener to obtain bamboo pieces of equal length. The width of the bamboo is determined by the spacing between the saw blades, which can be adjusted as needed. Generally speaking, the wider the width of the bamboo, the greater the amount of cutting when shaving bamboo green and bamboo yellow. The utilization rate of bamboo is low. However, if the bamboo pieces are too narrow, the loss of the road will increase.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">There are many aspects to pay attention to, for example, the bamboo pieces after sawing the bamboo tube are actually part of the circular arc of the bamboo tube. Because the green tissue of bamboo is dense and tough, the surface is also covered with a layer of wax. Bamboo yellow vascular bundles are sparse.
