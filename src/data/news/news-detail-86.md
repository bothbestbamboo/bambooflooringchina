<p><p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bothbest, a manufacturer and supplier of various sorts of bamboo products, has its manufacturing facility in Xuancheng, a city in Anhui province of China where sustainably harvested bamboos are easily available. The company now draws obvious advantages from its proximity to biggest bamboo reserve in China, and offers significant cost advantages to its buyers. The owners, however, maintained that they do not only offer cost-effective, but also sustainable solutions to their buyers in China and all over the world.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">We only source bamboos that are harvested for 5 to 8 years at length. We only procure bamboos that are sustainably harvested. The careful selection of the raw materials and the equipment results in increased stability and durability of the </span><span style="font-size: 12pt;"><a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com">bamboo flooring</a></span><span style="font-size: 12pt;"> products. Besides, due to the significant location advantages, we are able to produce bamboo products, more specifically flooring and decking products, at a significantly low cost, said a sales and marketing executive of the China based company.</span></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">We also use top-flight German and Italian milling equipment, which reduces the downtime and increases the overall productivity of our factory. Our skilled local workforce is certainly an asset and we want to increase our capability in the future as well, added the sales and marketing executive. The company is now aiming to increase its domestic and overseas sales. At the same time, the company wants to ensure top-quality as they do not want to compromise on the quality.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-size: 12pt; font-family: Verdana;">Since our inception, we have been consistent with quality as offering high value for money has been our longstanding objective. We will certainly focus more on keeping the bamboo decking manufacturing costs low. Besides, our increased focus on bringing more variety and diversity in our products speaks volumes about our immediate and future business goals. We want to comply with the product quality standards set by Chinese and international regulatory agencies, told the CEO and managing director of the company. He said that the company might participate in a few trade fairs and similar events in the next couple of months to promote their sustainable and cost-effective bamboo products.</span>
</p></p>
