<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 11pt;">Wood flooring has long been a hallmark of beauty, warmth, and longevity in interior design.&nbsp; Among the many hardwood species available, American Walnut (Juglans nigra) distinguishes out for its deep, rich tones and distinctive grain patterns.&nbsp; When created into <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com/products/product-type-BambooFlooring.html">engineered wide plank flooring</a>, it becomes a premium alternative for both home and commercial settings.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
This article discusses the elegance of American Walnut engineered flooring, its origins, available sizes, recommended installation methods, routine maintenance, and leading suppliers in the U.S.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
American Walnut Wood‘s Beauty</span></span>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
In North America, American walnut, often known as black walnut, is one of the most sought-after hardwood species.&nbsp; This wood species, which is well-known for its opulent chocolate-brown tones, lends refinement to any area.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Features of the American Walnut:</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Variations in color include lighter brown and deep chocolate, with hints of purple.&nbsp; The wood‘s patina becomes a little lighter with time.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Grain Pattern: It has a highly ornamental aspect because to its straight grain with sporadic curls and waves.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Hardness: It is softer than oak but still robust for indoor flooring, with a Janka hardness value of 1010 lbf.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Workability: Walnut is a popular choice among flooring makers because it is simple to mill and sand.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Sustainability: American walnuts, which are mostly sourced from the US, are farmed ethically using sustainable forestry methods.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Origin Country and Source</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Native to the eastern United areas, American walnuts are widely distributed in areas including Indiana, Missouri, Ohio, and Pennsylvania.&nbsp; The wood is valued for its superior lumber, which is frequently utilized in exquisite flooring, cabinets, and furniture.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
FSC-certified American walnut is used by reputable flooring manufacturers to guarantee ethical forestry practices.&nbsp; With stringent grading guidelines established by the National Hardwood timber Association (NHLA), the United States is one of the biggest manufacturers of walnut timber.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Flooring Sizes Available</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
American Walnut offers engineered broad plank flooring in a range of sizes to accommodate various interior requirements.&nbsp; Wide planks give rooms a more airy and opulent sense while highlighting the wood grain‘s inherent beauty.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Typical Dimensions:</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>

<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Lengths of planks: 2 to 10 feet; longer planks give a more continuous appearance.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>

Options for Thickness:</span></span></div>

<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Suggested Installation Techniques</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
American Walnut wide planks, an engineered flooring product, are more stable than pure hardwood.&nbsp; Because the HDF core or multi-layer plywood minimizes expansion and contraction, it is perfect for a variety of installation settings, including radiant heating rooms.</span><br>

<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Regular Upkeep to Promote Longevity</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
American walnut is a high-end flooring option, and it needs to be properly cared for to keep looking beautiful.&nbsp; Despite being more robust than solid wood, engineered planks nevertheless require regular upkeep to protect their finish and avoid damage.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Cleaning on a daily and weekly basis: Use a vacuum or sweeper to get rid of any dirt or debris that can scrape the surface.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Apply a pH-neutral wood floor cleanser to a damp, but not wet, microfiber mop.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Steer clear of harsh chemicals, steam mops, and too much water since these can harm the finish.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Preventive Actions:</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
To avoid scratches, use felt pads underneath furniture.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
To reduce wear, place mats or rugs in high-traffic areas.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
To stop wood from moving, keep interior humidity levels between 35% and 55%.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
To prevent scratches on surfaces, keep pet nails clipped.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Long-Term Care: If you‘re using an oil-based finish, re-oil or renew it every few years.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
When the wear layer begins to show wear, lightly sand and refinish.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
A professional flooring expert should be consulted for spot repairs if there are serious scratches or dents.</span></span></div>
