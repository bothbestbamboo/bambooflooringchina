<p><p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Guangde, Xuancheng, China, March 27, 2017 – There are numerous building industry professionals, architects and others who take the help of eco-friendly building materials to design a green or energy-efficient building. Bothbest offers an amazing bamboo panel range that is environmentally green, stable and adjustable to help incorporate in the architecture of green, sustainable and energy efficient constructions.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">The company has a wide selection of EcoNice </span><span style="font-size: 12pt;"><a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com/products/product-type-BambooPanels.html">bamboo panels</a></span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">, available in different thicknesses and colors. One can choose from panels available in single layer or multiple layers and can use them in buildings in a desired manner. They also have panels with horizontal grains or vertical grains to choose from. These panels show the natural knuckles and grains of bamboo and look natural and rustic with their visible bamboo stalks. With the visible growth patterns of bamboo and its original color, the panels give a natural and luxurious feel to a room.</span></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">According to the spokesperson of the company, the popularity of bamboo products is growing worldwide, as bamboo is a green and recyclable material. Architects now use bamboo panels a multi-purpose material to decorate their buildings. Bothbest supplies top quality and consistently manufactured EcoNice panels made of bamboo that can have a wide range of applications in a building, such as countertops, desks, cabinets etc. These are now more commonly used in architectural panels, offering an architect the desired flexibility and versatility to decorate a room or a building.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Besides, bamboo panels can also be used in making a variety of furniture pieces, such as doors, kitchen cabinets etc. Available in elegant colors and in excellent density, these panels are easy to process to be used in different applications. The spokesperson reveals that they use urea-formaldehyde (UF) adhesive in their panels that emit low volatile organic compound (VOC) and hence are very environment-friendly. They use multiple layers which are glued together to form a robust board to enhance the durability of the application.</span>
