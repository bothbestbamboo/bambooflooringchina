<p><p><span style="font-family: Verdana;">
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">With the rapid development of economy and society, more and more people pay attention on healthy lifestyle, for example, they are likely to choose environmentally friendly building materials for home decoration. Under such circumstance, <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com">bamboo floor</a> will become people’s wise choice. In fact, in recent years, bamboo flooring gradually comes into people‘s sight because of its better environmentally friendly feature. And I am sure in the future bamboo flooring will lead the trends in floor market.
</span></span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana; font-size: 12pt;">
Let’s talk about the market prospect of bamboo floor in China. According to related statistical data, the total area of bamboo forest in China is as many as 5 million hectare, and both its planting area and annual output is the Number one in the world. In other words, bamboo is the abundant natural resources in China. In addition to its quantity, bamboo’s quality is so high, as is know to all, moso bamboo is the best bamboo among all these varieties because of its high application value and better performance, 90 percent of moso bamboo all over the world now is in provinces in the south China. In the past, bamboo‘s market requirement exceeds supply, and now with fast development of bamboo flooring industry, such contradictory will be remitted.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Compared with wooden floor, bamboo flooring has its remarkable advantages</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">.</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;"> Firstly, the market price of solid wooden floor is about $30 to $ 50 per square meter, what’s more, pure solid wooden floor’s price is as higher as $70 to $ 90 per square meter, which is too much higher than bamboo flooring. Secondly, bamboo raw material itself is so elegant, therefore people will feel more high-end when using bamboo flooring for decoration.
</span></span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana; font-size: 12pt;">
Compared with solid wood composite floor, environmentally friendly characteristic is its advantage. Every year, there are too many fresh bamboos, after its growing period and stable phase, its texture will be reduced year by year and then become fragile, therefore bamboo should be chopped down every year but its growing environment will not be changed, it is so friendly to environment.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">
Compared with intensified wood floor, bamboo flooring has less chromatic aberration. As you know, bamboo‘s radius is more less than that’s of trees, and it will not be affected by sunlight, as a result, there is no obvious night side and sunny side. At the same time, due to abundant bamboo texture, its color and lustre are uniform. In theory, the working life of bamboo floor is as long as 20 years because it is more stable than other wood floors.</span>
</span></p></p>
