<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo flooring Wax</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">A wax coat needs periodic buffing using complex, expensive and giant machinery. This would be too much effort to make for most of the people. This is one of the reasons, a wax surface coating is only suggested for a bamboo flooring in obvious applications of commercial. A urethane and proper finish are more suitable for most installations. Once a wax treatment has been applied on the bamboo flooring then no other type of coating would be required on it.&nbsp;</span></span>

<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Regular cleaning&nbsp;</span></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Small dust particles of sand and another type of bacterial pollutants represent the biggest threat to the beauty of the surface and health of the people living around. These tiny dust particles behave like the shingles of a portion of sandpaper and when people walk over the surface, they are more tend to travel across the house and cause various infections to people living there.&nbsp;</span></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">In order to prevent these small dust particles, travel across the house, you should either vacuum or sweep a bamboo floor constantly. Do not let the coarse material to build upon the floor or they blemish the floor’s surface over time.&nbsp;</span></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Mats and rugs can also be a reason through which dust particles travel into the house from one corner to another, so to prevent it, make you are cleaning the rugs and mats daily with a mop. You can use cleaning products that are specifically manufactured to clean bamboo floors.&nbsp;</span></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Water&nbsp;</span></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">As compared to the hardwoods, bamboo is more unaffected to damage caused by water. Though, it is still dangerous to clean the bamboo floor with water. Never clean the bamboo floor with a wet mop or immense water no matter what. It can still cause damage to your floor like it might lose its shine.&nbsp;</span></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">If by mistake, liquid spilt on the bamboo floor, it required to wiped off immediately with cotton cloth so that it leaves no water drop left on the floor. If you are using bamboo cleaning products that are required to work with water then make sure you are using very less amount of water in it or as per the instructions given on the product. You can also use non-greasy products to clean the bamboo floor. For more information, you can confirm it from the experts of bamboo flooring, they will tell you the name of the best products and methods to clean the surface.&nbsp;</span></span>
</span></p>

<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>

<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Maintenance through prevention&nbsp;</span></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">In wet milieu, place mats or rugs in front of high dampness places, for example, in front of bath stalls or sinks. Be sure that these rugs don’t have any backing of rubber as it will suffocate the bamboo which eventually led to the discolour.&nbsp;</span></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Make sure that heavy furniture shouldn’t be dragged over the bamboo floor. You must also put felt pads or thick fabric under the legs of heavy furniture so that it does not cause any divot to the floor. If your furniture contains casters, do not forget to replace with felt supported casters or removed permanently.&nbsp;</span></span></span></p></p>
