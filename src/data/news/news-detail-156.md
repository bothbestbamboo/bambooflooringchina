<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;">Fashionable and eco-friendly, bamboo is durable and looks more interesting than many other types of wood. A rich bamboo flooring adds style and elegance to the kitchen. <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com">Bamboo floor</a> is a hot trend for modern kitchens, bamboo is a mixture of stylish flooring and durability. Although technically it is grass, bamboo is actually more difficult than many types of wood. Bamboo is supplied in plates or boards in various sizes, colors, patterns and textures. Bamboo parquet is a real Eco-chic surface, the floor on which is quite comfortable to stand. So you can feel comfortable in the kitchen.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size:14px;">What you need to know when choosing bamboo flooring, and what care for the bamboo floor is required?&nbsp;</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size:14px;">Bamboo floor is durable and durable, but it can be scratched and dents remain on it, which means that it is contraindicated to communicate with the claws of pets and dragging heavy objects. Therefore, high-quality polishing of parquet is a reliable alternative to expensive repairs, and the perfect kind of flooring in your home!</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size:14px;">The substrate must be perfectly smooth, otherwise the bamboo will show all its shortcomings. Because bamboo is very susceptible to moisture, and a waterproof backing layer is vital if bamboo parquet is installed on a concrete slab. Bamboo is produced in many colors, but natural bamboo is light, and it is an excellent choice for those who prefer an eco-friendly style in their home. Bamboo floors contain an aqueous base, and do not contain solvents, which contributes to improving the quality of the air in the house.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size:14px;">Bamboo is made from renewable resources, unlike wood, bamboo quickly ripens in about six years (and not 60 as trees). Collecting bamboo does not kill plants, when the shoots are cut off, the roots remain intact and young shoots can grow freely.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size:14px;">Bamboo flooring brings happiness</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size:14px;">Unlike previous eras today, decorating the interior of your house, more and more often the focus is not sophistication and luxury, but environmental friendliness and functionality, simplicity and airiness. That is why many choose bamboo floors in the interior, natural or oriental ethnic styles. For these styles, the most common use of natural materials, which includes bamboo.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size:14px;">According to today‘s trendy ecological trends, and the recommendations of Feng Shui, bamboo products bring happiness to the apartment. The bamboo interior of the room can be very different, and bamboo can be used in many ways:</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size:14px;">Bamboo, as a finishing material.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size:14px;">The wallpaper from the split bamboo gives the walls a unique look and creates a warm cozy atmosphere in the house. Bamboo wallpaper is resistant to sun rays and mechanical damage. To give them freshness, you should only wipe the wallpaper with a damp cloth.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size:14px;">Bamboo panels are ideal for rooms with high humidity (baths, pools, toilets). Weaving at bamboo panels, as a rule, large and multilayered. Panels are made from mats that are hand-weaved and impregnated with acrylic glue, and then pressed.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size:14px;">The ceilings can also be decorated with bamboo panels of finer weaving, and the slots between them are sealed with special slats, which are biscuit bamboo shoots that are easily glued. In addition, this way you can create the illusion of a two-level ceiling.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size:14px;">Bamboo in the interior, as an additional element.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size:14px;">Bamboo trunks are a unique material for creating interior partitions, decorative curtains, screens for heating batteries and decorative columns. Curtains and bamboo blinds create unusual comfort and coziness. Chinese craftsmen make bamboo dishes, flower vases, ashtrays, boxes for sweets and other items.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size:14px;">The convenience of working with bamboo flooring is also conditioned by the fact that this relatively new type of flooring is flat, as well as from any other wood.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size:14px;">With the latest technology, manufacturers are turning bamboo stems into a high-quality ultra-smooth floor to meet the rapidly growing demand for this exceptionally stylish floor covering.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size:14px;">Like other wooden floors, the processed reed is supplied in short laminated strips, boards or tiles, proceeding from this installation is carried out in the same way as any other similar type of flooring material. Being tough and durable, bamboo parquet will last for many years, if it is properly installed and maintained.</span><br>
