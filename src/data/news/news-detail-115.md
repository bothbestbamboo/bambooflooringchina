<p><p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Nowadays, flooring concepts related to bamboo are gaining popularity in urban cities as well. It is worthwhile to note that a majority of bamboo products originate from China. It is a type of flooring that is completed by bamboo sticks. Its rise in popularity can be attributed to the fact that many urban households prefer <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com">bamboo flooring</a> because it keeps the house cool during hot and humid summer season. Furthermore, this type of flooring gives a rustic and retro look to the style of the house. Also, the maintenance cost of bamboo flooring is much less as compared to other traditional concepts of flooring. Amidst all these exciting new flooring ideas, the idea of China </span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">bamboo flooring</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;"> is slowly gaining popularity. </span></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Characteristics of China Bamboo flooring: The reason behind the increasing popularity of bamboo flooring can be attributed to the fact that it has qualities that have a close resemblance to hardwood floors. In the process of selling the bamboo flooring to its potential customers, the bamboo floor manufacturers strengthen its wood capabilities that can be easily felt without the help of any machines like saw cutters. With the advancement of newer technologies, it has become seemingly possible to increase the strength of bamboo flooring. Certain chemicals are increasingly used so that the China bamboo flooring made in certain households is sturdy and can withstand the impact of heavy appliances present in the household. </span><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">In certain test processes, the strength of China Bamboo flooring is more than that of white oak and rock maple. This phenomenon has highlighted the need of bamboo to make stable urban household carpet. Moreover, the benefits of bamboo flooring lie in the fact that it is ecologically friendly. No harmful chemicals are either ground or used during the installation process. Further, this type of flooring is water resistant. This is why it is extensively employed in tropical climates to resists water from seeping into the house. On the other hand, it can be easily maintained. This refers to the fact that households have to spend less on shining the surface of their floors. </span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Advantages of this kind of flooring: The utilization of natural resources is of utmost importance to maintain the ecological balance in the growing depleting nature of natural elements. Moreover, the use of such flooring is a craze among the construction companies in the recent times. </span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The price of bamboo flooring is considerably low as compared to other traditional forms of flooring. This is the primary reason behind their popularity in flooring concepts. Also, they can also be given other shapes from which several other floor decoration techniques can be obtained. On the other hand, nowadays people are becoming more conscious about the ecology of planet earth. Hence, they to showcase these values through making floors made of bamboo.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Furthermore, bamboo flooring is more durable in nature that makes them first choice of flooring among potential buyers. These types of China bamboo flooring are sturdier and can be used for other purposes apart from flooring like building boats, doors and constructing other household furniture. However, the concept of un-carbonized bamboo is slowly coming to the fore which would further elevate the image of bamboo flooring. Moreover, bamboo flooring is a trendy concept that has the same feel as that of a hardwood floor. </span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Disadvantages of this kind of flooring</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Every new design has its own set of pros and cons. Bamboo flooring has certain disadvantages. Polishing the bamboo flooring is a frequent problem that is increasingly encountered by buyers and households. It is so because bamboo loses its shine over a period of time. In addition, bamboo floor emits volatile organic chemicals. Most of the bamboo floorings have certain adhesive in them that releases this kind of harmful chemical. This type of volatile organic chemical can harm the human beings when they come in contact with the China bamboo flooring. </span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">On the other hand, a bamboo floor cannot be kept safe from scratches. This is due to the reason that they have polished surface that readily loses its shine. Tiny sand particles and pet claws can easily scratch the outer portion of bamboo thus making it vulnerable to further abrasions.&nbsp;</span></span>
