The US Floors‘ Journey</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
US Floors‘ narrative is one of creativity and dedication to sustainability. Piet Dossche founded the business in 2001 as a distributor of distinctive and eco-friendly flooring options. US Floors grew its product line over time and became well-known for its commitment to sustainability and high quality.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
The launch of COREtec in 2013 marked a significant turning point for the business. Many of the drawbacks of conventional vinyl flooring, including its susceptibility to dampness and lack of foot comfort, were resolved by this ground-breaking product. COREtec revolutionised the flooring business with its waterproof core, realistic designs, and simplicity of installation.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Shaw Industries, a Berkshire Hathaway company, purchased US Floors in 2016. Through this acquisition, US Floors was able to increase its resources and reach while keeping its emphasis on sustainability and innovation. Driven by its goal of offering flooring solutions that are both aesthetically pleasing and ecologically conscious, the brand is still thriving today as a market leader in flooring.</span><br>
