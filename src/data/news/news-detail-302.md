<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Strand Woven Bamboo: What is it?</span></span>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Bamboo strands are compressed under intense heat and pressure to create strand woven bamboo, a manufactured product. A solid, long-lasting substance that is frequently stronger than conventional hardwoods like oak or maple is produced by this technique. The end product blends the strength and durability needed for outdoor use with bamboo‘s quick renewal.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Why Use Outdoor Strand Woven Bamboo?</span></span>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Bamboo is frequently thought of as a lightweight, fragile material that is perfect for indoor décor or furnishings. Strand-woven bamboo, on the other hand, is revolutionary, particularly for outdoor uses. This is the reason:</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Amazing Strength: Although bamboo has an inherent strength, the strand weaving technique increases it to a level that is on par with some of the hardest hardwoods. This makes it ideal for outdoor guardrails, grates, and handrails where longevity is crucial.</span></span>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Weather Resistance: Strand woven bamboo is perfect for outdoor settings since it is coated to resist moisture, UV radiation, and temperature changes. It can withstand warping, splitting, and rotting with the right care, making it resilient in a variety of climates.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Eco-Friendly: Some kinds of bamboo can grow several feet in a single day, making it one of the fastest-growing plants on the planet. Strand woven bamboo is an environmentally friendly substitute for conventional wood because of its quick renewability.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Aesthetic Appeal: Bamboo has a pleasant, organic look that goes well with outdoor areas. Bamboo may blend in perfectly with any decor, whether you‘re trying for a more rustic vibe or a sleek, minimalist approach.</span></span></div>
