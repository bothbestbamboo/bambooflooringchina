<p><p><font face="Verdana"><span style="font-size: 11pt;">Now, it has become an increasingly popular trend to use bamboo panels instead of wood to produce and process furniture. The excellent material and performance of bamboo boards have been recognized by consumers. <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com/products/bamboo-plywood.html">Bamboo plywood</a> board is favored by the market as an increasingly widely used furniture board.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">It is recommended that users adopt the following processing methods for bamboo boards:
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">1. Please check whether the bamboo furniture board is complete before use.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">2. If the single-layer bamboo furniture board is used in combination with other material boards, please use the substrate material in combination with the bamboo furniture board to ensure that there are basically no impurities, no grease and other substances. The composite surface of the substrate must be completely flat and free of irregularities. If the base material is wood material, the bamboo board should ensure that the moisture content of the material is close to that of the bamboo furniture board to avoid damage to the furniture board due to a large amount of hygroscopic expansion. Substrate or a lot of water shrinkage loss after compounding with bamboo furniture board.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">3. Bamboo furniture board can be compounded with various materials under the condition of using suitable adhesives. Choose your adhesive carefully and learn about the variety, properties and usage of adhesives from your adhesive dealer.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">4. Brush the glue evenly to ensure that the brushed surface covers the entire composite surface without leaving blanks.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">5. Before the adhesive is cured, mix the bamboo furniture board with the base material, and flatten the surface of the board by pressure to squeeze out the excess adhesive and air bubbles inside.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">6. If the entire multi-layer bamboo furniture board is directly used as a combination of furniture panels and other furniture accessories, please master the width and size of the bamboo furniture board. Too wide boards are prone to arch deformation, so it is recommended to use a vertical and horizontal cross structure. The beat of the bamboo furniture board is the larger area of ??the whole board.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">The correct and reasonable processing method of bamboo board can fully reflect the unique color, texture and texture of bamboo, and improper processing method will make the bamboo board unusable, which may cause the board to crack, deform, or even mold, which cannot achieve the expected effect. Therefore, it is very important to master the correct bamboo board processing method.</span></font></p></p>
