An Overview of Lumber Liquidators‘ Past</span></span>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Tom Sullivan, a building contractor who spotted a chance to offer premium flooring at affordable costs, started Lumber Liquidators in 1993. At first, the business concentrated on purchasing excess hardwood flooring straight from mills and offering it to clients at a discount. Lumber Liquidators became one of the biggest speciality flooring merchants in North America by growing its product line and forming alliances with manufacturers over time.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
The business changed its name to LL Flooring in 2020 to better represent its changing clientele and range of products. In addition to running more than 400 physical stores around the United States, LL Flooring provides a comprehensive online shopping experience. Millions of households are drawn to the company‘s pledge to offer "the highest quality flooring at the lowest possible prices."</span><br>
