<p><p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;">These days, people are being inclined to the growing popularity of bamboo floors installation. The idea works behind this innovation is its affordability, beauty and environment friendliness. A few research and reports says that it can hold a greater amount of carbon in comparison to other woods. In this way, it significantly contributes to the environment by reducing the level of greenhouse gases that cause the climatic change. Additionally, bamboo takes maximum 6 years to mature which is rather less than those which takes more than decades.&nbsp;</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;">Flooring industry is one of the most booming industry and bamboo floors come among its latest innovations that have achieved massive attention of its admirers. Besides being environmentally friendly, their aesthetic beauty looks soothing to the eyes. People can pick out from the huge array of natural colors of it. These natural shades resulted from the proper carbonization of bamboo. Among those natural colors light tan and honey brown are the most favored one.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;">Requires less maintenance</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>

<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;">Because bamboo is such a tough and flexible flooring material, and does not scratch or wear easily as other hardwood flooring, there is rather less maintenance required. Environmentally conscious people will find that, the floor which requires lesser chemicals to clean it meaning that it is much more environment friendly.</span>

<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;">Carbonized bamboo is generally softer than the natural bamboo. And the color of the carbonized bamboo is much darker. It is much more long-lasting and steady than the hardwood floors. Other reasons of its popularity are that these are stain resistant as well as moisture resistant.</span>
