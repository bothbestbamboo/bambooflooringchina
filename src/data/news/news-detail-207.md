<p><p><font face="Verdana"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">On the afternoon of May 10th, the Seminar on Technical Route Research on Carbon Footprint of Forest Products held an online meeting in Beijing. This meeting was hosted by the National Carbon Footprint Innovation Alliance (in preparation), undertaken by Beijing Green Forest Certification Co., Ltd., from Zhongnan Forestry Experts and leaders from University of Science and Technology, China Forest Products Industry Association, China Academy of Forestry and International <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com">Bamboo</a> and Rattan Center attended the meeting and delivered important speeches at the meeting.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Achieving carbon peaking and carbon neutrality is an extensive and profound economic and social systemic change. It is necessary to incorporate carbon peaking and carbon neutrality into the overall layout of ecological civilization construction. Ex-carbon peak, goal of carbon neutrality by 2060. "Achieving the "dual carbon" goal is a major strategic deployment of the Party Central Committee, and it is also a solemn commitment to combating climate change with world significance. From the perspective of the international situation, the EU carbon tariff has been passed at the EU Council on March 15, 2022. , it is likely that companies that export products to the EU will soon provide the carbon footprint of their products and impose carbon tariffs.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The government work report of the NPC and CPPCC in 2021 clearly stated that we must do a good job in the work of "carbon peaking" and "carbon neutrality". The time is short and the challenges are enormous. As a forest products industry enterprise in my country, energy saving, emission reduction, quality improvement and consumption reduction are the development priorities of my country‘s forest products industry. The key work for the next development of the industry and enterprises.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Dr. Shi Lei from the International Bamboo and Rattan Center shared the theme of "Theory and Practice of Bamboo Forest Carbon Footprint". He first made a comparative analysis of domestic and foreign carbon markets. At present, my country‘s carbon trading market still has a large room for improvement compared with foreign countries. At the same time, among the domestic certification and verification agencies, the carbon footprint and carbon verification of the forestry manufacturing industry are still blank. He enumerated the practice of bamboo in carbon sequestration and increased sequestration, and developed and promulgated two industry standards for carbon measurement of bamboo forests and bamboo products. The carbon sink value of bamboo industry has a significant impact, fully improving the carbon sink capacity of bamboo forests.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Wu Shengfu, general manager of Beijing Green Forest Certification Co., Ltd. and secretary general of the National Carbon Footprint Innovation Alliance (in preparation), gave a detailed introduction to the current work of the Carbon Footprint National Innovation Alliance. He said that the alliance is currently conducting a number of research projects. At the same time, forest products industrial enterprises, such as advanced enterprises such as wood-based panels, wooden floors, wooden doors, and wooden furniture, have taken the lead in carrying out carbon footprint certification with Beijing Green Forest Certification Co., Ltd.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Carbon Footprint National Innovation Alliance (under preparation) is convening experts and alliance members to jointly formulate the following product carbon footprint standards; particleboard/density board carbon footprint standard, plywood carbon footprint standard, blockboard carbon footprint standard, wood floor carbon footprint standard, bamboo floor Carbon Footprint Standard, Panel Furniture Carbon Footprint Standard, Cabinet Carbon Footprint Standard, Wooden Door Carbon Footprint Standard, Wooden Furniture Carbon Footprint Standard, Small Wood Products Carbon Footprint Standard, Wooden Toys Carbon Footprint Standard, Outdoor Furniture Carbon Footprint Standard, Paper and Paper Products Carbon footprint standard, wood structure building carbon footprint standard, etc.
</span></font><br>
</p></p>
