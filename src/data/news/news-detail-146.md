<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Daily maintenance for bamboo outdoor decking board</span></strong></span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">1, Do not tread on the floor within 12 hours after installation.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">2, It is recommended to wash with garden hose.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">3, If the surface of the floor is very dirty, it can be cleaned directly with undiluted detergent, but cannot be too frequently.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">4, Rub floors along the floor or bamboo lines until there is no dirt on the surface.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">5, Avoid hard object impact, sharp cutting, metal friction, etc.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">6, The deck board should be oiled every three months. Before oiling, the decking should be fully dry for 24-48 hours (depending on the local weather conditions and outside temperature), oil thickness is proportional to the times. The deck should not be exposed to water before drying.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">7, After the deck becomes dry, please use a polishing pad or polishing machine to polish the floor surface to make the floor surface more durable and beautiful.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>
</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">8, Cleaning the floor with a trioxide clean brush or a sanding floor can remove cracks and roughness on the floor. </span>
