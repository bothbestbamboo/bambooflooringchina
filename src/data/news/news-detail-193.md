<p><p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 16px;">Bamboo flooring is in vogue nowadays which has made the people more fascinated towards the benefits of bamboo flooring. Whatever we buy from the market, it is important to keep some information about the product so that one cannot deceive us in selling duplicate product to us. It is also significant to observe that what is the requirement of the area where we want to install the <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com">bamboo flooring</a>? <p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 16px;">Area where bamboo flooring is required: Bamboo flooring is suitable for any kind of place due to its durability and versatility. It is mandatory to see where the bamboo are going to be installed. Bamboo flooring looks fantastic in the living room, kitchen, lobby, bedrooms, and corridors but not suitable for bathrooms. Bathrooms are water resistant still this is the area where water is spilled all the time. Therefore, bamboo flooring will not be a wise decision. Instead any other suitable alternative which is water resistant can be used.</span>

The method of installation varies due to the kind of subfloor in your property. Thus, it is advised to consult the professionals of floor fitter before the installation of the bamboo flooring.</span>

<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 16px;">Type of flooring finish is desired: In all other kinds of flooring, there are two categories of the finishing of the floor depending upon the final choice of the owner. One is a satin matt lacquer which is more shiny and glossy and the other one is anti-slip lacquer which has matt finish and does not shine like satin matt lacquers. The anti-slip lacquer is suitable for the places which have high traffic because all kinds of visitors old or young visit the places.</span>
