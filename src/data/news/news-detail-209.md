<p><p><font face="Verdana"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo floor is a combination of solid wood floor and floor tiles. Bamboo floor is made of natural high-quality bamboo as raw material, and is made of high temperature and high pressure after painting and drying. It has the natural texture of bamboo, fresh and elegant, giving people a feeling of returning to nature, elegant and refined, and the bamboo floor has both the natural beauty of the original wood floor and the durability of the ceramic floor tile.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">(1) The texture of <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com">bamboo flooring</a> is delicate and smooth, moisture-proof, moisture-proof and corrosion-proof, and has strong toughness and elasticity. Compared with solid wood flooring, it has small color difference, high hardness, strong toughness and elasticity, and is warm in winter and cool in summer.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">(2) The growth cycle of bamboo is shorter than that of solid wood, and it is a very environmentally friendly building material. However, compared with solid wood floors, bamboo and wood floors have a single texture of raw materials, and their styles have certain limitations.
</span></font></p>
<font face="Verdana">
<p><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">(3) Bamboo floors vary in price due to different materials and processing methods, generally 90-300 yuan/m2, suitable for families who choose simple, ordinary mid-range decoration.
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 12pt;"><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Buying skills of bamboo floor
</span></font></span></p>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">(1) Appearance: Observe whether there are bubbles on the surface paint of the bamboo floor, whether it is fresh and bright, and whether the bamboo joints are too black; beautiful.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">(2) Color: The color of the natural bamboo floor should be golden yellow and the whole body is translucent; the carbonized bamboo floor should be bronze or brown with uniform color. Except for the antique floor, the color tone of the bamboo floor should be uniform, but a slight color difference that does not affect the decorative effect is allowed.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">(3) Sealing method: Pay attention to whether the bamboo floor is lacquered on six sides. Since the bamboo floor is a green natural product with capillary pores on the surface, it will be deformed due to moisture absorption, so it is necessary to seal all the surrounding areas, the bottom surface and the surface.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">(4) Age: The age of bamboo is not as old as possible. The best bamboo age is 4 to 6 years. Bamboos under 4 years old are not mature, and the bamboo is too tender; bamboos over 9 years old belong to old moso bamboo, and the skin is too thick and brittle to use. You can pick up a piece of bamboo floor and observe it. If it feels lighter in your hand, it means that the bamboo is used; if the texture is blurred, it means that the bamboo is not fresh and it is an older bamboo.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">(5) BACK. Check whether there are cracks around, whether there are any traces of ash, and whether it is clean and tidy. Check whether there is bamboo green and bamboo yellow remaining on the back and whether it is clean and tidy. After purchasing, don‘t forget to check the goods to see if there is any gap between the sample and the real thing.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">PVC flooring can greatly reduce the budget and is the best choice for short-term residences. PVC floor, also known as plastic floor, is produced with polyvinyl chloride and copolymer resin as the main raw materials. It is a new type of lightweight floor decoration material that is very popular in the world today. Many people have the impression that PVC flooring is not environmentally friendly. In fact, this idea is wrong. The qualified PVC floors are all green and environmentally friendly materials. Compared with other types of floors, the price of PVC floors is very low. Using it to decorate the floor can greatly reduce the budget. It is very suitable for short-term residences or simple decoration. There are many colors, and the wood grain type is more classy.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">(1) PVC floor has high water absorption and low strength, and it is easy to break.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">(2) PVC floor has a wide variety of colors, such as solid color, carpet pattern, stone pattern, wood floor pattern, etc., and can even be customized.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">(3) The texture of the PVC floor is realistic and beautiful, with colorful accessories and decorative strips, which can combine to create a beautiful decorative effect.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">(4) PVC floor has poor air permeability and is not resistant to sunlight, so it is not suitable for use in sunny balconies and wet bathrooms in the home.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Buying skills of PVC floor
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">(1) Appearance: There should be no cracks, fractures and delaminations on the surface. Slight wrinkles and bubbles are allowed, and slight pattern deformation is allowed.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">(2) Thickness: When purchasing PVC floor, be sure to pay attention to the thickness, the thicker the floor is usually the better the quality. Under normal circumstances, choose a plastic floor with a thickness of 2.0mm to 3.0mm and a wear-resistant layer of 0.2mm to 0.3mm.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">(3) Odor: close to the floor and smell the obvious pungent odor. If so, it proves that the raw materials are not environmentally friendly enough. Good products do not have obvious pungent odor. Even if there is some plastic smell, it will soon be able to spread.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">(4) Brand: It is difficult to judge the quality of PVC raw materials with the naked eye, and the products made of recycled materials are worse than the original materials in terms of environmental protection and durability. Choosing brand products can avoid harming health by buying recycled materials.</span></font></p>
