<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Marine plywood: What is it?</span></span>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Marine plywood is a unique kind of plywood made to resist extreme weather, especially exposure to dampness and water. In contrast to regular plywood, marine plywood is made using premium veneers and watertight glues to guarantee longevity and resistance to delamination, warping, and cracking.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Important characteristics of marine plywood</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Waterproof Construction: To guarantee that it can withstand extended exposure to water, marine plywood is made with waterproof adhesive, frequently WBP—Weather and Boil Proof glue.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Void-Free Core: Superior marine plywood is void-free, which means that there are no spaces or pockets of air between the veneer layers. This guarantees consistent stability and strength.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Rot Resistance: Suitable for outdoor or aquatic applications, a variety of marine plywood choices are treated to withstand rot, fungi, and other types of degradation.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
High Strength: Marine plywood‘s closely bonded layers provide it exceptional strength, making it appropriate for heavy-duty applications.</span></span></div>
