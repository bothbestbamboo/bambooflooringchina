<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Compared to hardwood floors, what virtues does the bamboo floor have?</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:16px;"><br>

<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Compared to hardwood floors, bamboo floors have many virtues. First of all, the bamboo flooring is a product that contributes to the environment and the bamboo growing season is much shorter than the decades of hardwood forests to mature. Each year, a "mother tree" makes several new stems that grow in their entire length in a few months to mature and give hardness in about five years, so bamboo floors have abundant natural resources. Secondly bamboo floors have higher hardness, so it is more durable than hardwood flooring. The third, bamboo floors can be installed in a very short time being this very conveniently, (as it comes ready and finished there is no need to polish, sand and give additional finishes like woods, which take up a long time until months for installation). Once installed, can be nailed or glued. Except for the above, bamboo floors have better grain and are more elegant and graceful than hardwood floors.</span></span></p></p>
