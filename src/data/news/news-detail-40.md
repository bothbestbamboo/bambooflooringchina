<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><strong>Excellent Qualities of Bamboo</strong>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;">
Selecting flooring for your house means you only want it to last for a long time. You don’t want flimsy flooring that would cost you more because you need to replace it again after a few years. Well, bamboo is a strong material and you can even compare it with other wood type floorings that are made from hard wood such as oak or walnut. The seller and manufacturer of bamboo flooring is quite important, too. Great quality is the first choice than the cheap price.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;">
Since bamboo is really sturdy, it can last for a long time&nbsp; and can survive from the elements. As a result, your flooring would last for a longer time and you don’t have to spend more when it comes to renovating your flooring.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;">
One of the factors to why bamboo flooring is sturdy is that it’s water resistant. Remember, bamboo when dried well can resist water and thus making the material last longer.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;">
As a matter of fact, because bamboo flooring is water resistant, it has been used even in kitchen renovations where other types of hardwood flooring materials are not advisable for use as flooring.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><strong>The Aesthetic Worth Of Bamboo</strong>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;">
The fact is bamboo is really not a wood at all. It is considered as a grass and it is the tallest grass. Because bamboo is a type of grass it has a unique look and texture when compared to real wood. Also it is light, besides being durable and the color can complement your home especially if the design is oriental.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;">
Well, you might be thinking that bamboo can’t be complemented on your flooring because the color is light, it can still be done if you want it dark. All it takes is to make the bamboo flooring darker by adding varnish paint or a dark colored wax and this will make the color of the bamboo darker. Remember, bamboo is versatile and you can do just about anything to it.
</span></p>
