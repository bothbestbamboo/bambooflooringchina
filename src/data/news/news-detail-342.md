E0 Grade Bamboo Flooring: What Is It?</span></span>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Bamboo flooring is created by compressing and treating processed bamboo fibres to produce long-lasting boards. The flooring‘s formaldehyde emission level is either zero or so low that it satisfies the highest international criteria for indoor air quality, hence the "E0 grade" label. Low-emission materials, such as E0 grade bamboo flooring, are essential for areas where kids spend a lot of time since formaldehyde, a volatile organic compound (VOC), can be hazardous in high concentrations.</span><br>

Bamboo can be harvested in as little as three to five years, in contrast to hardwood trees, which might take decades to reach maturity. Because of this, bamboo flooring is a more sustainable option than conventional hardwood flooring.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
