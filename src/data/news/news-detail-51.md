<strong>Understanding Strand Woven Bamboo Floorings</strong>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
Strand woven bamboo floor planks are created from a special type of bamboo plant called Moso. After cutting the bamboo stalks they are boiled for long periods, which help in removing the sugar content in them. With sugar free residue, bamboo becomes termite and insect resistant. Afterwards these boiled stalks are dried again for longer period. Now these dried bamboo strands using low VOC adhesive are pressed and compressed using high pressure for flattening them. Bamboo planks thus produced are two times harder and double denser than regular bamboo floorings.
</span></p>
