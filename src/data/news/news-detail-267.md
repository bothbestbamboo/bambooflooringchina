<span style="font-size: 12pt;">
What is Strand Woven Bamboo?</span></span>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 16px;"><br>
</span>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">
Before we dive into why strand woven bamboo is a fantastic flooring choice, let us briefly understand what it is. Unlike traditional bamboo flooring, which is made by slicing bamboo stalks into strips and gluing them together, strand woven bamboo is made by shredding the bamboo into fibers and then compressing those fibers with adhesives under extreme pressure. The result is a denser, harder, and more durable material that can stand up to heavy foot traffic, making it ideal for high-use areas like bedrooms.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">
1. Durability: Built to Last</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">
One of the most significant advantages of strand woven bamboo flooring is its incredible durability. Bedrooms might not experience as much foot traffic as other parts of the house, but they are still subject to daily use. If you have pets, kids, or even heavy furniture, strand woven bamboo can withstand the impact without showing wear.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">
Strand woven bamboo is often rated two to three times harder than traditional hardwoods like oak or maple. This means it is resistant to scratches, dents, and other damage that can occur over time. With proper care, your bedroom floor can look as good as new for years to come.</span><br>

2. Eco-Friendly Choice</span><br>

<span style="font-size: 12pt;">
