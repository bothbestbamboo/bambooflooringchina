For what reason is bamboo flooring used?</span></span>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Because bamboo flooring is made from a rapidly expanding renewable resource, it stands out as an environmentally beneficial choice. Bamboo grows to full size in 5-7 years, as opposed to 20-50 years for hardwood trees. This not only makes it a sustainable resource, but it also makes it frequently less expensive than conventional timber flooring.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Bamboo flooring is valued not only for its sustainability but also for its longevity. The exceptional hardness of strand-woven bamboo, in instance, frequently surpasses that of conventional hardwoods like oak or maple. Furthermore, because of Australia‘s varied climate, which ranges from core aridity to coastal dampness, bamboo floors are resistant to warping and swelling.</span><br>

<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Australia‘s Most Popular Styles of Bamboo Flooring</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Whether you‘re remodelling a historic home or creating a contemporary flat, bamboo flooring comes in a range of designs to suit any style. The following are some of the most popular designs among homeowners in Australia:</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
1. Woven bamboo strands</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Strand-woven bamboo is a popular option because of its exceptional toughness and elegant look. In order to create a dense and durable substance, bamboo fibres are compressed under intense heat and pressure. It is perfect for high-traffic areas like kitchens, corridors, and living rooms because of its robustness. Because of its adaptability in design, strand-woven bamboo is frequently available in natural, coffee, or carbonised tones.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
2. Grain, both vertical and horizontal</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Grain patterns that are horizontal and vertical are popular for people who want a more conventional bamboo appearance. Horizontal bamboo has a more organic and rustic appearance due to its larger grain pattern, which highlights the natural nodes of the bamboo stem. In contrast, the pattern of vertical bamboo is tighter and more consistent, giving it a sleek, modern look.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
3. Finishes with matte</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
In Australia, matte finishes are becoming more and more popular because of their subtle elegance and usefulness. Because matte flooring are less likely to reveal dust or scratches than shiny ones, they are perfect for busy homes. Modern minimalist homes are also enhanced by the subtle sheen of bamboo with a matte surface.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
4. Bamboo that is distressed and hand-scraped</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Bamboo flooring that is hand-scraped and weathered is a popular option for homeowners who appreciate the allure of vintage or industrial-style decor. These designs give character and cosiness to any area with their textured surfaces that resemble old wood.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
5. Bamboo flooring with wide planks</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 14.6667px;"><br>
