<p><p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size:14px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Not many are aware that China is the leading nation in the world that owns a variety of bamboos and also the area covered by the several species (approximately 40 genera and more than 400 types).&nbsp; With the passage of time, the <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com/products/bamboo-veneer.html">bamboo veneer</a> has become a popular one in the market, given its eco-friendly characteristic. Besides, it is also the right choice when it comes to making of modern day designs in construction projects like hotels, resorts, museums as well as vehicles.</span></span>

<span style="font-family: Verdana;">

<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">If you see it from the Indian perspective, bamboo is termed as an ‘underused grass’. With environmental benefits galore, the plant is slowly making its way to a number of uses, primarily for its availability. Today, bamboo is utilized more than timber or steel because it is a ‘fast-growing, high-yielding and sustainable natural resource’.</span>

<span style="font-family: Verdana;">

<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Why bamboo?</span></strong></span>

<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">So if you wish to pay attention to aesthetics besides focusing on a unique way of flowing, choose bamboo for its versatility. After the </span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">bamboo veneer</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;"> process gets over, the furnishings made are sent to the market, where you get it across a wide range of stores. Not to forget, the online market is equally competitive in this regard too.</span></span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">

<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">The many benefits of bamboo veneers</span>

<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Since a number of architects and interior designers have had to face questions about the environment-friendliness of their projects, bamboo veneer naturally becomes their first choice. If you are bent to do the same, consider pondering upon the advantages of going ‘all bamboo’! Here’s a list of reasons why bamboo veneer gives you an edge over several of its counterparts:</span>

<span style="font-family: Verdana;">

<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:14px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">-&nbsp;Eco friendly</span></strong></span>

<span style="font-family: Verdana;">
</span>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">There’s no doubt that bamboo veneer is one of the topmost picks for its renewable nature. Most bamboo in its grass form grows no less than a foot and half every day. So, it would be worthwhile choosing bamboo for a dose of nature in the interiors!</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">

<span style="font-size:14px;"><strong><span style="font-size: 12pt;">-&nbsp;Grain pattern</span></strong></span>

<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">There are dual grain patterns in bamboo varieties- vertical and planked. The vertical grains are usually thinner and tighter of ?” grain. On the other hand, the planked form is a thicker ?” grain, which resembles butcher blocks. The best part about both these rains is that they can come in either of the two colors, which makes a total of four different varieties in grains.</span>

<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Ideally, bamboo has its natural color and that’s more of a yellowish or bronze shade. There’s however, a carbonized color that looks like the buckskin and is of a tan shade. Whether you choose the latter or former, the colors are equally good and impart a lovely look when used.</span>
</span></p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;">

<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>

So, when you use a product that has originated from far off lands, you generally get allured, much like any other customer. But the place of origin doesn’t render much of a difference to the quality of the item, provided the manufacturing, processing and production were good.</span>
