<p><p><font face="Verdana"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bamboo plywood is a series of complex processed and exquisitely pressed bamboo pieces. In the production process of bamboo plywood, advanced anti-drying technology is adopted, so that the moisture content of bamboo plywood is controlled at about 8%-14%. High pressure adhesive can effectively suppress high temperature. Therefore, the <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com/products/bamboo-plywood.html">bamboo plywood</a> will not be surrounded by temperature for a long time to cause cracking and deformation.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Advantages of bamboo sheet:
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">1. Bamboo plywood adopts the method of splicing bamboo strips, which is made of composite bonding at high temperature, and has the characteristics of strong stability, not easy to deform, and no glue.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">2. In addition, because the bamboo plywood has taken off the sugar, starch and other substances in the bamboo, it also has the characteristics of strong anti-insect and durable. This bamboo plywood is very wear-resistant and mildew-proof.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">3. Bamboo plywood Bamboo is mainly taken from nature, replacing the original wood, and has less negative impact on the human body.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">4. The surface of the bamboo plywood is smooth, soft, supple and natural. It is a high-grade material with stable quality, and it is easy to match the color of the home.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Disadvantages of bamboo sheet:
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">1. In terms of stability, the shrinkage and expansion of bamboo panels are smaller than other panels, and are easily affected by seasonal effects, such as wet weather or hot weather in the south, bamboo panels may be slightly deformed. In addition, bamboo plywoods are also prone to scratches, so generally avoid scratching with sharp objects.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">2. The maintenance of bamboo plywoods is time-consuming and laborious. Regular waxing is required to prolong the service life, which brings inconvenience to personal friends.
</span></font></p>
