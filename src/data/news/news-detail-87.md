<p><p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">Bothbest, a top manufacturer of bamboo products from China, uses the latest manufacturing technologies and the finest bamboos as raw materials to provide a rather cost-effective and environment-friendly flooring solution to its customers spread all over China and beyond. The Xuanchen, China based bamboo products manufacturer uses bamboo since this is a low-carbon and completely renewable resource. The company takes pride in being one of the leading resource-saving <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com">bamboo flooring</a> products manufacturer in China.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">Having been spent eight years in the bamboo decking products manufacturing industry, the company now offers both finished and unfinished </span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">bamboo flooring</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;"> products. In recent years, bamboo industrialization process and bamboo export industry has grown rapidly in China, and we are proud and delighted to be a part of the industry, told a senior sales manager of Bothbest at a press conference held in Xuancheng, China.</span></span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 16px;"><br>
</span>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">It’s a dynamic and ever-changing market landscape now and the rise of new technologies and manufacturing procedures has lowered the manufacturing costs and the transport costs as well. We are happy to ride the growth and we will bring more customization in our products in the near future, added the marketing executive.</span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">There are two things that our customers precisely love our products for, the aesthetic brilliance of the bamboo decking and bamboo flooring products and the solid durability and stability of the same, he added. Bothbest, according to him, uses latest German and Italian milling equipment and Dynea European quality glue for making their products. They procure the raw materials, the bamboo shoots and other types of natural resources, from Xuancheng only, a place in Anhui province of China which is reportedly the biggest reserve of bamboos and where the company has its main manufacturing unit. </span>
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
<span style="font-size:16px;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">The CEO and managing director of Bothbest recently appeared in a press conference. “With world’s natural resources fast depleting, it’s time we should switch to more environment-friendly and renewable resources such as bamboo. Bamboo grows rapidly and is a more cost-effective and more eco-friendly alternative to hardwood”, he told the press at a conference held in Xuancheng today.</span> </span></span>
</p></p>
