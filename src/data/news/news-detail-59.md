<p><p><span style="font-family: Verdana;">
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">When being asked how to plant <a href="https://www.bambooflooringchina.com">bamboo</a> and how to maintain bamboo after being planted, can you give the right answer? If you want to plant bamboo for enjoyment of sight, or as bamboo industry to get selling profit, it is necessary for you to know related knowledge. Let us talk about matters needing attention when planting bamboo.
</span></span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">
Firstly, it is important to choose right variety of bamboo according to your living environment. As is known all, bamboo has regional planting feature, for example, </span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">some bamboos will die under too much cold environment while some bamboos will not grow very well in tropical zone</span><span style="font-size: 12pt;">, besides, some bamboos will be affected during process of growth if the ray of light is not suitable. In short, growing environment is the first factor you have to consider and then choose right bamboo variety.
</span></span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana; font-size: 12pt;">
Secondly, how to build the bamboo garden. Nowadays some bamboos will be planted in residential quarter in city so that people will enjoy its elegant appearance, this is called the bamboo garden in city. However, because of poor soil texture in city, like soil from construction site mixed, it is not easy to plant bamboo garden very well, what’s more, some bamboo will die because of rotten bamboo root. How to overcome such condition? Here is a solution you can take try, you can use 75 percent of flower mud, 15 percent of peat soil and 10 percent of sand to use as its growing soil. At the same time, if there is ponding condition in residential quarter, in fact, it is common to see such condition in new construction residential quarter, water should be drained away in time.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana; font-size: 12pt;">
Thirdly, after bamboo garden built, it is necessary to water and shadow sunlight during planting process, bamboo leaves is easy to be dried up so that it should be watered frequently.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana; font-size: 12pt;">
Then you should know how to maintain bamboo after being planted, here are some tips you can follow.
After being planted, no matter the weather, blowing and raining, bamboo should be drenched through, and then fill into soil steadfastly. This is the same as other nursery-grown plant planting.
</span></p>
<p>
<span style="font-family: Verdana;"><br>
</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 12pt;">
Every morning and every night, bamboo should be watered for a time, in addition, dead bamboo should be removed in time, and other new bamboo should be planted in this area.
It is important to cover soil carbon on bamboo after being planted.
About three months after plant, it is time to take apply fertilizer, please be careful that fertilizer should be in soil near bamboo. According to different bamboo shoot condition, the times of applying fertilizer is different, as usual, it should be two or three times every year.</span>
</span></p></p>
