<p><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 10pt;">When people choose bamboo flooring, they’re usually balancing two expectations: a natural, warm look and the ability to stand up to daily wear. The surface finish is what connects those expectations to reality. Among the various coating systems used on bamboo, Treffert coating has earned a solid reputation for enhancing scratch resistance while maintaining clarity and color stability.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
From a factory technologist’s perspective, the coating is not just a final cosmetic step. It’s a functional composite layer with a specific chemistry, curing profile and mechanical behavior that directly affects performance on site. Understanding the role of Treffert coating helps explain why some bamboo floors survive years of traffic and moving furniture with minimal visible damage, while others show micro-scratches and dulling much sooner.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
What Makes Bamboo Different from Wood in Coating Terms</span></span>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Before looking at Treffert’s role, it’s worth briefly revisiting the substrate itself. Bamboo is a grass, not a hardwood, which gives it a few special characteristics that influence finishing:</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
High silica content: Bamboo often contains more silica than typical hardwoods. Silica behaves abrasively during sanding and can affect adhesion if the surface preparation isn’t controlled.</span></span>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Density and hardness variation: Strand-woven bamboo, horizontal and vertical constructions all differ in density. Strand-woven in particular can reach very high Janka hardness, which is an advantage for dent resistance but increases internal stresses during curing.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Different fiber structure: Long, aligned fibers and the use of adhesive in strand-woven products create more interfaces. Coating must bridge these micro-variations without cracking.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Because of these factors, bamboo behaves differently from oak or maple on the coating line. A coating system that works well on classic parquet can show unexpected adhesion issues, gloss instability or premature micro-cracking on bamboo if it isn’t adapted properly. This is where specialized systems such as Treffert have made an impact.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
The Treffert Coating System: More Than Just a Top Layer</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Treffert is best known in the flooring industry for its multi-layer UV-curable coating systems. When used on bamboo, it usually consists of several distinct layers, each with a specific function:</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Primer / sealer layer</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Promotes adhesion to the bamboo or to the adhesive-rich surface in strand-woven products.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Helps to equalize absorption, which is important on mixed, high-density substrates.</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Reduces the risk of side-bonding in tongue-and-groove joints by controlling penetration.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Intermediate build coats</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Provide film thickness, flexibility and impact resistance.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Level out minor surface irregularities from sanding or pressing.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Act as a buffer between the rigid top coat and the more elastic bamboo.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Top coat(s) with abrasion and scratch resistance additives</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Contain special hard particles and cross-linking agents to resist micro-scratches.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Define the gloss level (from ultra-matte to high gloss).</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Provide the main protective barrier against household chemicals and staining.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
This layered approach means that scratch resistance is not just a single-parameter property. It’s a balance among hardness, flexibility, adhesion and surface energy.</span></span><span style="font-size: 10pt; font-family: Verdana;">&nbsp;Treffert systems are designed to find that balance specifically for flooring applications.</span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;">
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
How Treffert Coating Improves Scratch Resistance</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Scratches on bamboo flooring come in two main forms:</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Micro-scratches: Fine, shallow lines that scatter light and create dull areas, especially noticeable on darker or high-gloss finishes.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Macro-scratches: Deeper grooves caused by sharp objects, grit under shoes, furniture movement or pets.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Treffert coatings address both through a combination of material science and curing technology.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Hard, Cross-Linked Surface</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Treffert UV coatings use oligomers and monomers that form dense, cross-linked polymer networks when cured. This dense network:</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Increases surface hardness, making it more difficult for sharp particles to cut deeply.</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"><br>
</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 10pt;">Maintains a more uniform gloss level over time by resisting the surface deformation that produces dull spots.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
However, hardness alone can make a coating brittle. Treffert systems are formulated to keep enough elasticity in the intermediate layers so the system absorbs impacts without cracking.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Micro-Ceramic or Mineral Reinforcement</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Many scratch-resistant Treffert top coats incorporate micro-sized mineral or ceramic fillers. Properly dispersed and matched in refractive index, these fillers:</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Increase resistance to abrasive wear (for instance from fine sand or dust).</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"><br>
</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 10pt;">Reduce the depth of scratches by “shielding” the underlying polymer matrix.</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"><br>
</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 10pt;">Avoid obvious haze or cloudiness so the bamboo grain remains visible.</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;"><br>
</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 10pt;">From a factory viewpoint, the key is to maintain correct solids content and viscosity.&nbsp;</span></span><span style="font-size: 10pt; font-family: Verdana;">Poor mixing or inaccurate addition of these fillers can lead to settling, inconsistent resistance between production lots, or uneven surface appearance.</span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;">
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Optimized Curing Profile</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Treffert UV systems are sensitive to curing parameters. Their performance depends on:</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Lamp intensity and spectrum: Correct matching of photoinitiators and lamps ensures full cure, especially in the top layer where scratch resistance is needed most.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Line speed: Too fast and you risk under-cure, leading to softer surfaces that scratch easily. Too slow and you overheat the board, which can cause stress or discoloration.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Substrate temperature: Particularly important for bamboo, where excessive heat can cause micro-cracking or dimensional movement.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Properly cured Treffert coatings show a good balance of hardness and toughness, which improves both scratch and abrasion resistance during actual service.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Laboratory Measures vs. Real-Life Scratch Resistance</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
As technologists, we often focus on test results: Taber abrasion, scratch tests, gloss retention, etc. Treffert systems are typically validated via:</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Taber abrasion tests (e.g., CS-10, S-33 wheels): To evaluate wear-through resistance after thousands of cycles.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Scratch resistance tests (such as ISO 1518 / Martindale adaptations): To determine the load required to produce visible scratches.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Falling sand or similar abrasion tests: To simulate grit impact.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
On bamboo floors coated with Treffert systems, results often show:</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Higher cycle counts before wear-through compared with standard UV acrylates.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Reduced gloss loss in the traffic zone.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Lower visibility of fine scratches on matte and silk-matte versions.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
However, real-life conditions add variables that tests cannot fully replicate: different types of dirt, varying humidity, heavy furniture, pets and cleaning routines. Treffert coatings are engineered to perform well across this spectrum, but performance in practice still depends heavily on correct factory processing and later on-site care.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Process Factors in the Factory That Affect Scratch Resistance</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Even with a high-quality coating system like Treffert, final scratch resistance on bamboo is only as good as the production process. Several steps are particularly critical.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Surface Preparation and Sanding</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Use sharp sanding belts and appropriate grit sequences (for example 180–240 before coating, adjusted to product type).</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Control dust removal to avoid sanding dust inclusion, which can appear as micro-defects and weak points in the coating.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Avoid overheating during sanding; overheating can glaze the bamboo surface and compromise adhesion.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
A well-prepared surface allows the Treffert primer to anchor properly, which is the foundation for long-term resistance and minimizes the risk of delamination under stress.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Application Weight and Uniformity</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Scratch-resistant systems demand tight control over coating weight for each layer:</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Too thin: Insufficient film build leads to early wear-through and higher risk of micro-telegraphing of substrate defects.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Too thick: Internal stresses increase, making the film more prone to cracking on high-density bamboo and at edges.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Common application methods include roller coating and curtain coating. For each, viscosity, temperature and line speed need to be harmonized to maintain a uniform and defect-free layer.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Curing Control</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
With Treffert UV systems, lamp maintenance and calibration are essential:</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Regularly measure UV intensity and adjust lamp power, distance and reflector cleanliness.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Monitor board surface temperature at the end of the line. For bamboo, keeping the temperature within a narrow range reduces internal tensions and color shifts.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Verify degree of cure by simple methods (solvent rub tests) alongside more advanced lab checks when needed.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Stable curing conditions lead to consistent hardness and therefore more predictable scratch resistance batch after batch.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Visual and Tactile Aspects: Scratch Resistance Meets Design</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Scratch resistance must be balanced with design and feel. Treffert coatings on bamboo are available in a variety of gloss levels and textures, which influence how scratches are perceived.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Gloss Level</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
High gloss: Highlights depth and color variation, but any fine scratch is very visible. Even if the coating is technically scratch-resistant, users can see micro-swirls easily.</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Silk-matte / semi-matte: Often the best compromise for bamboo, where natural fiber patterns should be visible, but small marks should not stand out.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Ultra-matte: Very forgiving in terms of scratch visibility. Treffert’s ultra-matte systems use special matting agents that still maintain good cleanability and stain resistance.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
From a factory perspective, consistent gloss across production is essential. Any unevenness (matte patches, glossier stripes) can be misinterpreted by customers as wear or early surface damage.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Texture and Embossing</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
When Treffert coatings are applied onto bamboo with surface embossing or brushing:</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Micro-texture helps hide light scratching because the surface already diffuses reflections.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Brushed surfaces can align with bamboo’s natural fiber direction, giving a more authentic feel.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
The coating formulation must retain the texture, not flood it. Adjusting viscosity and application weight is important so the Treffert system protects the surface without leveling out the designed structure.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Interaction with Stains and Colors</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Many bamboo floors are stained, carbonized or otherwise color-modified. Treffert coatings must preserve these colors and protect them from UV and household chemicals.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Key points:</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Color stability: UV absorbers and stabilizers in the coating help slow yellowing or fading, particularly important for lighter-toned bamboo.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Chemical resistance: Treffert finishes typically withstand common household agents such as cleaning detergents, wine, coffee and oils within standard exposure times, reducing the risk of stains that might force more aggressive cleaning and increase scratch risk.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Because bamboo is sometimes more reactive to moisture and heat, the coating’s barrier effect also helps stabilize dimensional movement, indirectly reducing micro-cracking and stress lines that could later develop into scratch pathways.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
On-Site Behavior and Maintenance Considerations</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Once the coated bamboo leaves the factory, the role of Treffert coating is to serve as the front line of defense under real conditions. A few practical aspects matter for long-term scratch resistance:</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Furniture movement: Treffert coatings resist scratching better when combined with proper felt pads and regular inspection of chair glides. Hard plastic or metal feet will still leave marks on any floor.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Dirt control: Entrance mats and regular sweeping minimize grit, which is the primary cause of micro-scratches. A hard coating delays damage but cannot completely prevent it if abrasive particles are constantly present.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Cleaning products: Neutral pH cleaners compatible with UV-coated floors are recommended. Harsh chemicals or abrasive pads can attack even high-quality coatings.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Proper maintenance instructions provided by the manufacturer should clearly reference the characteristics of Treffert-type UV finishes so that end users understand what the floor can and cannot tolerate.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Sustainability and Emissions Aspects</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Bamboo is often chosen partly for environmental reasons. The coating system should not undermine that positioning. Treffert UV coatings offer several advantages related to sustainability and emissions:</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Very low VOC emissions during use: UV-curable systems are largely solid, with minimal solvent content. After proper curing, they emit very little into indoor air.</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Compliance with flooring emission standards: Treffert systems are commonly used to meet strict standards such as European emission classes and various indoor air quality labels.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Efficient energy use: UV curing lines, once optimized, allow high throughput and short cure times, which reduces energy consumption per square meter compared to some traditional oven systems.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
From a factory point of view, careful process design also helps reduce coating waste, making the overall product more resource-efficient.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Why Treffert Coating Is Well-Suited for Scratch-Resistant Bamboo Floors</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Matching bamboo with a coating system is not a simple one-to-one transfer from hardwood technology. The substrate’s density, structure and dimensional behavior require a coating that can:</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Achieve high surface hardness without becoming brittle.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Adhere reliably to a complex, sometimes silica-rich surface.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Maintain gloss and clarity while incorporating mineral reinforcement for abrasion resistance.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Cure fully under industrial UV conditions without overheating or stressing the bamboo.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Treffert’s multi-layer, UV-based systems have been engineered with these requirements in mind. When the full system is correctly applied—appropriate primers, intermediate layers, scratch-resistant top coats, and carefully controlled curing—the result is a bamboo floor with:</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Enhanced resistance to everyday scratching and abrasion.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
More stable appearance in high-traffic zones.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Good balance between protective performance and natural aesthetics.</span></span></div>
<div><span style="font-family: Verdana;"><span style="font-size: 13.3333px;"><br>
</span><span style="font-size: 10pt;">
For a factory technologist, this means that investments in line calibration, surface preparation and process control are well rewarded. The coating becomes a reliable, predictable component of the product’s performance, rather than a frequent source of complaints.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 10pt;">
Scratch resistance is ultimately a combination of material, process and use conditions. Treffert coating plays a central role on the material side, enabling bamboo floors to meet the growing expectations of both residential and commercial customers who want a natural, sustainable floor that can still cope with the realities of daily life.</span></span></div>
