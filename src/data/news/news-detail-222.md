<p><font face="Verdana"><br>

The structural composition of bamboo panels is very different from that of wood. The strength and density of bamboo boards are higher than those of ordinary wood, and the strength of bamboo products is higher than that of ordinary wood products. Bamboo products are smaller than wood products when used as a structural material. Bamboo board has straight texture, smooth texture, simple color, easy bleaching, dyeing and carbonization, which can be comparable to some broad-leaved trees. Bamboo wood composite floors and bamboo floors can replace precious hardwoods and have broad application prospects in furniture, decorations and interior decoration.
</span></font></p>
<p><font face="Verdana"><br>
<span style="font-size: 12pt;">2.
