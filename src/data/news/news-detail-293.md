<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
What is Corundum?</span><br>
<br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
A naturally occurring mineral, corundum is mostly made up of aluminum oxide (Al?O?). With a Mohs mineral hardness rating of 9, it is among the hardest materials on the market, second only to diamond. Corundum is a great material for many uses, especially flooring, because of its exceptional hardness, chemical stability, and abrasion resistance.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Different Corundum Types</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Corundum comes in two primary forms:</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Natural Corundum: This form is found in nature, frequently in metamorphic rocks as a crystalline structure. Depending on its hue, sapphire or ruby are the two most prevalent names for natural corundum.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Laboratory-controlled procedures are used to create synthetic corundum. Because of its availability and consistent qualities, synthetic corundum finds widespread use in industrial applications.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
The characteristics of corundum flooring</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Corundum stands out in the flooring business due to its special qualities:</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
1. Superior Hardness</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
The exceptional resistance to scratches and abrasions is guaranteed by corundum‘s high degree of hardness. Because of this, it is ideal for high-traffic locations such as retail stores, warehouses, and industrial settings.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
2. The Resistance to Chemicals</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Because of its exceptional chemical resistance, corundum flooring is appropriate for settings like industrial plants and labs where spills and exposure to harsh materials are frequent occurrences.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
3. Low Upkeep</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
Corundum‘s non-porous structure prevents it from absorbing liquids, which facilitates cleaning and upkeep. Usually, regular cleaning with gentle detergents is enough to keep it looking brand new.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
4. The stability of heat</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
For kitchens and industrial spaces where heat exposure occurs frequently, corundum flooring is perfect since it can tolerate high temperatures without warping or deteriorating.</span><br>
<br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
5. Visual Flexibility</span><br>
<span style="font-size: 11pt;">
From sleek modern styles to rustic ones, corundum flooring can be tailored to fit any design aesthetic thanks to its wide range of treatments and hues.</span><br>
<br>
