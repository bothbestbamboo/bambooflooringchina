Bamboo Flooring Important Features

Posted on 2014-9-18 8:57:24

Undoubtedly, hardwood flooring is the most popular flooring trend these days amongst homeowners and property developers. After stone, vinyl and tile flooring hardwood flooring have gained its much-awaited anticipation. For improving overall value of your home, for durability, for minimum maintenance and for allergen free flooring bamboo flooring is considered best flooring options. Different people have different reasons for choosing bamboo flooring, it does not matter, what your reasons are but what matters is what you know about bamboo flooring.


Advantages of Using Bamboo Flooring


#1: Unmatched Bamboo Properties


Usually when you are installing floors for your home, no one likes floors that need replacements after few years. Bamboo for sure is the hardest hardwood floor compared to other options. Considerably the strength rating achieved by bamboo is relatively higher than maple and red oak. Therefore, bamboo flooring is expected to stand tough for any kind of wear and tear for longer duration. Being natural resilient bamboo will withstand any kind of dents of pressures preserving its durability and look. Additionally bamboo with its natural water resistant quality becomes most favored option for flooring and when it undergoes lamination process then it becomes additionally resistant to wrapping and gaping.


#2: Aesthetic Appeal of Bamboo Flooring


In no manner bamboo should be mistaken for wood as it basically is a type of grass thus bamboo usually has a very distinctive look compared to hardwood flooring obtained from trees. The natural color of bamboo with some darker tints and sometimes, lighter bands from the nodes make it even more attractive and alive. When bamboo flat boards are prepared then usually they are made in such manner that they all have uniform color and tight grain matching each other. Only if you want a dark colored bamboo floor then you can think of carbonized bamboo, which makes the bamboo color darker from within using sugar present in bamboo flanks. However if you want bamboo color to suit your interior designing theme then preferring stained bamboo floors in variety of colors will offer you the color of your choice.


#3: Bamboo Floors Offer Environmental Benefits


If you are environment conscious then bamboo floor is just for you as bamboo is a grass plant, which can be harvested again in quick time compared to trees. Bamboo needs only five years to grow up to 50 feet whereas hardwood trees require at least 30 years to grow and develop that much. Although you can plant and harvest new bamboo plants usually bamboo grows from the cut sprouts needing no additional harvesting efforts. Being natural in origin, you will require fewer chemicals and fewer efforts to maintain and clean your bamboo floors compared to other floor types.
