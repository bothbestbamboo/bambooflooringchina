Installing Bamboo Inside and Out: My Experience with Strand Woven Flooring and Decking in Dubai

Posted on 2025-4-22 15:50:21

Dubai offers a distinctive blend of contemporary luxury, extreme weather, and chic interior design goals.  I live in this energetic city and work as a flooring factory technician, so I understand how important it is to select materials that are not only aesthetically pleasing but also practical and long-lasting given the harsh conditions in the Middle East. I want to be the guy to recommend UAE bamboo flooring in Dubai.

I just made the decision to improve the living room and the garden deck, two important spaces in my house.  I gave it some thought before deciding on outdoor bamboo decking for the yard and strand woven bamboo flooring for the living room.  Although these materials are both composed of bamboo, they have rather different uses and need different qualities to function properly.

Allow me to analyze these options, weighing the benefits and drawbacks, and describing why bamboo is so effective in Dubai for both indoor and outdoor uses.

The Reasons Behind My Living Room‘s Strand Woven Bamboo Selection


The focal point of my house is the living room.  We host guests there, relax after work, and spend time with our loved ones.  It required a flooring solution that was stable in spite of Dubai‘s air-conditioned interior climate, could withstand everyday wear, and looked exquisite.

Strand Woven Bamboo: What is it?

 Strand woven bamboo is created by compressing shredded bamboo fibers with resin under intense heat and pressure, as opposed to conventional horizontal or vertical bamboo.  The end product is a very strong and dense flooring material that is frequently harder than maple or oak.

Benefits of Strand Woven Bamboo Flooring: Outstanding Sturdiness
Living rooms and other high-traffic areas are ideal for it.  Strand woven bamboo resists dents, scratches, and wear better than other hardwoods.

Stable in Climate-Controlled Interiors

 Strand woven bamboo‘s strong structure keeps it more sturdy in Dubai, where air conditioning may dry out typical wood.

Eco-friendly and sustainable

 Bamboo is an environmentally friendly choice because it regenerates quickly, which is something I‘m quite concerned about as a technologist.

Chic and Adaptable

 It complements a range of décor styles, from modern to rustic, and comes in natural, carbonized, and gray tones.

Simple to Keep Up

 It only requires periodic mopping and routine sweeping.  Topcoat finishes shield it from light moisture, and waxing is not necessary.

Cons of the Cost of Strand Woven Bamboo Flooring

 Compared to laminate or conventional flooring options, it is more costly.  But in my opinion, this was an investment in long-term success.

Sensitive to Changes in Humidity

 Bamboo can expand or contract somewhat, yet it‘s still preferable to solid wood.  Installation with expansion gaps and proper acclimatization are crucial.

It may be slippery.

 Certain finishes, particularly for kids or dogs, can be slippery.  To lessen this problem, I decided to use a matte finish.

The Reasons Behind My Garden‘s Bamboo Decking Choice

 I have barbecues, drink coffee in the morning, and unwind on chilly evenings in my outdoor garden area.  Without sacrificing appearance, I required a decking material that could tolerate harsh sunlight, sand, and the odd downpour.

Describe bamboo decking.

 Indoor flooring and bamboo decking are made in different ways.  Usually, it is thermally treated and strand woven to withstand moisture, UV rays, and biological deterioration.  The denser boards frequently have anti-slip coatings, which are necessary for outdoor safety.

Benefits of Bamboo Decking

 Superior Sturdiness for Outdoor Applications

 Bamboo decking that has been thermally treated is resistant to deterioration, warping, and splintering.  When cared for appropriately, it is even more resilient than many tropical hardwoods.

Modern Performance with a Natural Appearance

 Bamboo decking offers the advantages of engineered materials while simulating the warm, earthy tones of wood.  It melds perfectly with stone or garden vegetation.

A Sustainable Substitute for Hardwood

 Bamboo matures in five years, making it a renewable alternative to hardwoods like teak or ipe that cause a lot of deforestation.

Low Upkeep with Appropriate Coating

 Bamboo decking requires less maintenance than natural wood when coated with UV-protective coatings or oil-based sealants.  To extend mine‘s life, I want to reseal it once a year.

Underfoot, it‘s cooler than composite

 Composite decking can get uncomfortable hot in the Dubai sun.  Bamboo, especially when sheltered, remains comparatively cooler.

Cons of Bamboo Decking: Needs Consistent Upkeep

 Although it doesn‘t require much care, it does require yearly cleaning and re-oiling to keep its color and water resistance.

The starting price

 High-quality bamboo decking costs more than certain softwood or plastic composite alternatives.  But its lifespan makes up for the initial investment.

Needs Skilled Installation

 Outdoor bamboo must be erected with consideration to drainage, ventilation, and spacing.  I engaged experts who have knowledge of outdoor bamboo systems in the United Arab Emirates.

My Last Remarks Following Installation

 After living with both materials for several months, I can safely declare that bamboo was the perfect decision for both places.  My living room‘s strand-woven bamboo flooring is sturdy underfoot, elegant, and hasn‘t worn out yet.  It gives the room coziness and style without requiring ongoing maintenance.

My garden‘s bamboo decking has withstood Dubai‘s intense sunlight and sporadic sandstorms quite well.  Soon after installation, I used a UV-protective oil to help maintain its color and stop surface cracking.

Both materials have demonstrated their worth, not just in terms of their functionality but also in the way they have improved my home‘s ambiance.  Bamboo offers me the technical strength I need as a flooring specialist along with the natural appearance I adore.

Consider more than just beauty when thinking about using bamboo for your own indoor or outdoor areas in the United Arab Emirates.  Examine the technological requirements, comprehend the upkeep requirements, and spend money on expert installation.  Bamboo might be one of the best home décor options you can choose if you do it correctly.
