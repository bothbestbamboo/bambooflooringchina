Solid Bamboo Flooring: The Next Best Thing in the World of Flooring

Posted on 2016-10-25 15:02:32

Homeowners these days often search for new flowing options that are affordable and eco-friendly as well. They also look for a touch of creativity and uniqueness while still remaining within their budget. There are some homeowners who splurge on home building materials, while a thin minority (as yet) chooses to go with cheaper and eco-friendly alternatives, such as solid bamboo flooring. Solid bamboo decking and flooring is indeed an intelligent choice when it comes to building a new home, or renovating an old one.


The amazing thing about bamboo as a flooring material is that many different options are available in terms of style, based on the natural colour and pattern. The demand for this kind of product is steadily growing, though in terms of market share, hardwood floors are still miles ahead. Manufacturers have developed many new style options that suit all tastes and budgets. Bamboo as a material can be highly elegant and also adds a distinct character to your home’s interiors.


Contrary to the popular notion, bamboo is not a tree, but it is the tallest species of the grass family. Also, bamboo does not need a lot of human intervention and innovation to grow. The plant can easily adapt to the environment. Also, bamboo multiplies real fast and can regrow in about three to five years. There are Chinese manufacturers of solid bamboo flooring who source sustainably harvested bamboos from the backyards of China. Therefore, the manufacturing method they follow is completely aligned with the inherent rules of the nature and you can easily get a more eco-friendly home if you use solid bamboo flooring at your home.


Also, bamboo flooring is any day harder and more heavy-duty compared to the wood floorings. It is as durable as oak and has greater ability to resist dents compared to many other hardwoods. This squarely translates into longer work life of the flooring. Solid bamboo flooring can give many years of uninterrupted service without much intervention, care or maintenance from your side. This kind of flooring is in fact meant for high-traffic areas that get many footfalls every day. It can withstand pressure as much as any hardwood you can name.


These days, various colour variants of solid bamboo flooring are available. From natural light golden to carbonized, you can get a whole lot of options while buying this kind of flooring. Installation is remarkably easy, and so is regular care and maintenance. You can find both pre-finished and unfinished bamboo flooring products. Solid compressed bamboo flooring has also high moisture resistance, which makes it an ideal choice for places that get huge rainfall or snowfall through the year. Also, you can install it in your bathroom. If you are on a lookout for the best deals in solid bamboo flooring, look up Google to find the most trusted dealers.


Bamboo is an emerging flooring trend. Bamboo is definitely an affordable alternative to hardwood but the look is almost similar. Natural bamboo has a light yellow colour, but carbonized and stained bamboo comes in a number of shades. It is true that the industry is still in its infancy, but it’s certainly a new take on modern living, which is slowly changing the dimensions of the building industry bit by bit.


Solid bamboo flooring is ideal for those who love living in a country atmosphere but cannot go far from the maddening crowd of the city due to professional or familial obligations. For this people, bamboo flooring is a perfect option as they can get a slice of the unpolluted country life and feel the nature’s touch by having solid bamboo flooring at their home. As far as energy efficiency is concerned, bamboo flooring has no real match as well. It is too date the most environment friendly flooring option which you can choose for your home. It’s just that you need to find the best manufacturer. Remember, there are just too many fly-by-night kind of manufacturers.
