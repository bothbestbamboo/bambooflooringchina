Eco Green Bamboo Flooring: No. 1 Choice for Households

Posted on 2019-5-31 8:51:26

Bamboo flooring has become popular with each passing day, as it is very much similar to classic wood flooring. Bamboo flooring looks exotic and has an inexpensive appearance which makes it one of the most attractive floorings for many people.


Knowing about the benefits and limitations of Bamboo Flooring before installation is important to make sure that your floor is perfectly matching with your lifestyle.

Why you should choose bamboo flooring? 


In short, bamboo flooring is a natural surface coating material which is produced from Bamboo. It is a great option among other traditional wood floors. Let us understand the advantages and disadvantages of this type of flooring, with the hope that you will find this information useful.


Bamboo is a durable product: 


High-quality bamboo flooring will last as long as traditional wood flooring. This situation is present in several sectors where quality is a key feature. Non-carbonized bamboo is considered to be harder and more durable as compared to red oak.


- Bamboo is elegant: 


For those who are attracted by a clean and modern aesthetic, bamboo flooring lends itself perfectly to this style. Although similar to wood, the differences can be subtle.


- Colors and many styles:


One of the advantages of bamboo flooring is that it is available in a variety of styles and colors. Note: It is important to know what the different colors represent because the dark colors usually result from a heat treatment that has softened the bamboo.


- Bamboo is good for the environment:


As bamboo is a fast-growing grass; it reaches full maturity in five to seven years; bamboo is considered by many to be much more durable than traditional wood. Because the use of natural materials is currently at the heart of many people‘s concerns in building a house, bamboo flooring is an ideal choice for many eco-friendly people.


- Bamboo is easy to maintain:


Bamboo flooring requires little maintenance because it can be easily cleaned with mild soap; no special treatment is needed.


- Repairing of Bamboo floor is possible:


As bamboo flooring can dent, scratch or fade over time, it is good to know that they can be repaired if needed. Generally, the installation of bamboo flooring involves many topcoats so that bamboo can protect itself. If necessary, simply sand the surface and then reapply a top coat to give a boost to your floor.


- A bamboo parquet resists water:


In comparison with traditional wood flooring, bamboo comes out early as being more resistant to water damage and stains.


-  Bamboo flooring is not expensive:


Although bamboo is an exotic wood, it can be less expensive than a traditional wood floor.


- Bamboo flooring is subject to scratches:


A poor quality bamboo floor can get scratched easily, it is difficult or impossible to keep a completely smooth bamboo floor. Furniture, animals, and even heeled shoes can damage the surface of the floor over time, even the tiny particles present in everyday life.


- A bamboo floor gets moisture easily:


Relying on the size of your place, bamboo flooring may get worse in very humid or dry environments. For example, if the bamboo flooring is installed in a wet area, the chances are that it may get swell. On the other hand, in dry regions, bamboo flooring can have cracks.


- A bamboo floor can be toxic:


Some bamboo flooring from China may contain high levels of toxic chemicals, such as glues and formaldehyde-based finishes. Make sure the bamboo flooring you want to buy is certified and high quality based. Although bamboo is presented as a very environmentally friendly option, sometimes forest practices can be irresponsible and harmful to the environment.


Indoor and outdoor bamboo flooring: the differences


The natural characteristics of bamboo associated with processing and industrial finishes make it an ideal choice for both indoor and outdoor - outdoor environments. As regards the installation, for interiors, different solutions and installations are offered, as well as wooden parquet. Generally, the various pieces are fitted with a joint to facilitate a quick and easy installation. In addition to the color and type of installation, you can choose to use it too, in the event that your home has installed underfloor heating as it does not suffer any negative effects.


A quick overview of prices


The price of bamboo flooring is much cheaper than wood parquet, although the latter varies according to the types of wood chosen. However, it may vary depending on the company that sells it and based on the treatment and finish chosen. We advise against using it just naturally because it would risk being damaged more easily.
