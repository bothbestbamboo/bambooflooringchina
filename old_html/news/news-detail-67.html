Advantages And Disadvantages of Bamboo Flooring

Posted on 2014-10-27 10:48:53

You can find lots of articles online about advantages of bamboo flooring, however, in my standpoint, it is necessary for you to learn its disadvantages because only you can accept its shortcomings, you can accept bamboo flooring completely.


This is the exact article about comprehensive analysis on bamboo flooring’s merits and demerits, are you interested?


Let us talk about its disadvantages at first


Firstly, the paste is easy to be fell off. You can full understand this shortcoming if you know the whole productive process of bamboo flooring. It is made from bamboo, and at beginning, it is small size bamboo chip, then different bamboo chips should be pasted together to form a large size flooring, during this productive steps, binding material should be used so that it is easy to be fell off if poor quality binding material was used.


Secondly, it is easy to be divided into different layers. In short, bamboo flooring is the combination between flooring and wood, because of uncertain factors such as strong sunlight or humidness, it will be divided into different layers gradually.


Thirdly, the performance of water proof is not good. Compared with solid wood flooring, bamboo flooring has the same shortcomings, once being soaked in water, it will be expanded and even out of shape, what’s worse, oil painting will be bubbled, after a long time, it will go mouldy and finally rotted.


Fourthly, it is not suitable for room at which there is floor heating. Solid wood floor can be paved at room even if there is floor heating, which is important equipment in cold northern area in winter, however, this is not suitable for bamboo flooring because it will be out of shape under too much high temperature environment.


After reading these four shortcomings of bamboo flooring, do you still have interested in it? Of course, please take it easy on these disadvantages because different floors also have their own disadvantages.


Now, it is time to talk about advantages of bamboo flooring.


Firstly, it is too much cheaper than solid wood floor


When buying decoration materials for home decoration, the first thing people have to consider is budget because lots of money should be spent on it. If you want to choose cheaper flooring with great cost performance, bamboo flooring should be your first choice.


Secondly, it is easy to be installed


When installing bamboo flooring at room, just follow simple steps and there is no need painting, in short, both time and work can be saved. Meanwhile, it will not lead to indoor pollution.


Finally, it is beneficial to health


According to research report, risk of sickness such as rheumatoid arthritis, heart disease would be reduced when paving bamboo flooring at home.
