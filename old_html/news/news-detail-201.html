What are the disadvantages of solid wood flooring besides being expensive?

Posted on 2022-5-7 9:18:23

Nowadays, many people prefer solid wood decoration materials, especially the choice of flooring. Most people think that solid wood flooring is the best among all floors, because solid wood flooring is environmentally friendly, and the renovated house is both beautiful and practical. There are many types of floors on the market. When buying floors, you must not blindly follow the trend. Although solid wood floors have many advantages, they may not be suitable for your own use.


Solid wood floors are made of natural materials. Solid wood floors do not use glue and do not emit a lot of formaldehyde. Therefore, solid wood floors are more environmentally friendly than other types of floors sold on the market. The pattern on the surface of the solid wood floor is very natural, and it is very comfortable to step on the solid wood floor. This kind of floor can be described as warm in winter and cool in summer, but the solid wood floor also has some shortcomings. The first is that its cost performance is very low.


The price of solid wood floors is particularly expensive, but wood floors are very easily corroded by some chemical substances during use. If the home is dark and humid, the solid wood floors will be deformed. Maintenance, otherwise the solid wood floor will definitely not be able to be used for a long time. Families living in the south must not choose solid wood floors.


The laminate floor is made of four layers of materials. The advantage of the laminate floor is that it can effectively isolate the moisture on the ground, and the emission of water vapor will not cause too much harm to it, because the laminate floor can prevent moisture. It is anti-expansion and can meet the heating function of geothermal, so its practicability is relatively high. The disadvantage of laminate flooring is that the comfort is relatively poor, and the production materials are not very environmentally friendly, and will emit some substances that are harmful to the human body. When using laminate flooring, although there is no need to maintain the wooden floor, if there is water stains on the floor, it must be wiped immediately, otherwise problems such as expansion will occur after a long time.


Solid wood engineered floor is similar to solid wood floor. This kind of floor has three layers. Its advantage is that the heat preservation is very good, and the effect of installation is also very good. The aesthetics of solid wood engineered floor and solid wood floor are comparable, but solid wood engineered floor The environmental protection is relatively poor, and it is afraid of wear and tear.


The bamboo floor is made of natural bamboo, and there are more than 20 production processes. This kind of floor is used by some large office buildings. maintainance.
