What are the Pros and Cons of Bamboo Flooring?

Posted on 2022-2-24 9:11:46

While planning to make any kind of changes in your house, the most important thing that you need to consider is the flooring that you will choose. The choice of flooring can impact the interiors of a place as it completes the look of interiors. As compared to the past years, there are now a variety of options that you will find in flooring, you can select the one that goes well with the look of your house and your budget as well.


In today time, most people have become considerate about the use of the flooring that does not harm the environment in any manner. You will find people who are minimalistic, thus they ensure that they do not buy anything that can increase their carbon footprints. The online platform gives you the option to explore various designs and styles that are available in flooring.


Bamboo Flooring


Bamboo flooring has been there for a long time now, but only recently, it has become a popular choice for people who wish to add new flooring to their house. While bamboo flooring has gained popularity because of the go green reason, but it is not something that everyone likes. It is necessary for you to understand all the aspects before you invest in this flooring option.


What are the benefits of Bamboo Flooring?


The best part about bamboo as the material is that it is renewable. There are many varieties that you can grow it per foot in a day. With such a great growth rate, it is easy to understand that how renewable bamboo is as compared to other materials such as the hardwood trees that can take almost 120 years to gain maturity.


Many people are not aware of the fact that bamboo is very strong, it is harder than all other materials that are used for flooring purposes. There are some species of it that are as hard as the red oak tree.  Bamboo, as a flooring material is always considered higher than maple.


The excellent part about bamboo is that it has a great resistance to moisture, this is one of the reasons why people prefer to use it. As bamboo is grown more in tropical places, thus it is also resistant to any kind of spills. As compared to the common belief, it is also an excellent alternative for the bathroom and kitchen area.  These are the places where traditional hardwoods can be tough to use.


Once the installation of bamboo flooring is complete, then it is also easy to take care of the material. If you want the bamboo flooring to look new for a long time and you don’t want any kind of dust and dirt on it, then vacuuming and sweeping is all you need to do.


Bamboo is easily available for the purpose of flooring and other purposes as well.  You can find a good bamboo flooring dealer/manufacturer that can offer you the best quality flooring options as per your specific needs and budget.  You can also find many designing and styling options in flooring which can help you choose the best one for your house.


Why Bamboo flooring is not the right option for you?


Just like any product, bamboo also has pros and cons, thus it is essential to discuss the negative aspects that are linked to it: Most of the bamboo that is imported in different countries, it is produced in China. This is where the eco-friendly choice of people goes for a toss as it has to be shipped from a different country. However, the harvesting of bamboo has also started in other countries so that people can easily buy it without spending a lot of money on shipping. 


You have to be careful while choosing a dealer if you wish to use the flooring for a long time. There are many manufacturers/dealers that you can find on the internet, but this does not mean that all of them will offer quality products. 


Many people end up buying cheap quality bamboo, which makes them face trouble in the future. Thus, it is essential to assess the quality and price of the product that you are planning to buy.
