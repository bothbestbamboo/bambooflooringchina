Do you know about green building bamboo board materials?

Posted on 2022-6-23 9:25:19

Green bamboo sheet materials can reduce the environmental load of buildings. Bamboo board can realize sustainable development of resources through bamboo, and bamboo plywood board is a good building material for saving energy and resources. It is designed with bamboo board material. The building can provide a safe, healthy and comfortable living space and is compatible with the natural environment. It can realize the harmonious coexistence and sustainable development of people, buildings and the environment. So, what is the main idea of ??modern green building bamboo sheet material design? The concept of modern green building bamboo board material design mainly includes the following aspects:


1. Bamboo board materials can save resources: the global forest resources are tight, the development and utilization of bamboo can effectively control deforestation, and replace wood with wood with the same cost performance. Bamboo has a fast growth cycle, early material production, high yield and wide application range. Generally speaking, after 5-10 years of bamboo afforestation, it can be cut down and used every year to save resources.


Secondly, the design of green building bamboo board materials can make people feel back to nature: the natural advantages of bamboo and the excellent performance after molding bring a green and fresh wind to the building materials market. Bamboo board has the natural texture of bamboo, fresh and elegant, giving people a feeling of returning to nature, elegance and elegance.


Bamboo boards can be used as architectural decorations to make indoor air fresh, with proper temperature and humidity, so that occupants feel good and healthy in mind and body. The development and utilization of bamboo resources have received more and more attention. As countries around the world increase the development and utilization of bamboo resources, the importance of bamboo boards in environmental protection, rural poverty alleviation and international trade has become increasingly prominent.


Who said that bamboo furniture panels cannot be used in the north?


Many people think that the bamboo furniture board market is in the south, and the northerners will not use bamboo furniture boards. The main reason is that bamboo furniture boards will crack and deform in the north, which is not suitable for the cold and dry climate in the north. In fact, these are all one-sided understandings. Bamboo furniture panels can also be used well in the north, and even some bamboo furniture can be used in the north. Longer service life.


Why does this prejudice arise? In fact, it has something to do with the environment in which the consumers themselves grew up. The south is rich in bamboo, so bamboo furniture boards are very common in the south, and everyone‘s adoption of bamboo furniture boards is also very high. This is not the case for consumers in the north. Bamboo is not produced in the north. They have basically come into contact with bamboo furniture. The common one is bamboo flooring. However, the quality of bamboo flooring produced by some small manufacturers of bamboo flooring is not good enough or the installation of the flooring is not in place. , and the floor is a three-point place, and the seven-point installation is a very critical part in installation. However, small bamboo furniture board manufacturers tend to ignore this part, which easily leads to deformation and cracking of bamboo furniture boards, so they have a prejudice against deformation and cracking of bamboo furniture boards.


But these are not the fact that the bamboo furniture board itself is not suitable for use in the north, but some human factors lead to the unsatisfactory situation of the bamboo furniture board, but now the large and regular manufacturers are very strict in the monitoring of bamboo products. The dry and humid climate dehydrates the bamboo strictly to ensure the quality of the bamboo furniture board. In particular, regular large-scale manufacturers have stricter control in this regard.
