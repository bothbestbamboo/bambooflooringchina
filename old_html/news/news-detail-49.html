Want to Buy Bamboo Floors?

Posted on 2014-9-24 14:23:26

When there are so many options and varieties of flooring are available in market then why increasing number of consumers are interested to install bamboo floor. Well, due to the increased durability, cost effectiveness, toughness, hardness and water resistant quality undoubtedly bamboo is one of the most pursued flooring materials these days.


Bamboo Floors Offer Style Strength and Comfort


Being environmentally responsible more and more homeowners are inclined towards using bamboo as first choice for flooring material. Due to the rising awareness of preserving environment and natural habitats bamboo is considered as the safest and environment conserving material that can be used for flooring rather than hardwood. Bamboo not only is considered as safe and environment friendly but also is also stronger, durable and cost effective compared to other flooring options in hardwood and laminates. Furthermore, the style, elegance and aesthetically pleasing look it provides to the entire home add beauty and glamour to the entire interior decorum of homes thereby increasing its value.


Tips for Choosing Bamboo Flooring


First thing to consider before buying bamboo flooring is color. You have to be specific and should possess clear and concise idea of what type of color you need in bamboo that sill justify and suit the existing interior theme of your home. Usually there are two major options in bamboo flooring when it comes to color, natural and carbonized. If you prefer natural color then it will be little pale having natural bamboo color but if you want darker shade of color then you should settle for carbonized bamboo boards.


Additionally if you want bamboo color representing rainbow color then asking bamboo floor dealer about stained bamboo flooring is better option. Apart from the major natural and carbonized bamboo colors stained bamboo floors provides you flexibility and convenience to choose almost any color you desire.


Second thing is style, although there are different patterns and styles in bamboo floors major types are vertical and horizontal grains. These types have acquired their name due to the gluing process in which the bamboo strips are attached either in vertical or horizontal fashion. Each type has it distinctive aura and is potential enough to bring in the required warmth, beauty and elegance to your home floorings.


Recently a new pattern called engineered flooring has become increasingly popular in bamboo floors. When you have rooms with sufficient amount of moisture, then using engineered bamboo floors is the best option. Due to unmatched water resistant this type of flooring will prevent cupping, wrapping and warping of floor compared to its other counterparts.
