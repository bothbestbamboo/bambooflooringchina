Pergo Flooring Reviews: A Comprehensive Look at Quality and Variety

Posted on 2025-1-3 7:31:23

Pergo is a brand that frequently sticks out when it comes to flooring selection. With a broad selection of flooring alternatives to suit a variety of needs and tastes, Pergo has been a reliable brand for decades and is renowned for its quality and creativity. Pergo has established a position for itself in the flooring market by offering hardwood, bamboo, and engineered flooring. This article gives you a thorough grasp of what makes Pergo a preferred alternative for both businesses and households by delving into the brand‘s history, product line, and some of its most well-liked choices.

The History of Pergo: A Flooring Pioneer


In 1977, Pergo started its adventure by introducing laminate flooring to the world in Sweden. The ground-breaking design swiftly gained popularity in both Europe and the US after receiving accolades for its robustness, simplicity of installation, and visual appeal. Pergo‘s inventiveness extended beyond laminate; the company now offers luxury vinyl flooring, hardwood, bamboo, and engineered wood. Pergo now works as a division of Mohawk Industries, one of the biggest flooring producers in the world, guaranteeing that its goods satisfy the strictest requirements for sustainability and quality.

A Wide Variety of Flooring Choices

 The range of products offered by Pergo is as diverse as its clientele. Every kind of flooring is made to satisfy particular needs, whether you‘re searching for something opulent, environmentally friendly, or incredibly durable. The most popular flooring categories offered by Pergo are broken down as follows:

Bamboo Floors

 Bamboo flooring from Pergo is a great option for anyone who appreciate natural beauty and sustainability. Bamboo is an environmentally friendly choice because it is a resource that is rapidly regenerated. Bamboo floors from Pergo are made to last thanks to its resilient surface, which can withstand dents and scratches. Additionally, they are coated to resist moisture, which makes them appropriate for high-traffic areas like kitchens.

Pergo bamboo flooring, which comes in a range of textures and finishes, strikes the ideal mix between contemporary design and environmental responsibility. Bamboo‘s organic grain patterns give it a classic appearance, and its resilience guarantees that it can withstand normal wear and tear.

Hardwood floors

 Hardwood flooring from Pergo is an excellent option for people who adore the timeless appeal of wood. This flooring, which is made of real wood, is sophisticated and cosy. From oak and maple to hickory and walnut, Pergo provides a large selection of hardwood species with finishes ranging from matte to high gloss.

Pergo hardwood is unique because of its sophisticated surface treatments. These treatments add to the wood‘s inherent beauty while offering a barrier that keeps stains, scratches, and fading at bay. You may get the best of both worlds with Pergo hardwood: classic style and contemporary toughness.

Wood Flooring Engineered

 For people who like the appearance of hardwood but require something more environmentally friendly, engineered wood is a wise choice. Pergo‘s engineered wood floors are built with a solid, layered core and a true wood veneer. In addition to making the flooring more stable, this design enables its installation in spaces like basements or rooms with varying humidity levels where solid hardwood might not be appropriate.

There is a design for every taste with Pergo‘s engineered wood flooring, which is available in a range of finishes and designs from rustic to modern. Additionally, Pergo‘s unique click-lock mechanism, which does not require glue or nails, makes it simple to install the floors.

Flooring Laminate

 Even though bamboo, hardwood, and engineered wood are the main topics of this blog, it‘s important to note that Pergo is most well-known for their laminate flooring. Because of its cost, robustness, and adaptability in terms of design, this category continues to be among its most popular. Pergo laminate floors offer countless design options at a fraction of the price of hardwood, stone, or tile.

Top-Selling Pergo Items

 Some of the most notable products in Pergo‘s catalogue have grown to be favourites among consumers. Here are some of the most well-liked products:

Pergo Outlast+ Waterproof Laminate Flooring: This laminate alternative is noteworthy for its remarkable waterproof qualities and genuine wood-look designs, even though it does not fall within the bamboo or hardwood classifications. It‘s a popular option for busy homes, bathrooms, and kitchens.

 Premier Hardwood Pergo Max: Solid hardwood planks with improved resilience to handle daily adversities are included in this collection in a range of species and finishes.

 Pergo Portfolio + Engineered Wood with WetProtect: This engineered wood series, which is well-known for its water-resistant technology, blends style and functionality, making it perfect for high-moisture environments.

 Bamboo Flooring with Pergo Elements: This bamboo flooring is a favourite among environmentally aware homeowners because it offers the ideal balance of form and function and is fashionable, long-lasting, and low maintenance.

 The Reasons Pergo Is Unique

 Pergo flooring is still a popular option for a number of reasons. First, the company constantly places a high value on innovation, implementing cutting-edge technology like ScratchGuard and WetProtect to improve the functionality of its flooring. Second, Pergo uses eco-friendly production techniques and materials that are sourced sustainably as part of its commitment to sustainability. Third, the brand has a large selection of styles, so whether you want sleek modern aesthetics or a rustic farmhouse style, there is something for everyone.

The ease of use of Pergo‘s installation systems is another important component. A lot of its flooring include click-lock systems that make do-it-yourself installation simple. Because of this, Pergo is a desirable choice for people looking to save the expense of hiring a professional installer.

A Reliable Brand in Flooring

 Decades of invention and fine craftsmanship have shaped Pergo‘s renown. Pergo has flooring options that blend style, toughness, and functionality, whether you‘re drawn to the eco-friendliness of bamboo, the classic elegance of hardwood, or the adaptability of engineered wood. Pergo remains a pioneer in the flooring sector, trusted by both experts and homeowners, by adhering to its heritage while embracing new technologies.

Pergo is a company worth taking into consideration when looking at flooring options because of its wide selection and solid reputation. Pergo flooring is more than just a surface to walk on thanks to its dedication to quality and design; it‘s an investment in long-lasting style and functionality.
