Bamboo Veneer: Everything to Know Before Going For It

Posted on 2016-11-27 8:23:08

Not many are aware that China is the leading nation in the world that owns a variety of bamboos and also the area covered by the several species (approximately 40 genera and more than 400 types).  With the passage of time, the bamboo veneer has become a popular one in the market, given its eco-friendly characteristic. Besides, it is also the right choice when it comes to making of modern day designs in construction projects like hotels, resorts, museums as well as vehicles.


If you see it from the Indian perspective, bamboo is termed as an ‘underused grass’. With environmental benefits galore, the plant is slowly making its way to a number of uses, primarily for its availability. Today, bamboo is utilized more than timber or steel because it is a ‘fast-growing, high-yielding and sustainable natural resource’.


Why bamboo?


So if you wish to pay attention to aesthetics besides focusing on a unique way of flowing, choose bamboo for its versatility. There are endless ways of bamboo veneering and you will be muddled while learning it all. Nevertheless it’s different than most others and also a tad complicated than the regular wood veneer. After the bamboo veneer process gets over, the furnishings made are sent to the market, where you get it across a wide range of stores. Not to forget, the online market is equally competitive in this regard too.


The many benefits of bamboo veneers


Since a number of architects and interior designers have had to face questions about the environment-friendliness of their projects, bamboo veneer naturally becomes their first choice. If you are bent to do the same, consider pondering upon the advantages of going ‘all bamboo’! Here’s a list of reasons why bamboo veneer gives you an edge over several of its counterparts:


- Eco friendly


There’s no doubt that bamboo veneer is one of the topmost picks for its renewable nature. Most bamboo in its grass form grows no less than a foot and half every day. So, it would be worthwhile choosing bamboo for a dose of nature in the interiors!


- Grain pattern


There are dual grain patterns in bamboo varieties- vertical and planked. The vertical grains are usually thinner and tighter of ?” grain. On the other hand, the planked form is a thicker ?” grain, which resembles butcher blocks. The best part about both these rains is that they can come in either of the two colors, which makes a total of four different varieties in grains.


- Varieties in colors


Ideally, bamboo has its natural color and that’s more of a yellowish or bronze shade. There’s however, a carbonized color that looks like the buckskin and is of a tan shade. Whether you choose the latter or former, the colors are equally good and impart a lovely look when used.


- Exotic pattern


Despite its popularity throughout Asia, particularly in China and also India; the rest of the world (non-polar regions) also support the growth of bamboo. So, when you use a product that has originated from far off lands, you generally get allured, much like any other customer. But the place of origin doesn’t render much of a difference to the quality of the item, provided the manufacturing, processing and production were good.


- Cost effectiveness


Not that bamboo is extremely low-priced, but middle class group of people can easily afford. Also, the medium ranged bamboo, in terms of price, provides high quality, stylish and eco-friendly items. So, you know that it’s true value for money.
Compared to many other solid materials, operating with bamboo veneers means there are less hassles and the yields are far better than solid wood, especially if you compare the cost per square foot.


Factors to weigh beforehand


One word of caution however comes here- don’t get misled by seeing others resorting to floorings made of bamboo. Your choice matters more than anything and you should be wary of certain considerations like:


- The type of flowing used


- Hardness of the flooring


- Eco-friendliness of the manufacturing process


- If the flooring type can be rightly used for your need


- Availability of matching accessories


- Warranty period


Only after weighing the pros and cons of each, you should make your choice. Going by the latest trends, the use of engineered bamboo items has certainly increased. So invest only after thorough research.
