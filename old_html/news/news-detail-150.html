Stranded Woven Bamboo Flooring- why go for it?

Posted on 2017-11-12 8:38:28

Strand woven bamboo flooring is one of the most popular options when it comes to flooring on the market right now. It is a specialized kind of bamboo floor that has certain benefits over the regular ones. What differentiates this type of bamboo flooring from other ones is its hardness that can be further increased via the lamination process. While the bamboo floors are quite eco friendly and popular, as the process used for hardening used takes away the most significant and troublesome characteristics, as per the installers. 


Hardness


Hardness is a big factor and concern when it comes to bamboo flooring. While it is that bamboo flooring is durable and affordable when compared to other products, it is incapable of taking up the abuses that hardwood flooring is famous to endure. In order to make it more suitable covering option for your floor, stranded woven bamboo floors are further altered using glue to help bonding different strips together once they have been boiled in order to eliminate any sugar residues, as sugar is known to attract termites that can create problem in future. 


Carbonized Bamboo


Amongst the most popular products is the carbonized bamboo! This is the bamboo is put up through the boiling process that makes it darker as compared to the natural bamboo flooring. However, the boiling process tends to make the wood even softer. The stranded woven bamboo flooring available in the market offers alternative that provide it with rich color associated with the carbonized bamboo. It creates much harder planks with better characteristics. As compared to the hardwood floors, bamboo is likely to match it in hardness. In terms of care, with bamboo products in the form of stranded woven products, you can get study durable floors that provide years of longevity. 


The Universal Benefits


Strand woven bamboo also offers some exceptional benefits as compared to natural bamboo flooring. It is easier to keep this flooring into good shape. It can easily withstand heels, hard sole shoes and other form of traffic and abuses on visits throughout the day. When the planks become dirtier, you can clean it up easily with the help of a damp cloth or you can also use elbow grease. There is no need of using harsh cleansers on stranded woven bamboo as you need with the hardwood floors. 


You must be able to get warranty period on your bamboo woven flooring. You must check to see how long your manufacturers are ready to backup the product and provide protection against. You must keep in mind right from the start that the bamboo flooring is never the right choice for areas when people enter the house or other section where in it tends to face traffic with people wearing hard soles and boots. 


However, in a living room, you can try installing stranded woven bamboo floors or other commercialized flooring options. Strand woven bamboo flooring can sometimes be laid right over the top of your existing floor, so be sure to ask if this is an option for your home.


Strand woven bamboo flooring has very unique design and the density is much higher than normal floor, it is 1, 250 kg/m3, so it is really strong and it is not so easy to bend or cramp.


1. Color symmetry because the bamboo growth year is much shorter than the trees, so the sun is not seriously affected that no significant difference between the sun and the shadow side, so the bamboo floors are rich in bamboo grain and color symmetry;


2. Another brilliant benefit of bamboo flooring is the high surface hardness. For ex. the bamboo plant belongs to the lawn with plant fiber structure is its hardness is, of course, more than double the wood, no deformation.


3. Lifetime, Theoretically bamboo flooring life of up to 20 years. Compared, genuine wood, bamboo flooring has smaller contraction and extension, and keep their stability.


4. Keep warm, due to its large specific heat and its high density, so bamboo flooring in winter heat cannot loss, therefore, bamboo flooring has function of keeping warm.
