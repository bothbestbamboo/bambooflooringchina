What is spc floor? What are the advantages of Spc floor?

Posted on 2022-5-26 8:08:57

With the advent of new materials, floor decoration is no longer tile. In the world of wooden floors, more and more people try new products, but they also get their unanimous praise. What flooring materials are worth buying? SPC floor is environmentally friendly and wear-resistant, and has the texture of wooden floor, which is equivalent to the ceiling of the floor industry. What are the benefits of SPC flooring? let me tell you! What is SPC Flooring? SPC floor is composed of calcium powder, resin powder and polyvinyl chloride, and then formed under high temperature and high pressure. It does not need to be glued and does not generate formaldehyde, which is especially environmentally friendly. Among them, polyvinyl chloride plastic is a plastic product with a very wear-resistant surface, good elasticity, long service life, and no special maintenance in the later stage. The wooden floor we are talking about is made of MDF and glue, so the formaldehyde content is high, and the paving is not environmentally friendly.


The advantages of SPC flooring.


Wood floors are very different from SPC floors, precisely because of the difference in the structure of the latter. There are five layers of SPC floor, including UV coating to improve the surface‘s stain resistance; wear layer to make the floor durable; there is also color film layer, imitating the texture of wood floor; SPC substrate layer and quiet layer ensure the floor‘s durability. Environmental friendly. It is precisely because of these five structures that SPC floors have greater advantages than wooden floors and are more suitable for current home decoration.


1. Environmental protection and no pollution. The materials used in the SPC floor itself are non-polluting, and the polyvinyl chloride is degradable, so the production process is very environmentally friendly. There is no need to use glue even in the paving process, and it is installed directly through the lock of the SPC substrate layer. The process is simple and no formaldehyde is released.


2. Outstanding wear resistance. SPC floor has a special wear layer to avoid black marks when furniture is moved, suitable for dining room, living room and bedroom. Generally speaking, SPC floors are not afraid of scratches from sharp objects and suppression of heavy objects, and their appearance is still online.


3. Easy to clean, no maintenance required. There are no pores on the surface of the UV coating, which determines the good waterproof performance of the SPC floor. Even if the floor is mopped with a wet mop, there will be no water seepage, which is an incomparable advantage of wooden floors. We don‘t need to spend a fortune for maintenance in the future, as long as it is installed on the ground, there will be no problem in seven or eight years, especially suitable for lazy people like us.


4. High cost performance. When we buy wooden floor, the average market price is 200~400 yuan/square meter, and the price of SPC floor is relatively low, so the cost performance is very high. Wooden floors and SPC floors have the same appearance and texture. In order to save money, be sure to buy SPC floors!

How to maintain pvc floor


If you install PVC flooring at home, it is not only beautiful and stylish, but also very comfortable to the touch of your feet. Therefore, it is very popular. However, in order to prolong the service life of PVC floor, how should we maintain it in our daily life?


1. Cleaning: In areas with limited operations, you can choose to use a dry drag or vacuum to remove dust and grime from the floor. The PVC floor can also be cleaned by dragging it slightly wet. When cleaning, it should be noted that the floor should not have water accumulation.


2. To remove stains: Spray the diluted neutral cleaner on the floor, then brush the stain with a nylon cleaning pad, and finally wipe with clean water.


3. It is forbidden to soak the floor in open water, otherwise it will seriously affect the service life of the floor. Use a water suction machine to suck up the sewage in time.
