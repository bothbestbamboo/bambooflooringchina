Your Ultimate Guide to Understand Bamboo Flooring

Posted on 2020-12-1 8:30:35

Some people believe in upgrading their house every year and some occasionally. Whether you are from the first category or the second one, bamboo flooring can surely be your first choice after you understand it well. Durability, good appearance, low-cost price and eco-friendliness are some of the factors that make it first in the list of hardwoods. Bamboo flooring syndicate the pliability of hardwood flooring with the easy upkeep of laminate.


Like other sorts of flooring available in the market, bamboo flooring has a countless range of benefits. If you are thinking that it would be hard for you to keep the floor clean then do not worry. Bamboo flooring is easy to restore and maintain. In the available options of bamboo wood, you can easily find chemical-free products that help you to maintain the air clean and easy to breathe. In this guide, we will tell some of the benefits of having bamboo flooring in your house. Keep reading to discover some of the advantages of bamboo flooring currently available in the market. This guide will also help you to understand how bamboo floors help you to enhance the value of your house. 


The guide to understanding the bamboo flooring 


Bamboo is not a wood but a type of grass, the stalks and shoots of which are processed to create a floor. It takes only five years to regrow a bamboo whereas trees take more than 50 years to grow into an adult. These grasses can easily grow with any requirement of extra take care. In fact, the procedure of making bamboo flooring from bamboo takes place without terminating the plant itself. After undergoing a certain procedure that includes pressurizing, fusing and boiling bamboo material are formed into planks that look like wood.


An overview of bamboo flooring


If we are talking about imperviousness of Bamboo flooring than it is more water-resistant as compare to the other types of hardwood flooring that makes it an affordable and good option for areas in the home that may suffer through the moisture of light. This is more applicable in the case where the bamboo floor is covered over proper underlayment or sub-flooring. 


There are basically three types of bamboo flooring i.e., Strand woven, horizontal and vertical 


1. Strand woven bamboo flooring 


This type of bamboo flooring is created from the fibres of shredded bamboo that are bonded under penetrating pressure and heat to offer the sturdiest sort of bamboo floor available in the market. 


2. Horizontal bamboo flooring 


This type of floors exploits substitutive layers of the plant to highpoint the growth joints of the bamboo. This sort of procedure dispensation for a more organic impression. 


3. Vertical bamboo flooring 


This type of floors is manufactured by the fusion of stubbles in a vertical style that produces consistent, perpendicular lines that harvest a contemporary feel and look. 


All of these types of bamboo flooring varieties available in the market can be manufactured to resemble numerous sorts of hardwood flooring to fit the appealing you have in mind for the space of your house. 


Pros of using Bamboo flooring 


1. It is Eco-friendly 


This sort of flooring is made from the natural vegetation which is known as “Bamboo Plant”. This is a renewable resource that has growth factor quicker than other types of trees used in manufacturing for hardwood flooring. The bamboo plant attains its maturity in just five years whereas another type of trees takes more than 50 years to grow as an adult. Some other type of trees like oak and maple might take ten to twenty years to grow fully-still more than the bamboo plant. 


2. It is easy to maintain 


Bamboo flooring is easy to clean, maintain and restore-if you are buying a quality one. You can clean them with regular vacuuming and sweeping and intermittent damp swabbing. One of the best qualities of bamboo flooring is that it collects no dust particle just like usual floors do. 


3. Durable 


Certain types of bamboo flooring are durable as some hardwood floors, for example, red oak. Durable forms of bamboo flooring are manufactured from appropriately handled un-carbonized bamboo. You can easily find such type of bamboo flooring in the market.
