BuildDirect Flooring Reviews: A Comprehensive Look at Quality and Style

Posted on 2024-12-28 7:52:19

Your home‘s overall style and feel can be defined by the flooring you choose. BuildDirect has distinguished itself from the other businesses in the market by providing a wide selection of flooring materials at affordable costs. BuildDirect offers a range of alternatives to accommodate different tastes, budgets, and styles, from bamboo to oak, engineered to laminate. To help you choose your next floor, this blog examines BuildDirect‘s flooring selection in further detail, covering its background, best-selling options, and top product categories.

Presenting BuildDirect: An Online Flooring Solutions Pioneer


With its direct-to-consumer business strategy, BuildDirect, a North American corporation, is credited with transforming the home improvement sector. BuildDirect was established in 1999 with the goal of streamlining the purchasing process by cutting out intermediaries, enabling customers to obtain premium flooring and other home renovation supplies at discounted costs.

With comprehensive product descriptions, ratings, and tools to assist clients in visualising their projects, the company‘s web platform provides a smooth buying experience. BuildDirect is a preferred brand among designers, builders, and homeowners because to its dedication to quality, variety, and affordability.

Product Types: A Wide Variety to Fit Any Style

 BuildDirect offers a large selection of flooring to suit a variety of preferences and needs. The primary flooring categories offered by the brand are:

1. Flooring made of bamboo
Bamboo flooring has become more and more popular because of its distinctive look and sustainability. Bamboo alternatives from BuildDirect include strand-woven bamboo, which is renowned for its remarkable resilience. Bamboo flooring from BuildDirect is ideal for anyone looking for an eco-friendly yet fashionable option. It comes in natural tones and darker treatments.

2. Flooring made of hardwood
A wide variety of solid hardwoods are available at BuildDirect, and hardwood flooring is a timeless classic. Because they are made from species like oak, maple, hickory, and acacia, these flooring are guaranteed to last and have an opulent finish. In order to fit their interior design idea, customers can select from a variety of board sizes, stains, and finishes.

3. Engineered Hardwood Flooring
Engineered hardwood offers improved stability along with the beauty of natural wood. The engineered hardwood flooring from BuildDirect have a premium plywood core with a top layer of real wood. They are perfect for basements or other humid regions because of their construction, which makes them resistant to moisture and temperature changes.

4. Flooring Laminate
With more longevity and the same visual appeal as hardwood, laminate flooring is a more affordable option. BuildDirect offers a variety of aesthetic choices with their laminate floors, which are made to resemble real wood, stone, or tile. They are a popular option for hectic homes because of their easy installation techniques and scratch-resistant surfaces.

Top Products: Items That Consumers Adore

 Some flooring solutions have gained popularity among consumers because of their value, quality, and design. These are a few of BuildDirect‘s best-selling items:

1. Flooring made of Jasper hardwood
One of BuildDirect‘s main hardwood brands is Jasper. Jasper flooring, which is renowned for its exquisite craftsmanship, comes in a range of wood species, finishes, and plank widths. It is a popular option for people looking for traditional elegance because of its rich textures and durable performance, which are valued by customers.

2. Bamboo Flooring in Mazama
Mazama bamboo flooring is known for its remarkable strength and environmentally friendly design. Particularly strong and resilient to deterioration, the strand-woven bamboo choices are appropriate for high-traffic spaces.

3. Laminate flooring made of laminate
Lamton laminate flooring is a dependable choice for anyone on a tight budget. It is made to resemble stone and hardwood while being impervious to stains and scratches. It is a favourite among do-it-yourselfers due to its low cost and simple installation.

4. Vinyl Plank Flooring by Vesdura
Vesdura vinyl plank flooring is noteworthy even though it was not included in the main categories that were previously covered. It is a flexible choice for basements, baths, and kitchens because it combines the appearance of hardwood with the water resilience of vinyl.

What Is Unique About BuildDirect?

 BuildDirect stands out from other flooring brands for a number of reasons:

Direct-to-Consumer Model: BuildDirect provides premium goods at reduced costs by eschewing conventional retail channels.

 Extensive Selection: BuildDirect offers a range of price points and styles, from high-end hardwoods to affordable laminates.

 Customer Tools: To assist clients in making well-informed decisions, the company‘s website offers resources such as the BuildDirect Flooring Calculator and complimentary samples.

 Sustainability: To appeal to clients who care about the environment, BuildDirect gives priority to eco-friendly solutions like bamboo flooring.
Customer Service: The company‘s customer service representatives are renowned for their expertise and promptness, which guarantees a seamless shopping experience.

 An Overview of BuildDirect‘s History

 The goal of BuildDirect‘s founding was to make home renovation easier. By adhering to its basic principles of quality, affordability, and innovation, the company has developed into a reputable name over time. Finding the ideal flooring without visiting a showroom has become simpler for professionals and homeowners because to its emphasis on developing an easily navigable online platform.

The company‘s commitment to sustainability is another indication of its progressive mindset. BuildDirect encourages eco-friendly options without sacrificing design or functionality by providing items like bamboo and engineered hardwood.

Whether you prefer the classic charm of hardwood, the eco-friendliness of bamboo, or the usefulness of laminate, BuildDirect‘s flooring selection has something to offer everyone. In the flooring sector, BuildDirect is still a reputable brand thanks to its wide range of products, customer-focused philosophy, and dedication to cost. Examining BuildDirect‘s selection of flooring options could help you choose the ideal option for your area if you‘re thinking of upgrading.
