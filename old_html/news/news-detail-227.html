What are the sizes, dimensions and advantages of bamboo panels

Posted on 2022-6-26 7:53:55

Bamboo plywood is a series of complex processed and exquisitely pressed bamboo pieces. In the production process of bamboo plywood, advanced anti-drying technology is adopted, so that the moisture content of bamboo plywood is controlled at about 8%-14%. High pressure adhesive can effectively suppress high temperature. Therefore, the bamboo plywood will not be surrounded by temperature for a long time to cause cracking and deformation.


Advantages of bamboo sheet:


1. Bamboo plywood adopts the method of splicing bamboo strips, which is made of composite bonding at high temperature, and has the characteristics of strong stability, not easy to deform, and no glue.


2. In addition, because the bamboo plywood has taken off the sugar, starch and other substances in the bamboo, it also has the characteristics of strong anti-insect and durable. This bamboo plywood is very wear-resistant and mildew-proof.


3. Bamboo plywood Bamboo is mainly taken from nature, replacing the original wood, and has less negative impact on the human body.


4. The surface of the bamboo plywood is smooth, soft, supple and natural. It is a high-grade material with stable quality, and it is easy to match the color of the home.


Disadvantages of bamboo sheet:


1. In terms of stability, the shrinkage and expansion of bamboo panels are smaller than other panels, and are easily affected by seasonal effects, such as wet weather or hot weather in the south, bamboo panels may be slightly deformed. In addition, bamboo plywoods are also prone to scratches, so generally avoid scratching with sharp objects.


2. The maintenance of bamboo plywoods is time-consuming and laborious. Regular waxing is required to prolong the service life, which brings inconvenience to personal friends.


What are the specifications of bamboo sheets?


In addition to our traditional specifications of 2550*1250, bamboo plywoods can be customized in the following sizes, lengths and sizes: 2000*1000, 2000*500, 2000*600, 2200*600, etc., and the conventional thicknesses are 3mm, 4mm, 5mm , 6mm, 7mm, 8mm, 9mm, 10mm, 12mm, 14mm, 15mm, 16mm, 19mm, 20mm, 25mm, 30mm, etc.


The development of bamboo plywood materials is relatively extensive. Bamboo plywoods are widely used in furniture, decoration, bamboo and wood crafts, toys, sports equipment, cabinets, ceilings and other industries.
