Bamboo Flooring - Why west is embracing this natural flooring option?

Posted on 2017-8-31 17:12:05

Parquet is an excellent choice for floor renovation in your home. Quite a new material for bamboo flooring is bamboo, and it is becoming more and more popular these days. Bamboo flooring is a beautiful, durable and environmentally friendly floor covering, and also has an acceptable price.


Tooth Extraction Procedure


You decided that there will be no tiles, linoleum, laminate or carpet on the floor of your house, but a real wooden floor. Then you need to make the right choice among the wooden floor coverings. With this, there is no need to hurry; you need to compare all the pros and cons of different materials with the beginning.


In recent years, people prefer bamboo parquet floors, rather than classical ones. Since bamboo has many advantages in appearance and operation.


There are five main reasons why you can choose your bamboo floor.


Strength


Bamboo flooring is best suited for places where there is a lot of stress. Do not scratch if there are children or animals. Also, due to its strength, falling objects and places with frequent attendance (living room or hallway) will not harm the surface of the parquet.


Heat


Because of its natural origin, bamboo flooring keeps warmth inside your home. Most hard materials for floors have a beautiful appearance, but some try to give the unusual appearance to the emergence of the floor. Here we must not forget that sex should bring a sense of comfort. Bamboo flooring will create a combination of warmth, comfort and a beautiful appearance.


Naturalness


Bamboo flooring is environmentally friendly material because it is made from grass, which quickly grows after harvesting. For the production of bamboo, parquet is not used chemical additives.


Color


Parquet from bamboo comes in two shades. The first so-called natural light, since it fits almost any interior, its sales are skyrocketing. The second one is amber. This excellent shade is obtained by the interaction of carbon and starch, which is contained in bamboo. Such a bamboo is called "charred."


Appearance


Bamboo flooring is one of the newest floor coverings that are on the market. But it is already in great demand among buyers. Bamboo flooring will certainly embellish the look of your house or office.


Parquet from bamboo is formed in such a way that it turns out very easy. This means that the installation of such flooring in the house will be a relatively simple process. Because the bamboo fiber is compressed during production, the parquet is durable and flexible. This parquet is resistant to moisture and scratches.


Different colors and texture of bamboo flooring are achieved due to varying degrees of carbonation. Carbonized bamboo flooring, in fact, is subjected to heat treatment to darken its surface and give particular strength. The longer it heats up, the darker the color will turn out and the stronger it will be. Since, in fact, bamboo is a grass, not a tree, and then when choosing bamboo floors, it is important to pay attention to the quality of the producer.



Why is bamboo flooring unique?


Bamboo flooring is unique: the parquet provides excellent sound, noise and heat insulation, regulates the microclimate of the room and successfully resists moderate mechanical influences. Parquet from a bamboo, as well as a massive bamboo board possess high durability and provide ease of stacking.


Durable and reliable bamboo flooring is made of three-layer, glued under high pressure. Parquet is covered with five layers of wear-resistant varnish. Massive parquet from bamboo has a length of 960 mm, a width of 96 mm and a thickness of 15 mm. Parquet board made of bamboo is a unique wear-resistant material. Bamboo is one of the heaviest wood materials, it is heavier than oak by 30 percent and 3 times denser than conventional wood covers. This gives the floor coverings of bamboo natural resistance to moisture, mold and exposure to insects.


Bamboo flooring does not shrink and does not swell like the rest of hard wood flooring. Bamboo floor - the choice of a person who is anxiously related to his own home.


Parquet from a bamboo is applied for creation of unique design interiors with notes of a natural beauty. Eco-friendly bamboo flooring helps to beat stylish and non-standard interiors.


The main binding of designers to the bamboo floor is a natural decorative pattern, characteristic of bamboo, which has a parquet board made of bamboo. Bamboo floors are the epitome of fashion for environmentally friendly natural materials.
