Wellmade Bamboo Flooring: Reviews, Product Highlights, and Brand Overview

Posted on 2025-8-22 8:54:00

For homeowners, contractors, and designers seeking a sustainable and durable flooring option, bamboo continues to gain traction as a stylish and eco-conscious alternative to hardwood. Among the key players in the bamboo flooring industry, Wellmade Performance Flooring stands out for its innovation, product range, and consistent reputation. With over two decades of experience, Wellmade has built a brand that blends performance with environmental responsibility.

This article explores Wellmade‘s history, product types, best-selling bamboo floors, and what customers are saying about their experience with the brand.

A Closer Look at Wellmade Flooring


Wellmade Performance Flooring is based in Wilsonville, Oregon, with manufacturing operations in China. The company was founded in the early 2000s and has since grown into a recognized leader in engineered and strand-woven bamboo flooring. It also produces luxury vinyl plank (LVP), rigid core SPC flooring, and other engineered hardwood products.

What sets Wellmade apart is its commitment to combining technology and sustainability. Their products are FloorScore? certified for indoor air quality, and they manufacture bamboo flooring using low-VOC adhesives, often meeting or exceeding CARB Phase 2 standards.

Their patented HDPC? (High-Density Plastic Composite) core technology is another standout innovation, providing enhanced water resistance and dimensional stability — a feature especially attractive to homeowners in moisture-prone regions.

Types of Bamboo Flooring Offered by Wellmade

 Wellmade offers a variety of bamboo flooring options to meet different design, installation, and performance needs. Their bamboo lines are typically available through major home improvement retailers like Home Depot and Lowe’s, as well as through wholesale and private-label partnerships.

1. Strand Woven Bamboo Flooring

 Construction: Made by compressing shredded bamboo fibers under high heat and pressure, resulting in an extremely hard surface.

 Durability: Among the hardest flooring products on the market — often rated over 3,000 on the Janka hardness scale.

 Style: Available in both solid and engineered formats, with smooth, hand-scraped, or distressed finishes.

 Installation: Offered in tongue-and-groove or click-lock formats.

 2. Engineered Bamboo Flooring

 Construction: Features a real bamboo veneer over a stable plywood or HDPC core.

 Moisture Resistance: Better suited for areas with fluctuating humidity or temperature.

 Design Versatility: Compatible with floating, glue-down, or nail-down installation

 3. HDPC Strand Bamboo

 Technology: Combines strand bamboo with a waterproof HDPC core and an attached pad.

 Benefits: Waterproof, dent-resistant, and suitable for basements, kitchens, and even bathrooms.

 Ease of Installation: Click-lock system allows for fast DIY-friendly installation.

 Best-Selling Wellmade Bamboo Products

 Several products in the Wellmade lineup have become customer favorites based on durability, appearance, and ease of installation. These top-performing styles are frequently found in both residential and commercial settings.

1. Wellmade HDPC Strand Woven Bamboo in “Antique Java”

Color: Rich, dark brown with subtle variation.

Finish: Wire-brushed texture with a low-gloss finish.

Core: HDPC waterproof composite.

Reviews: Highly rated for its durability and visual appeal, often 
praised for hiding dust and scratches well.

2. Wellmade Strand Woven Engineered Bamboo in “Natural”

Color: Classic light blonde bamboo tone.

Finish: Smooth finish with UV-cured aluminum oxide coating.

Durability: One of the hardest and longest-lasting finishes available.

Popular Use: Preferred for brightening up small rooms or creating a clean, minimalist aesthetic.

3. Wellmade Click-Lock Bamboo in “Gunstock”

Style: A medium, reddish-brown tone similar to traditional hardwoods.

Installation: Easy click-lock system ideal for DIY installations.

Customer Feedback: Widely appreciated in online reviews for its ease of installation and affordable price point.

What Customers Are Saying

Across retailer websites and flooring forums, Wellmade bamboo products generally receive positive reviews, with most customers highlighting the following:

Pros:

Durability: Standout strength, especially in strand-woven and HDPC lines.

Aesthetics: Wide range of colors and finishes, from rustic to modern.

Water Resistance: HDPC core options offer confidence for use in kitchens and bathrooms.

 Ease of Installation: Click-lock systems are DIY-friendly and reduce labor costs.

 Cons:

 Color Variation: As with all natural bamboo products, some customers report slight variation between planks or batches.

 Surface Scratching: While extremely hard, some finishes can show minor scratches from pet claws or furniture if not properly protected.

 Availability: Limited in-store availability in some regions; not all colors are stocked consistently.

 What Makes Wellmade a Trusted Brand

 Wellmade’s consistent performance is rooted in its attention to manufacturing quality and sustainability. The use of EcoBond? adhesives, FSC-certified bamboo sources, and formaldehyde-free cores speaks to the company‘s environmental values. Their floors are designed not only to look beautiful but also to perform well under real-life conditions — whether that’s in a busy family home or a commercial office.

Another key factor is the company’s strong retail partnerships. By aligning with Home Depot and other major outlets, Wellmade ensures that their products are accessible, competitively priced, and supported by warranties and installation services.

Wellmade bamboo flooring offers a compelling combination of eco-conscious design, advanced technology, and real-world durability. Whether you‘re looking for a waterproof solution for a high-traffic area or a warm, natural finish for a cozy living room, their product lineup has something to match.

For flooring professionals and technologists, Wellmade stands as an example of how technological innovation and environmental responsibility can go hand-in-hand — a model worth watching as bamboo flooring continues to evolve.
