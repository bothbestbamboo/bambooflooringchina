Carbonized Strand Woven Bamboo Flooring with EVA Backing: The Perfect Blend of Elegance and Durability

Posted on 2024-11-26 7:21:35

Both designers and homeowners are constantly searching for flooring alternatives that combine sustainability, durability, and aesthetic appeal. Now for the cutting-edge flooring option that meets all requirements: carbonised strand woven bamboo flooring with EVA backing. This cutting-edge flooring is swiftly rising to the top of the market for contemporary homes and businesses because to its amazing appearance, environmentally responsible origins, and exceptional performance.

What Is Special About Carbonised Strand Woven Bamboo Flooring?


Conventional bamboo floors and even traditional hardwoods are very different from strand woven bamboo flooring. In order to create a material that is noticeably harder and more resilient than regular bamboo or many hardwoods, bamboo strands are compressed under extreme heat and pressure. The carbonisation process gives the flooring a sophisticated and classic appearance by intensifying the bamboo‘s inherent golden hues into rich, warm tones.

The EVA (ethylene-vinyl acetate) backing, however, is what makes this flooring unique and improves both its installation and functionality. High-performance bamboo and EVA backing combine to produce a flooring option that is both aesthetically pleasing and functional.

Carbonised Strand Woven Bamboo Flooring with EVA Backing Offers Several Advantages

 This flooring solution is a great choice for any room because it offers unparalleled performance and useful benefits. It‘s not just about looks. This is why it is unique:

1. Superior Durability
With a Janka hardness rating higher than that of most hardwoods, strand woven bamboo flooring is among the hardest flooring materials on the market. It is a fantastic choice for high-traffic areas of homes or businesses since it is resistant to dents, scratches, and wear. The carbonisation process improves the material‘s overall durability in addition to giving the colour depth.

2. EVA Backing for Increased Comfort
The backing layer made of EVA is revolutionary. It gives the flooring more cushioning, which makes it more comfortable to walk on, especially in areas like living rooms and kitchens where you stand for long periods of time. Furthermore, the EVA layer offers:

Noise Reduction: By absorbing sound, the backdrop reduces noise from moving furniture or footsteps.

 Resistance to Moisture: EVA backing serves as a barrier to shield your flooring from small spills and moisture, which makes it appropriate for kitchens and other humid locations.

 3. Sustainable and Eco-Friendly
One of the planet‘s most sustainable resources is bamboo. Bamboo is a quickly renewable resource that matures in 5–7 years as opposed to decades for conventional hardwoods. By selecting strand woven bamboo flooring, you can be environmentally responsible without sacrificing design or quality.

4. Gorgeous Carbonised Appearance
The bamboo has a rich, caramelised tone that radiates sophistication and warmth thanks to the carbonisation process. Because of this, it‘s a flexible choice that goes well with a variety of home design aesthetics, from sleek and modern to rustic and traditional.

5. Simple Upkeep and Installation
Compared to conventional hardwood or laminate flooring, installation is easier and more forgiving because of the EVA backing. Click-lock mechanisms are included with several variants, making installation simple and quick. Additionally, maintaining the flooring is simple; all that is needed to keep it looking immaculate is the occasional damp mop and a simple sweep.

Carbonised Strand Woven Bamboo Flooring with EVA Backing: Where Can It Be Used?

 This flooring is both long-lasting and adaptable. It may be used in practically any room of your house or place of business because of its strong construction and ability to withstand wetness. Here are a handful of examples:

Living Rooms: Use the warm tones of carbonised bamboo to create a warm and welcoming atmosphere.

 Bedrooms: Take advantage of EVA-backed flooring‘s comfort and sound-absorbing qualities for a tranquil haven.

 Kitchens: It is a useful yet fashionable option for busy kitchens because to its longevity and resilience to dampness.

 Offices: The EVA layer‘s ability to reduce noise makes it perfect for areas when silence is essential.

 Why Opt for EVA-backed carbonised strand woven bamboo flooring?

 This flooring choice is the ideal fusion of sustainability, style, and functionality. It provides a number of advantages that are difficult to overlook, whether you‘re remodelling your house or creating a brand-new area from the ground up:

Timeless Beauty: Any area is elevated by the carbonised finish‘s natural yet elegant appearance.

 Unbeatable Performance: It is designed to endure the rigours of daily life thanks to its blend of EVA-backed comfort and strand-woven durability.
Sustainable Appeal: Selecting bamboo allows you to be eco-friendly without compromising on style or quality.

Carbonised strand woven bamboo flooring with EVA backing is a fantastic option if you‘re searching for a flooring solution that provides the ideal balance of style, durability, and environmental conscience. It is an investment that improves the look and feel of your room thanks to its rich colours, exceptional durability, and extra comfort.

Enjoy the beauty, comfort, and sustainability that this cutting-edge flooring option offers your house or place of business when you upgrade your floors today. When you choose carbonised strand woven bamboo flooring, you‘re selecting a way of life rather than just a floor.
