Tarkett Flooring Reviews: Exploring Bamboo, Hardwood, and Engineered Options

Posted on 2025-1-11 7:59:55

By providing a large selection of creative, long-lasting, and aesthetically beautiful products, Tarkett has established a solid name in the flooring sector. The Tarkett name has been associated with sustainability and excellence for more than 140 years. Tarkett offers flooring options made to satisfy a variety of demands, whether you‘re searching for laminate for its usefulness, hardwood for its classic charm, or bamboo for its environmental friendliness. To learn more about what makes Tarkett a reliable alternative for homes and businesses, let‘s examine the company‘s background, line of products, and top choices.

The Legacy and History of Tarkett


Since its founding in 1886, Tarkett has developed into a world leader in sports surface and flooring solutions. The corporation operates in more than 100 countries and has its headquarters in Paris, France. Tarkett‘s dedication to sustainability, innovation, and workmanship is the foundation of its success.

From its first days of manufacturing linoleum flooring to its current line of innovative products, Tarkett has continuously adjusted to shifting consumer preferences and market trends. By employing closed-loop recycling systems and recycled materials in its manufacturing operations, the company is also a leader in environmentally friendly activities. Tarkett has gained notoriety as a conscientious and progressive brand as a result of its emphasis on sustainability.

Bamboo Flooring: An Eco-Friendly and Contemporary Choice

 Homeowners that care about the environment are increasingly choosing bamboo flooring, and Tarkett has premium solutions that blend sustainability and design. Bamboo is an environmentally favourable substitute for conventional hardwood trees because it is a renewable resource that grows considerably more quickly.

Bamboo flooring from Tarkett is designed to last and function well. Because of its exceptional resistance to wear, dampness, and scratches, it can be used in both residential and commercial settings. Tarkett‘s bamboo flooring come in a range of finishes, from light natural tones to darker, more dramatic hues, making them adaptable to a number of interior design styles.

Bamboo flooring is a great option for modern interiors because of its smooth, clean lines and durability, which allows it to endure daily wear and tear.

Hardwood Flooring: Classical Artistry

 Tarkett‘s hardwood line is a prime example of the timeless attractiveness of hardwood flooring, which is still a popular option for bringing cosiness and elegance to any area. Premium wood species including oak, maple, hickory, and walnut are used by Tarkett to make flooring that are both aesthetically pleasing and long-lasting.

Tarkett‘s hardwood flooring is distinguished by its painstaking attention to detail. Every plank is expertly made to ensure a smooth fit and consistent look. Additionally, Tarkett employs cutting-edge finishing methods to bring out the wood‘s inherent beauty while shielding it from wear, stains, and moisture.

In addition to wire-brushed and hand-scraped textures, customers can select from a range of finishes, such as matte, semi-gloss, and high-gloss alternatives. Additionally, Tarkett provides a variety of plank lengths and widths, allowing homeowners to construct custom and distinctive patterns.

Tarkett‘s hardwood flooring is perfect for living rooms, dining rooms, and bedrooms because of its rich textures and colours, which may create a cosy and opulent ambiance.

Laminate Flooring: Useful and Adaptable

 Because it is inexpensive, simple to install, and can mimic the appearance of natural materials like stone and wood, laminate flooring has grown in popularity. The laminate flooring collections from Tarkett are made to offer a premium substitute without sacrificing durability or elegance.

The multi-layer design of Tarkett‘s laminate floors increases their durability and robustness. It is a sensible option for busy homes and high-traffic areas because the top layer is covered with a protective coating that is resistant to fading, stains, and scratches. A high-resolution printed pattern that resembles tile, bamboo, or hardwood is placed underneath this layer.

The locking mechanism of Tarkett‘s laminate flooring is one of its best qualities; it makes installation simple and requires no glue. Because of this, it‘s a fantastic choice for do-it-yourselfers or anyone wishing to finish a project fast and effectively. There is a laminate floor to fit every taste thanks to Tarkett‘s assortment of styles, colours, and textures.

Well-liked Tarkett Items

 A number of Tarkett items have gained popularity among consumers as a result of their exceptional functionality, creative designs, and visual appeal.

iD Inspiration Planks of Vinyl

 Tarkett‘s iD Inspiration line of vinyl planks is noteworthy, even if its bamboo, oak, and laminate alternatives are its most well-known offerings. These planks provide a strong and waterproof substitute for conventional materials because they are made to resemble real wood and stone.

 Collection of Pure Hardwood

 One of Tarkett‘s most popular hardwood flooring collections is the Pure Hardwood Collection. It offers a variety of traditional and modern designs made from wood that is supplied sustainably. The collection is a popular option for homes because to its timeless charm and durability.

 The Woodstock Laminate Collection

 Among Tarkett‘s laminate products, the Woodstock Laminate Collection stands out. This series offers the allure of hardwood at a significantly reduced price thanks to its authentic wood grain patterns and textured finishes. It is a favourite among do-it-yourselfers because it is made to be quick and simple to install.

 The Bamboo Elite Series

 Tarkett‘s dedication to sustainability and innovative design is exemplified by its Bamboo Elite Series. This series is a popular choice for environmentally aware customers since it offers bamboo flooring with improved durability and a variety of fashionable treatments.

 The Reason Tarkett Is Unique

 The secret to Tarkett‘s success is its ability to strike a balance between sustainability, style, and utility. From comfortable living rooms to busy business settings, the company provides a broad variety of flooring options to suit the requirements of various settings.

By emphasising creativity and workmanship, Tarkett consistently pushes the limits of flooring design. Its goods are designed for long-lasting performance in addition to being aesthetically pleasing. Tarkett offers flooring options that improve your space‘s appearance and usability, whether you‘re drawn to the eco-friendly advantages of bamboo, the classic beauty of hardwood, or the useful adaptability of laminate.

Tarkett‘s dedication to sustainability and client pleasure guarantees that our flooring serves as a basis for improved living, not merely a surface.
