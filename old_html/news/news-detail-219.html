What is Outdoor High Durability Bamboo Flooring? What are the advantages?

Posted on 2022-6-17 10:11:47

With the rapid development of the decoration industry, more and more decorative materials have entered people‘s lives. Today, new building materials are gradually entering people‘s lives. There is a kind of outdoor high-resistant bamboo floor, which has great resistance and has become a new favorite when people are decorating. So what is outdoor high-resistant bamboo decking? The following small series will give us a detailed introduction:
High-resistance floor, also known as heavy bamboo wood floor, is a deeply carbonized preservative wood. In the production process, the process requires complicated machines, and if the control is not good, it is easy to catch fire. At present, there are not many manufacturers that really achieve high resistance and carbonization.


Therefore, many on the market are dyed, and the real high resistance cost is relatively high. The bamboo and wood products after high resistance treatment will have good performance even outdoors, durable, non-cracking, corrosion-resistant and wear-resistant. The service life is much better than ordinary bamboo and wood products.
The outdoor high-resistant bamboo floor is covered with a layer of heavy bamboo fiber on the surface of the bamboo floor. Its wear resistance is very high, and it is the best among bamboo floors!


However, some bamboo floors are prone to moth-eaten because natural bamboo materials contain a lot of protein and sugar. The thickness of common bamboo floors is 15%. If the 18- or 20-piece sheets are moth-eaten, there are two situations: 1. The amount of glue used is too large, resulting in moth-eaten; 2. The bamboo sticks and bamboo yellows are not clean and the technology is immature.
For outdoor high-resistance bamboo flooring, bamboo products are required to be able to withstand changing outdoor weather, strong temperature difference, ultraviolet radiation, high temperature resistance, corrosion resistance, termite resistance and other functional characteristics, in order to be competent!


In parks, scenic spots and other places, we can often use outdoor high-resistant bamboo flooring. This is a relatively environmentally friendly decorative material. It is derived from nature and uses high-quality bamboo. After more than 20 processes, the original bamboo pulp is removed, pressed with high temperature and high pressure, and then passed through multiple layers of paint, and then baked. dry. Outdoor high-resistant bamboo flooring has the following advantages:


1. Since it is derived from nature, its finished product still retains the fresh and natural feeling of bamboo, with a beautiful and special texture. The color is elegant and soft, can absorb ultraviolet rays, and effectively protect our eyesight.


2. Everyone knows that bamboo has good toughness, so the outdoor high-resistant bamboo floor also has this advantage. It has the advantages of high strength, good toughness, high density and durability. At the same time, it has the advantages of anti-static, anti-moth, fireproof and mildew proof. It is easy to install and is a building protection and decoration material.


3. The floor made of this material has good stability, and is not prone to cracking, deformation, discoloration, etc., no matter in high temperature or low temperature environment. It can be said that the cracking deformation rate is much smaller than that of solid wood floors. Warm in winter and cool in summer, it can automatically adjust and maintain the temperature so that it does not heat up. And low thermal conductivity, help reduce arthritis, heart disease.
