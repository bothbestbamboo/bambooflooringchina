Challenge Lab: Babies Do Better on Pure Solid Wood Floors Than on Formaldehyde Floors

Posted on 2024-10-28 7:21:57

Formaldehyde is without a doubt the top reaction when it comes to family health dangers. Children between the ages of five and six have breathing heights in the 80–120 cm range, which is where formaldehyde is most likely to cause harm. Jiusheng suggested using pure solid wood floor heating to better safeguard children‘s health because it doesn‘t emit formaldehyde in hot weather or during summer floor heating (solid wood naturally produces trace formaldehyde; no artificial formaldehyde is added). How can Jiusheng use the word "pure" with such assurance to highlight that it has accomplished true environmental protection and true health? The review team for this "Challenge Laboratory" problem visited Jiusheng R&D Center to learn more!

The true protection of the environment is 0 glue 0 formaldehyde.

The idea of "pure solid wood" has generated a lot of interest and contentious debate in the industry and in many spheres of life since Jiusheng Floor‘s news conference on February 29, 2024, with the topic "Focus on pure solid wood, 0 glue is 0 formaldehyde", was held. Jiusheng encourages "pure solid wood for floor heating flooring" and highlights the importance of "non-solid wood composite" from the standpoints of brand marketing, product promotion, and store image. What, then, distinguishes "solid wood composite flooring" from "pure solid wood floor heating flooring"? In contrast to Jiusheng pure solid wood floor heating flooring, which is made from an entire piece of wood and pure natural glue, solid wood composite flooring is clearly stratified from a structural perspective, with a layer of glue applied to the two surfaces between each layer to hold them together.

What makes 0 glue so eco-friendly, then? The national standard now only specifies environmental protection levels for composite products. Since pure solid wood is a natural material and the national standard does not require formaldehyde detection, it is acknowledged as the healthiest flooring category. To spread the word, the most strict environmental protection standard in the world, ENF level, is currently the highest level of environmental protection for domestic composite products. Even so-called ENF-level solid wood composite flooring, also known as formaldehyde-free flooring, has the unstated risk of producing too much formaldehyde in hot summer temperatures and floor heating settings. Consequently, using 0 glue takes environmental protection a step further by removing formaldehyde products from their source.

True health comes from all-around care.

Children‘s health necessitates multifaceted care in addition to zero formaldehyde. After being handled with floor heating technology, Jiusheng‘s pure solid wood floor heating floor is more stable and healthier in hotter climates. It may be utilized with confidence whether or not the house has floor heating. In addition to being skin-friendly and warm, the wooden floor makes it difficult for water droplets or moisture to accumulate on the surface during the rainy season and the return of the south. Additionally, it is difficult for the infant to slip, making it safer for them to play.

Jiusheng uses several cutting-edge technologies to improve original proprietary technologies, boost product competitiveness, and consolidate the fundamental performance of its goods in response to the increasingly complex and varied demands of its customers. The Jiusheng pure solid wood floor heating floor‘s flexible surface technology, light retention, and scratch resistance make it more resilient to wear and scratches than regular wooden floors. It also has a longer lifespan. The baby‘s lovely eyes are readily protected by the 7° micro-light floor surface layer created by the recently improved diffuse reflection technology. Four Sino-German dual patent floor heating technologies—the Hengxin technology, the German lock patent technology, the German PE quiet patent technology, and the microwave drying method—completely ensure product stability, allowing for the use of larger, more stable, and solid boards for floor heating.

Not only is the word "pure" added, but the flooring business is also being upgraded and transformed in response to health and environmental issues. It is a green floor that is better suited for Chinese newborns. Jiusheng pure solid wood heating floor, high appearance, high performance, 0 glue, 0 formaldehyde, and even in the event of extreme temperatures, it is not frightened of health problems.
