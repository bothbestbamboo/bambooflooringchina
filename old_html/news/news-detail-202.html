Learn 4 steps to extend the service life of bamboo flooring and wood floors

Posted on 2022-5-9 8:30:57

With the continuous development trend of social economy, people‘s consumption concept is also undergoing extraordinary changes. Everyone is slowly using bamboo flooring for interior decoration, but many customers do not understand how to maintain bamboo flooring. I will share with you the maintenance methods of bamboo flooring.


1. Maintain ventilation and dryness


Always ensure ventilation and dryness, so that the bamboo flooring and wood floor compounds can evaporate as much as possible, and often open windows and room doors to allow air convection, so that the room has a comfortable and clean natural environment.


2. Prevent damage to the surface of the bamboo wooden floor


The lacquer of the bamboo flooring and wood floor is a very core decorative design and protective layer, so it is necessary to minimize the collision of hard blocks, friction of metal materials, scratches of equipment, etc. In addition, it is also necessary to handle furniture when loading, unloading and exercising, and the feet of the furniture are also Put vulcanized rubber skin, etc.


3. Prevent sun exposure and precipitation from getting wet


If sunlight and precipitation immediately enter the bamboo flooring and wood floor of the house from the window, it will damage the bamboo and wood floor. The sun will not only accelerate the embrittlement of the car paint and glue, but also continue to cause the ground to crack. If it is wet by rain, it will cause the bamboo flooring and wood floor to swell and deform, and even make the ground grow mildew.


4. Frequent cleaning


In daily use, the road surface should be kept clean and hygienic, and the wooden floor should be cleaned. In fact, you should first use a broom to clean up the dust and impurities, and then use a dry towel to scrub. If you accidentally spill a chemical substance with water content on the road, you should wipe it off with a dry towel immediately. If you can, apply a layer of wax every once in a while to maintain the wood floors.
