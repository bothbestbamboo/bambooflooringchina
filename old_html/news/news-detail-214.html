Several advantages and installation matters of outdoor high-resistant bamboo decking

Posted on 2022-5-25 8:14:23

In our cognitive concept, the floor is generally indoor. I believe that what you see in places may be very few. Maybe in some villas, some other tourist spots or other places. It may be more common at home, and may be less common in residential areas. So if it is this kind of outdoor high-resistant bamboo decking, what should I pay attention to? Or when we choose him, how should we choose? Its advantages are that it can withstand changing outdoor weather, strong temperature difference, ultraviolet radiation, high temperature resistance, corrosion resistance, termite resistance and other functional characteristics.


The advantage of outdoor high-resistant bamboo decking is that when it is outdoors, it may rain often, and then it may be windy or snowy. If we have this kind of weather, it must be of very good quality to withstand these, bad weather, or these storms out there. So it must be very corrosive and waterproof. Because if it‘s not good, then it will get very wet, it will get moldy when it is very young, the place will rot, and then its quality will be destroyed, and the aesthetics may also be destroyed. If it is in some villas or going to scenic spots, this kind of place will definitely be damaged after it is destroyed.


The second benefit of outdoor high-resistance bamboo decking is that it is mildew-proof and moth-proof. Because they are usually outdoors, most of them are in some places with particularly good environments, where there are many trees, grasses and flowers. But if the environment is good, its green plants are independent. If the floor is not particularly anti-counterfeiting or moth-proof, it can be easily damaged by these bugs.


In fact, outdoor high-resistant bamboo decking has many advantages, such as strong aesthetics, or more durable, not easy to deform, and can also prevent electricity. It is a green and environmentally friendly product, and everyone can use it with confidence.


1. Green protection: Bamboo is an environmentally friendly and rapidly renewable resource. Regular wood takes 60 years to reach the lifespan of solid wood, while bamboo can regenerate 12 times in the same amount of time. Originating from nature, the finished product still retains the fresh and natural feel of bamboo, with a beautiful and special texture.


2. Hard and wear-resistant: According to the relevant hardness test results, the hardness of outdoor high-resistance bamboo decking is stronger than that of Jaramu, and also higher than most common tree species.


3. Healthy and pollution-free: The production process of outdoor high-resistance bamboo decking uses a harmless water-based surface layer as a protective layer, which does not cause any pollution to the environment.


4. Low expansion rate and low deformation: The production process of outdoor high-resistance bamboo decking includes slicing and compaction of bamboo capillary pores, which is not easy to deform and has high stability.


5. Scratch resistance: outdoor high-resistance bamboo decking made by Seiko, multi-layer high-quality foreign high-quality paint. Durable high quality paint low gloss polishing system provides a scratch safe aluminium oxide protective layer.


6. Easy to install: Outdoor high-resistant bamboo decking has its special installation structure, traditional flat buckle decking and lock buckle decking, no glue and nails are needed, and the installation is simple and fast.
