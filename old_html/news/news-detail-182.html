Solid Bamboo Flooring: The Element of Future

Posted on 2020-12-22 8:17:31

Solid bamboo flooring has appeared not many years ago, however, they have natural properties, with elegant finishes, amazing colour and  precise process to make a total beyond than the most traditional hardwood flooring. Thanks to their different shades, they adapt perfectly to classic or more modern atmospheres and any type of room. Its sustainability, maintenance and technical characteristics have made it the material to be taken into account for the development of pavements in the coming years.


Environmentally friendly


The most noteworthy quality of the solid bamboo flooring is its respect for the environment. While it is true that today, renowned brands and manufacturers of wooden pallets meet requirements of sustainability that allow to obtain the wood in the best conditions and destroy the environment as little as possible.


With the solid bamboo flooring, humans will have a leap in quality in terms of ecology. Thanks to its form of production, through bamboo reeds, the impact on nature is almost minimal. You can collect a third of the amount of bamboo that exists today, every year, without its presence being compromised and allowing it to reproduce sustainably to continue the exploitation of it.


In a world where resources are increasingly limited, we are forced to obtain products that can remain for many years for use without compromising the amount of resources that we will continue to consume.  This possibility makes it an exceptional material to achieve a natural, elegant, warm and sustainable place.


Versatility


Another of the strengths of the solid bamboo flooring is its versatility. The high quality of the product, gets a warm and pleasant feeling when you move around the house. In addition, the touch of its sheets, is of extreme softness and will allow you to enjoy the touch of the wood directly on your feet. All this, coupled with its hardness and resistance, make its sustainability not only when it comes to producing it, but also, at the time of maintenance and changing it, taking much longer than you must use to replace another type of solid wood flooring.


The large number of finishes, from natural vertical to softer shades in horizontal orientation, achieve an avant-garde effect on the decoration of spaces. On many occasions, your guests will not be able to distinguish whether it is solid bamboo flooring or a type of mahogany or brown oak. Thanks to their development, customers find everything from narrower and more traditional pallets, to wider pallets, which try to achieve a more current effect with the appearance of the fewest joints on the pavement.


The last of the characteristics that make it an indispensable floor in a current house, is its ability to dilate and contract, much less than in other types of wood, with which, the wood will be more stable and durable and will offer fewer problems in meetings with the skirting or doors of the house, being able to have larger floor cloths.


How to make a difference?


For those who are looking to save some money without giving up the warmth, hardness and naturalness of bamboo, Bamboo Industrial models have a wide range of finishes that will undoubtedly adapt to your furniture or the rest of the decoration of your house. Its economical price also makes it the first choice for those who are thinking of decorating their premises or office with a more sustainable material and thus complying with the relevant environmental quality seals.


If what you are looking for is a leap of quality with lighter colours and shades and a look very similar to the pine or spruce wood platform, the Pure Bamboo flooring range will delight your guests. Its normal or toasted format offers clients the possibility to choose between two totally different styles to decorate their space.


What are the differences between bamboo and wood floors?


Wood has always been one of the traditional materials used when looking to bring a special warmth to the pavement of a house. However, progress has made it possible to achieve the same effect today with other soils that are more environmentally friendly and priced lower. While bamboo regenerates in a short time, trees that are cut down to obtain solid wood take years to grow back.


In addition, although a priori may seem like a hoax, bamboo flooring is much stronger than wood, which results in remaining intact for much longer despite the humidity conditions or daily use of any family. Finally, it cannot be forgotten that wood has a much higher cost than any other material, not only because of its production process, but also because of the difficulty involved in assembly.
