What are the first-line brands of bamboo flooring

Posted on 2022-5-6 15:51:21

Bamboo flooring is a kind of high-grade decoration material used in residences, hotels and offices, etc. It is mainly used to decorate the ground. The main material of bamboo floor is bamboo, which is made of adhesive and high temperature and high pressure. Bamboo after removing sugar, fat, starch fat, protein and other special harmless treatment has super anti-moth-eaten function. The floor is non-toxic, firm and stable, and has the natural texture of bamboo. It has both the natural beauty of the hardwoood floor and the sturdy and durable advantages of ceramic floor tiles. It is a relatively popular floor material.


At present, the concept of low-carbon home green living is rampant. Environmental protection and health have become the most important points of attention of consumers when they are decorating. As a leading enterprise of wood flooring in China, it is even more obligatory for flooring to assume social responsibility in the field of low-carbon home furnishing. The relevant person in charge of flooring said: "It is an inescapable social responsibility and mission to advocate green environmental protection and low-carbon energy saving." According to The reporter learned that it is about to enter the field of bamboo flooring and launch heavy bamboo flooring to fill the gap in the domestic high-end bamboo flooring market. This major decision is another bold initiative after it has built a green industrial chain.


Ms. Zhou, When I was visiting the furniture fair this year, I found out that there are furniture made of bamboo fiber. At that time, I thought: "Hey, why haven‘t I seen bamboo floors in the market now?" When I was reading the newspaper a few days ago When I found out that I was finally going to push bamboo floors, I felt that I was really at the forefront of the market. Now I‘m hoping that the price of bamboo floors will be very affordable!


Now the floors on the market are getting more and more fancy, but for our generation, the floor is not as complicated as possible. I think the color of the floor should be simpler and more natural.
