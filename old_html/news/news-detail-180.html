What to Consider While Buying Bamboo Flooring for House?

Posted on 2020-12-7 9:08:52

If you are considering to install bamboo flooring in your space then here are some of the essential things to consider before taking a step forward. Read carefully. 


1. What kind of space is the bamboo flooring going into? 


Bamboo flooring is one the most durable and versatile material which is why its deal for houses and apartments becoming very popular among people. They use bamboo flooring for bedrooms, living rooms and even for hallways. There are two types of good quality of bamboo flooring available one is engineered bamboo flooring and another one is solid bamboo flooring. They both are dimensionally stable so can be used in home space where the temperature and humidity fluctuate, for example, space with log burners, conservatories, utility rooms and kitchens etc. This is good for such spaces because the floor will contract and expand to permit for the climate changes. 


The only space where experts ask not to install bamboo flooring in the bathroom as it may not be good water-resistant. 


2. What are the advantages of bamboo flooring? 


There are countless advantages of bamboo flooring but here are some considerate ones. 


1. Great and hygienic for people who suffer from certain allergies. 
2. Easy to maintain and make clean anytime. 
3. Compatible and best with underfloor heating. 
4. Easy and swift to connect over almost any subfloor. 
5. You can install it any room in your house. 
6. Durable and harder than any hardwood flooring. 
7. Best and cheaper substitute to hardwood flooring with the same ordinary appeal.
8. Sustainable and eco-friendly flooring material. 

9. Bamboo flooring is available in a broad variety of finishes, colours and styles. 


3. If there are any floor matching accessories available in the market? 


If you are obsessed with matching flooring accessories and doubting whether you could get anything that compliments to bamboo flooring then the answer is yes. In the market, there are plenty of bamboo flooring accessories are available to complement the design of the floor. Accessories like thresholds, stair nosing, flush reducers, pipe covers, beading and skirting etc. 


4. Whether the bamboo flooring is eco-friendly or not? 


Bamboo flooring is one of the best materials for your house that comes under budget and is eco-friendly. This product is a totally sustainable substitute for hardwood flooring. Bamboo is not a wood but a type of grass. This indicates that it can grow much faster and develops into a proper tree in maximum five years rather than trees that take more than twenty years to grow and develop into an adult. 


In order to check the eco-friendliness, you should have to check the company which is selling bamboo flooring have FSC certification (Forest Stewardship Council). This indicates that every firm involves in the procedure of manufacture and production of the bamboo flooring have also been given the certification of FSC. While buying bamboo flooring, do not forget to check the company’s FSC certification to check whether you are dealing with an authentic and trustworthy company or not. 


5. What sort of finish in flooring you should have to achieve? 


Bamboo flooring comes into two main authentic finishes: 


1. Anti-slip lacquers 

2. Satin-matt lacquers 


6. What colour and style of bamboo flooring are you desiring to have? 


Bamboo flooring is accessible in three main styles: Strand woven, Vertical and horizontal bamboo. 


Horizontal Bamboo: The layers of bamboo are wedged horizontally to make the board of flooring.


Strand Woven Bamboo: Strands of bamboo are manufactured through intense heat, pressure and weight to make the board of flooring. 


Vertical Bamboo: The layers of bamboo are wedged together plumb to make the board of flooring. 


In addition to these three variant types of structure, bamboo flooring is accessible in either a rustic effect (traditional look), a carbonised effect (dark brown tone), or a natural colour (golden, pale tones). Bamboo is a natural product, grain patterns and shades vary as per the quality and type of bamboo you are buying. If you want to add something unique and different, then adding variant colour stains to the bamboo that includes black, chocolate brown and white can help you at its best.
