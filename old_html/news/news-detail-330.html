Morning Star Bamboo Flooring Reviews: Stylish, Sustainable, and Affordable

Posted on 2024-12-7 7:47:08

Bamboo flooring has emerged as one of the most popular options for contemporary homes looking for flooring options that combine durability, style, and environmental responsibility. Morning Star Bamboo Flooring has established a reputation for providing high-quality flooring at reasonable costs among the other click solid strand bamboo flooring brands that are available. This brand, which is only available through LL Flooring (previously Lumber Liquidators), is renowned for its eco-friendly philosophy and extensive range of styles.

The history of Morning Star Bamboo Flooring, the advantages of its products, and the reasons why so many people trust it will all be covered in this blog. Morning Star may be the best option for you if you want to invest in eco-friendly flooring or renovate your house.

Presenting Bamboo Flooring by Morning Star


One of the biggest flooring dealers in the US, LL Flooring, created and markets the Morning Star Bamboo Flooring brand. Morning Star is the main bamboo flooring brand from LL Flooring, which specialises in offering high-quality flooring solutions at an affordable price.

Offering a variety of bamboo flooring styles to suit all tastes and price ranges, the business is renowned for its emphasis on sustainability and durability. Morning Star offers a contemporary look that complements a range of home décor, from lighter, natural finishes to darker, carbonised alternatives.

Morning Star‘s bamboo flooring is distinguished by its strand-woven design, which is designed to be exceptionally durable and frequently referred to as being twice as hard as wood. Because of this, it‘s a fantastic option for high-traffic areas, pet-owning households, and anyone looking for a durable flooring option without compromising design.

Why Install Bamboo Flooring? Morning Star‘s Advantages

 There are several advantages to selecting bamboo flooring over conventional hardwood or laminate, particularly when you choose a brand like Morning Star. Let‘s examine what makes this flooring choice so alluring in more detail:

1. Sustainable Flooring
One of the most environmentally friendly flooring options is bamboo. Bamboo grows quickly and can be harvested in five to seven years, in contrast to hardwood trees, which might take decades to reach maturity. Morning Star is a fantastic choice for those who care about the environment because it sources its bamboo ethically.

Furthermore, compared to traditional hardwood manufacturing, the production process for bamboo flooring often consumes less energy and produces less waste. Selecting Morning Star Bamboo Flooring lessens the environmental impact of your house while promoting eco-friendly behaviours.

2. Sturdiness and Power
The strand-woven bamboo flooring from Morning Star is made to resist severe abrasion. Strand-woven bamboo, which is substantially tougher and more resilient than conventional hardwood, is created by compressing bamboo fibres with adhesive under intense pressure.

Because of its resilience, it‘s perfect for hectic homes, particularly those with energetic kids or pets. Morning Star flooring is frequently praised by customers for its resilience to dents and scratches in high-traffic areas.

3. Versatility in Style
To accommodate various design tastes, Morning Star Bamboo Flooring provides a large selection of types and finishes. Morning Star offers solutions to fit your vision, whether you want the rich, warm hues of carbonised bamboo or the natural, light tones of conventional bamboo.

Additionally, the brand sells flooring with strand-woven, vertical, and horizontal designs. Because each variety has a unique appearance, you can alter how your room looks. For instance:

Bamboo that is horizontal displays the bamboo‘s inherent grain.
Bamboo that is vertical seems more consistent and contemporary.
Superior durability and a textured, upscale appearance are provided by strand-woven bamboo.

 4. Cost-effectiveness Without Sacrifice
Morning Star‘s cost is one of its main advantages. Morning Star is able to provide high-quality products at cheap pricing, even if some bamboo flooring brands are quite expensive. Because of this, it‘s a desirable choice for homeowners who wish to enjoy bamboo‘s advantages without going over budget.

5. Simple Installation Choices
A lot of Morning Star products have click-lock mechanisms, which allows for do-it-yourself installation. The installation procedure is made to be simple and effective, regardless of your level of experience doing it yourself or hiring a professional.

LL Flooring is the company that created the brand.

 LL Flooring, a significant player in the flooring market for many years, is the only retailer of Morning Star Bamboo Flooring. Established in 1993 under the name Lumber Liquidators, the business changed its name to LL Flooring in 2020 to better represent its expanded emphasis on a range of flooring options, such as bamboo, vinyl, laminate, and hardwood.

LL Flooring is renowned for its dedication to provide reasonably priced flooring alternatives without sacrificing quality. With more than 400 locations around the United States and a strong online store, LL Flooring makes it simple for clients to browse and buy their goods.

The business also places a high priority on sustainability, providing eco-friendly solutions like Morning Star Bamboo Flooring and sourcing products ethically. To guarantee a seamless shopping experience, LL Flooring also offers comprehensive product information, installation instructions, and customer service.

What Makes Bamboo Flooring by Morning Star Unique?
There are several important ways that Morning Star Bamboo Flooring sets itself apart from other bamboo brands:

Quality Control: By making sure Morning Star products adhere to stringent quality standards, LL Flooring gives customers peace of mind about their purchases.

 Variety of Styles: Morning Star has a large selection of structures, colours, and finishes to suit any taste.

 Durability Reputation: Morning Star‘s strand-woven bamboo is frequently cited as one of the most resilient flooring options.

 Accessibility: Morning Star is extensively accessible both in-person and online and is solely sold through LL Flooring.

 Are You a Good Fit for Morning Star Bamboo Flooring?

 For homeowners looking to blend affordability, sustainability, and style, Morning Star Bamboo Flooring is a great option. Its eco-friendly manufacturing technique appeals to people who value green living, and its endurance makes it especially well-suited for active households.

Nevertheless, like with any flooring choice, it‘s critical to take your unique requirements and tastes into account. Morning Star and other bamboo floors can be moisture-sensitive and need to be properly acclimated and maintained in order to function properly. Morning Star can be a lovely and useful addition to your house if you‘re prepared to take care of your flooring and adhere to installation instructions.


Morning Star Bamboo Flooring has established a solid reputation as an eco-friendly, long-lasting, and fashionable flooring choice. Supported by LL Flooring‘s dedication to both price and quality, this brand provides exceptional value for homeowners wishing to update their living area.

For your next flooring project, Morning Star Bamboo Flooring is a good choice, regardless of your preference for its contemporary style, eco-friendliness, or affordable price. To locate the ideal fit for your house, take your time looking through its selection, ordering samples, and speaking with LL Flooring representatives. Morning Star Bamboo Flooring can offer years of beauty and functionality with the right setup and maintenance.
