US Floors: Innovation and Quality in Flooring Solutions

Posted on 2025-1-16 8:22:21

US Floors is a well-known brand that is associated with innovation, sustainability, and superior craftsmanship when it comes to high-end flooring solutions. US Floors has established a reputation for itself in the flooring sector over the years by providing goods that blend durability, style, and environmental awareness. US Floors offers an amazing selection of options to suit a range of demands, whether you‘re a professional in the field or a household searching for a distinctive flooring solution.

This blog examines the US Floors brand, its line of products, its best-selling items, and the company‘s development into a reputable name in the flooring industry.

Presenting US Floors


A reputable name in the flooring sector, US Floors is well-known for its creative product designs and dedication to environmentally responsible operations. US Floors, a Dalton, Georgia-based company that specialises in environmentally friendly flooring options, provides a large selection of goods to meet both business and residential requirements.

US Floors‘ commitment to minimising its environmental effect while providing high-performing flooring solutions is a fundamental component of company philosophy. The company has distinguished itself from rivals in the industry by being a pioneer in the introduction of flooring goods produced from sustainable and renewable materials.

Perhaps US Floors‘ most well-known product is the COREtec range, which revolutionised the luxury vinyl plank (LVP) market. The firm is now a market leader in resilient flooring because to this invention, which appeals to customers who appreciate both practicality and style.

Types of Products Offered by US Floors US Floors provides a wide selection of flooring products that are made to satisfy a variety of needs and tastes. Among these goods are:

1. Tile and Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP)

 The flagship product line under US Floors is the COREtec collection, which offers opulent vinyl planks and tiles that are incredibly durable in addition to being aesthetically pleasing. Because COREtec products are completely waterproof, they are perfect for places like basements, bathrooms, and kitchens. Because of its revolutionary click-lock mechanism, they are very simple to install.

2. Flooring made of cork

 Leading the way in cork flooring, a sustainable alternative derived from cork oak tree bark, is US Floors. Because it is hypoallergenic, naturally insulating, and soft underfoot, cork flooring is a great option for those who care about the environment.

3. Flooring made of bamboo

 Another environmentally friendly choice from US Floors is bamboo flooring. Bamboo is renowned for its inherent beauty and toughness, and it adds a distinctive look that goes well with many different types of interior design.

4. Flooring made of hardwood

 Additionally, US Floors provides engineered hardwood flooring, which blends the classic beauty of wood with the usefulness of contemporary production methods. These flooring are made to endure normal wear and tear and still look beautiful for many years.

5. Carpet Tiles 

US Floors offers long-lasting, low-maintenance carpet tiles in a variety of hues and designs for commercial areas.

Best-Selling Items from US Warehouses

 Over the years, a number of US Floors products have gained popularity among consumers because to their outstanding quality and cutting-edge features. Among the most well-liked products are:

COREtec Plus

 In the market for high-end vinyl flooring, the COREtec Plus series is a pioneer. It comes in a variety of forms, such as stone-look tiles and wood-look planks, and is made with a waterproof core and a long-lasting wear layer. Because of its realistic appearance, ease of installation, and moisture resistance, this product is especially well-liked.

COREtec Pro Plus

 An improved version of COREtec Plus, the Pro Plus series provides increased durability for high-traffic areas. It offers the same waterproof advantages with extra robustness, making it a popular option for busy homes and business settings.

Organic Cork

 For individuals looking for a comfortable and environmentally friendly flooring alternative, US Floors‘ cork flooring is an exceptional choice. It is perfect for areas where comfort and silence are valued due to its inherent thermal and acoustic insulation qualities.

Strand Woven Bamboo Customers who adore the appearance of hardwood but prefer a more environmentally friendly option are drawn to US Floors‘ strand-woven bamboo flooring. It is a sensible option for homes with kids and dogs because of its high density and scratch resistance.

The US Floors‘ Journey

 US Floors‘ narrative is one of creativity and dedication to sustainability. Piet Dossche founded the business in 2001 as a distributor of distinctive and eco-friendly flooring options. US Floors grew its product line over time and became well-known for its commitment to sustainability and high quality.

The launch of COREtec in 2013 marked a significant turning point for the business. Many of the drawbacks of conventional vinyl flooring, including its susceptibility to dampness and lack of foot comfort, were resolved by this ground-breaking product. COREtec revolutionised the flooring business with its waterproof core, realistic designs, and simplicity of installation.

Shaw Industries, a Berkshire Hathaway company, purchased US Floors in 2016. Through this acquisition, US Floors was able to increase its resources and reach while keeping its emphasis on sustainability and innovation. Driven by its goal of offering flooring solutions that are both aesthetically pleasing and ecologically conscious, the brand is still thriving today as a market leader in flooring.

Why Opt for US Floors?

 Quality, innovation, and sustainability are the three main pillars upon which US Floors has established its name. Its goods are made to satisfy the demands of contemporary customers who want flooring solutions that are not only fashionable but also long-lasting and eco-friendly.

The company‘s COREtec brand, which has revolutionised the way people think about luxury vinyl flooring, is a clear example of its dedication to innovation. US Floors is a popular option for environmentally minded consumers because of its distinctive use of sustainable materials like bamboo and cork.

In addition to its product line, US Floors is renowned for its outstanding customer service and unwavering dedication to product warranty. US Floors serves a broad clientele by offering a large selection of styles, materials, and pricing ranges, guaranteeing that there is something to suit every preference and financial range.

US Floors has completely reimagined what it means to provide premium, eco-friendly flooring options. It stands out in the flooring sector thanks to its cutting-edge products, environmentally friendly operations, and commitment to client pleasure. US Floors has a product that can improve any space, whether you‘re drawn to the natural beauty of cork and bamboo or the waterproof performance of COREtec.
