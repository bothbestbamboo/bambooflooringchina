Bamboo Flooring in Australia: Leading Suppliers and Popular Styles

Posted on 2025-1-14 7:41:54

As an eco-friendly, fashionable, and long-lasting substitute for conventional timber floors, bamboo flooring has become increasingly popular in Australia over the years. Its eco-friendliness, affordability, and adaptability make it a popular option for both interior designers and homeowners. However, what is so unique about bamboo flooring, and who are Australia‘s top suppliers? Let‘s examine the major figures and the hottest trends that are changing Australian homes.

For what reason is bamboo flooring used?


Because bamboo flooring is made from a rapidly expanding renewable resource, it stands out as an environmentally beneficial choice. Bamboo grows to full size in 5-7 years, as opposed to 20-50 years for hardwood trees. This not only makes it a sustainable resource, but it also makes it frequently less expensive than conventional timber flooring.

Bamboo flooring is valued not only for its sustainability but also for its longevity. The exceptional hardness of strand-woven bamboo, in instance, frequently surpasses that of conventional hardwoods like oak or maple. Furthermore, because of Australia‘s varied climate, which ranges from core aridity to coastal dampness, bamboo floors are resistant to warping and swelling.

Australia‘s Most Popular Styles of Bamboo Flooring

 Whether you‘re remodelling a historic home or creating a contemporary flat, bamboo flooring comes in a range of designs to suit any style. The following are some of the most popular designs among homeowners in Australia:

1. Woven bamboo strands

 Strand-woven bamboo is a popular option because of its exceptional toughness and elegant look. In order to create a dense and durable substance, bamboo fibres are compressed under intense heat and pressure. It is perfect for high-traffic areas like kitchens, corridors, and living rooms because of its robustness. Because of its adaptability in design, strand-woven bamboo is frequently available in natural, coffee, or carbonised tones.

2. Grain, both vertical and horizontal

 Grain patterns that are horizontal and vertical are popular for people who want a more conventional bamboo appearance. Horizontal bamboo has a more organic and rustic appearance due to its larger grain pattern, which highlights the natural nodes of the bamboo stem. In contrast, the pattern of vertical bamboo is tighter and more consistent, giving it a sleek, modern look.

3. Finishes with matte

 In Australia, matte finishes are becoming more and more popular because of their subtle elegance and usefulness. Because matte flooring are less likely to reveal dust or scratches than shiny ones, they are perfect for busy homes. Modern minimalist homes are also enhanced by the subtle sheen of bamboo with a matte surface.

4. Bamboo that is distressed and hand-scraped

 Bamboo flooring that is hand-scraped and weathered is a popular option for homeowners who appreciate the allure of vintage or industrial-style decor. These designs give character and cosiness to any area with their textured surfaces that resemble old wood.

5. Bamboo flooring with wide planks

 The capacity of wide plank bamboo flooring to enlarge compact areas has led to its recent appeal. Open-concept living spaces benefit greatly from the smooth, expansive appearance created by the wider planks. From light natural tones to deep espresso hues, wide plank alternatives come in a variety of finishes.

How to Pick the Best Bamboo Flooring

 Considerations including colour, finish, and durability are crucial when choosing bamboo flooring. Because of its durability, strand-woven bamboo is a great option for flooring in high-traffic areas. The overall appearance of your rooms can be improved with matte or hand-scraped finishes for areas that need a more sophisticated feel.

Additionally, make sure the bamboo flooring you buy has been verified by respectable environmental groups like Good Environmental Choice Australia (GECA) or the Forest Stewardship Council (FSC). This guarantees that there are no dangerous ingredients and that the product is sourced sustainably.

Bamboo flooring‘s combination of sustainability, toughness, and visual appeal has solidified its position as one of Australia‘s top flooring options. There are numerous options for homeowners to discover the ideal match for their interiors thanks to a variety of reliable providers and styles. Whether you like the sleek modernity of strand-woven planks or the rough beauty of hand-scraped bamboo, bamboo flooring is a classic choice for Australian homes.
