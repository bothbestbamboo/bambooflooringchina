ENF-grade panels: the leader of new heights of environmental protection

Posted on 2024-10-31 7:21:37

In today‘s era of environmental protection and health, the environmental protection level of panels has become the focus of consumers‘ attention during the decoration process. Among them, ENF-grade panels stand out with their excellent environmental protection performance and have become a popular choice in the market. So, what exactly is ENF-grade board?

1. Definition of ENF-grade panels

ENF grade is the highest standard in the environmental protection level of panels in my country. According to the "Grading of Formaldehyde Emission of Artificial panels and Their Products" standard, the formaldehyde emission of ENF-grade panels is ≤0.025mg/m3. This standard is far lower than the common E0-grade (formaldehyde emission ≤0.050mg/m3) and E1-grade (formaldehyde emission ≤0.124mg/m3) panels on the market.

2. Characteristics of ENF-grade panels

1. Extreme environmental protection
The biggest feature of ENF-grade panels is their extremely low formaldehyde emission. Formaldehyde is a chemical substance that is harmful to the human body. Long-term exposure may cause health problems such as respiratory diseases and cancer. ENF-grade panels are strictly controlled in the production process to control the formaldehyde emission at an extremely low level, providing consumers with a safe and healthy indoor environment.

2. High-quality raw materials
In order to meet the environmental protection standards of ENF level, manufacturers usually use high-quality raw materials. For example, adhesives without formaldehyde addition and environmentally friendly wood raw materials are used. These high-quality raw materials not only ensure the environmental performance of the board, but also improve the physical properties and service life of the board.

3. Strict production process
The production process of ENF-grade panels needs to be strictly controlled. From the selection of raw materials, the optimization of processing technology to the strict control of quality inspection, every link must ensure that the formaldehyde emission of the board meets the ENF-level standard. For example, advanced hot pressing technology and environmentally friendly coating technology are used to reduce the generation and release of formaldehyde.

III. Types of ENF-grade panels

1. Solid wood particle board
Solid wood particle board is a man-made board made of wood or other wood cellulose materials, which is glued together under heat and pressure after applying adhesive. ENF-grade solid wood particle board usually uses adhesives without formaldehyde addition, which is environmentally friendly, stable and durable. It is suitable for furniture making, interior decoration and other fields.

2. Medium-density fiberboard
Medium-density fiberboard is made of wood fiber or other plant fiber as raw materials, prepared by fiber, applied with synthetic resin, and pressed into board under heating and pressure conditions. ENF-grade medium-density fiberboard also uses environmentally friendly adhesives and production processes, and has the advantages of high flatness, moderate strength and easy processing. It is widely used in furniture, cabinets, floors and other fields.

3. Multi-layer solid wood board
Multi-layer solid wood board is made of three or more layers of single board or thin board wood board glued and hot pressed. ENF-grade multi-layer solid wood board usually uses adhesives without formaldehyde addition and high-quality wood raw materials, which is environmentally friendly, stable and beautiful. It is suitable for high-end furniture, interior decoration and other fields.

IV. Advantages of ENF-grade panels

1. Protect family health
The extremely low formaldehyde emission of ENF-grade panels can effectively reduce indoor air pollution and protect family health. Especially for sensitive groups such as children, pregnant women, and the elderly, using ENF-grade panels can reduce their risk of being harmed by formaldehyde.

2. Improve the quality of decoration
ENF-grade panels not only have excellent environmental performance, but also have a high level in appearance and quality. It can provide high-quality material guarantees for decoration projects and improve the quality and grade of decoration.

3. Comply with environmental protection trends
With the continuous improvement of people‘s awareness of environmental protection, environmentally friendly materials are becoming more and more popular in the market. As one of the panels with the highest environmental protection level, ENF-grade panels are in line with the current environmental protection trend and have broad market prospects.

V. How to identify ENF-grade panels

1. Check the test report
Formal ENF-grade board manufacturers will provide test reports issued by authoritative organizations. Consumers can check the test reports to confirm whether the environmental protection level of the board meets the ENF-level standards.

2. Observe the appearance of the board
The appearance of ENF-grade panels is usually flat and smooth, without obvious defects and odors. Consumers can make a preliminary judgment on its quality and environmental protection performance by observing the appearance of the board.

3. Choose a well-known brand
Choosing ENF-grade panels from well-known brands can ensure the quality and environmental performance of the products. Well-known brands usually have strict quality control systems and good after-sales services, which can provide more protection for consumers.

Summary: ENF-grade panels are the highest standard in the environmental protection level of panels in my country. They are characterized by extreme environmental protection, high-quality raw materials, and strict production processes. It has a wide variety, including solid wood particle panels, medium-density fiberpanels, multi-layer solid wood panels, etc. ENF-grade panels have obvious advantages, which can protect the health of the family, improve the quality of decoration, and comply with environmental protection trends. When choosing ENF-grade panels, consumers can identify their authenticity by checking the test report, observing the appearance of the panels, and choosing well-known brands.
