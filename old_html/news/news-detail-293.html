Introducing Corundum: The Next Development in Wear-Resistant Flooring

Posted on 2024-10-30 7:22:19

In the field of flooring solutions, lifespan and durability are critical factors. Corundum has become an innovative choice as both businesses and homeowners look for materials that can endure long-term use and severe wear. In many different industries, corundum wear-resistant floor materials are growing in popularity due to their remarkable hardness and resistance to wear. The definition, characteristics, uses, and reasons why corundum is the best material for bamboo flooring that resists wear are all covered in this blog.

What is Corundum?


A naturally occurring mineral, corundum is mostly made up of aluminum oxide (Al?O?). With a Mohs mineral hardness rating of 9, it is among the hardest materials on the market, second only to diamond. Corundum is a great material for many uses, especially flooring, because of its exceptional hardness, chemical stability, and abrasion resistance.

Different Corundum Types

Corundum comes in two primary forms:

Natural Corundum: This form is found in nature, frequently in metamorphic rocks as a crystalline structure. Depending on its hue, sapphire or ruby are the two most prevalent names for natural corundum.

Laboratory-controlled procedures are used to create synthetic corundum. Because of its availability and consistent qualities, synthetic corundum finds widespread use in industrial applications.

The characteristics of corundum flooring

Corundum stands out in the flooring business due to its special qualities:

1. Superior Hardness
The exceptional resistance to scratches and abrasions is guaranteed by corundum‘s high degree of hardness. Because of this, it is ideal for high-traffic locations such as retail stores, warehouses, and industrial settings.

2. The Resistance to Chemicals
Because of its exceptional chemical resistance, corundum flooring is appropriate for settings like industrial plants and labs where spills and exposure to harsh materials are frequent occurrences.

3. Low Upkeep
Corundum‘s non-porous structure prevents it from absorbing liquids, which facilitates cleaning and upkeep. Usually, regular cleaning with gentle detergents is enough to keep it looking brand new.

4. The stability of heat
For kitchens and industrial spaces where heat exposure occurs frequently, corundum flooring is perfect since it can tolerate high temperatures without warping or deteriorating.

5. Visual Flexibility
From sleek modern styles to rustic ones, corundum flooring can be tailored to fit any design aesthetic thanks to its wide range of treatments and hues.

Corundum Wear-Resistant Flooring Applications
Materials for corundum floors that resist wear are adaptable and suitable for a range of environments:

1. Industrial Establishments
Flooring is exposed to foot traffic, spillage, and heavy machinery in factories and warehouses. Because corundum flooring is so resilient, it can withstand these kinds of stresses without suffering severe wear, which lowers maintenance expenses over time.

2. Commercial Areas
The visual appeal and longevity of corundum flooring are advantageous in retail settings, dining establishments, and office buildings. Even in places with a lot of activity, its resistance to scuffs and scratches keeps it looking polished.

3. Laboratories and Hospitals
In medical environments, cleanliness is essential. Corundum is a good option for labs and hospitals where hygienic conditions are crucial because of its non-porous surface, which inhibits the growth of microorganisms.

4. Residential Application
More and more homeowners are realizing the advantages of corundum flooring for spaces like basements, entryways, and kitchens. For busy homes, its longevity and ease of upkeep make it a great option.

5. Uses outside
Outdoor use is appropriate for some corundum flooring formulations, which offer a non-slip surface that can endure weather conditions without losing its aesthetic appeal.

The benefits of corundum flooring
There are various benefits to selecting corundum flooring that resists wear:

1. Economical viability
Although the initial outlay may be greater than for more conventional flooring materials, corundum‘s durability and low care requirements can result in cheaper overall expenses over time.

2. Sustainability
Corundum is a more environmentally friendly option than synthetic equivalents, which could need more energy-intensive production methods. Furthermore, because of its endurance, it requires less replacement, which cuts down on waste.

3. Improved Security
In areas where spills are frequent, corundum flooring can be coated to increase safety by preventing slippage. In industrial environments and commercial kitchens, this is especially crucial.

4. Personalization
With a wide range of colors and treatments, corundum flooring may be customized to match any design scheme or aesthetic taste while providing both style and practicality.

The Method of Installation

For best results, corundum flooring installation is usually done by specialists who possess the necessary precision and knowledge. This is a synopsis of the installation procedure:

Preparation: Leveling and cleaning the subfloor are necessary. It could be necessary to remove any old flooring and make repairs in order to guarantee a sturdy base.

Measurement: The quantity of corundum material required is ascertained by taking precise measurements.

Cutting and Fitting: Any necessary corner and edge changes are made to the corundum panels so they fit the designated space.

Application of Adhesive: The subfloor is coated with an appropriate adhesive, and the corundum panels are positioned with care.

Final Touches: To complete the appearance, any finishing touches, such trim or sealing, are applied after the adhesive has dried.

Difficulties and Things to Think About

Despite the numerous advantages of corundum flooring, there are a few things to bear in mind:

Initial Cost: Compared to other flooring options, the initial cost may be higher, which could put off some buyers. But frequently, the long-term savings offset this upfront cost.
Professional Installation: Because of its weight and the level of accuracy needed for installation, it is advised to hire professionals, which raises the total cost.

Minimal Flexibility: People who like to change their interior design often might want to take into account the fact that corundum flooring is difficult to remove or alter once it is put.

A notable development in flooring technology is the use of corundum-resistant floor materials. From industrial settings to private residences, they serve a variety of purposes because to their remarkable hardness, chemical resistance, and aesthetic adaptability. Although the initial outlay could be greater than for more conventional flooring solutions, they are a smart decision in the long run because of their low upkeep and durability.

As industries continue to change and the need for durable and sustainable materials increases, corundum flooring is a particularly innovative option. Corona is set to change the standards for flooring, whether you‘re a homeowner searching for a chic and long-lasting solution or a business owner trying to upgrade your area. With corundum, you can embrace the flooring of the future—where durability and beauty collide.
