Can Bamboo Flooring Be Refinished? A Complete Guide

Posted on 2025-2-17 7:47:51

Bamboo flooring has grown in popularity as a fashionable and environmentally responsible substitute for conventional hardwood floors. Compared to many wood flooring options, it is more sustainable and has a sleek, contemporary appearance. Bamboo is susceptible to deterioration over time, just like any other flooring material. When bamboo flooring is scratched, dented, or faded, homeowners may question if it can be refinished.

The quick answer is that bamboo flooring can be refinished, but before beginning the project, there are a few things to think about. The type of bamboo flooring you have, the degree of damage, and the thickness of the wear layer all affect the refinishing procedure. Everything you need to know about refinishing bamboo flooring will be covered in this tutorial, from determining if your floor is a good fit to the procedures involved.

Recognising the Different Types of Bamboo Flooring


It‘s critical to comprehend the various varieties of bamboo flooring before choosing whether to refinish your bamboo floor:

1. Flooring made of solid bamboo

 Completely composed of bamboo, solid bamboo flooring is typically refinishable several times. How frequently bamboo may be sanded and polished depends on its thickness.

2. Bamboo flooring that has been engineered

 A thin layer of bamboo is attached to a core made of fibreboard or plywood to create engineered bamboo flooring. The bamboo veneer‘s thickness affects refinishing. Sanding can cause irreparable harm to the floor if the veneer is too thin.

3. Strand-Woven Bamboo Flooring

Compressing bamboo fibres with resins creates the hardest kind of bamboo flooring. Its density makes refinishing more difficult, despite its extreme durability. It is possible, but specific equipment and methods are needed.

How to Assess the Refinishability of Your Bamboo Floor

 Not every bamboo floor can be refinished. Here are some important things to think about:

1. The wear layer‘s thickness

 The thickness of the bamboo layer is important for both engineered and solid bamboo floors. It can probably be refinished at least once if it is at least 2-3 mm thick. A thicker wear layer (5mm or more) allows for many refinishing cycles.

2. Damage Amount

 A quick sanding and a fresh coat of finish can frequently fix minor blemishes or surface damage. However, rather than refinishing, a board may need to be replaced due to significant gouges or water damage.

3. Prior Refinishing

 Check the amount of material left on your bamboo floor if it has already been restored once or twice. Over time, excessive sanding may cause the bamboo veneer to erode, revealing the core underneath.

Methods for Refinishing Bamboo Floors

 For optimal effects, take the following actions if you‘ve decided that your bamboo floor can be refinished:

1. Get the space ready

 Take out the room‘s rugs and furniture.

 Dust and debris should be removed from the floor by thoroughly cleaning it.
Look for any nails or staples sticking out, then fix them.

 2. Floor Sanding

 Start with 100–120 grit of fine-grit sandpaper and use an orbital sander or drum sander.

 To avoid scratches, sand along the direction of the bamboo grain.

 Particularly for engineered bamboo flooring with a thin wear layer, refrain from oversanding.

 For corners and edges, use a handheld sander.

 3. Dust Removal

 Sanding dust can be eliminated by vacuuming the entire area.

 To guarantee a clean surface, use a moist microfiber cloth to wipe the floor.

 4. Using a Fresh Finish

 Select a finish that meets your demands, such as hardwax oil for a natural appearance or water-based polyurethane for longevity.

 Use an applicator pad, brush, or roller to apply the finish uniformly.

 Before adding a new layer (usually two to three coats), let the previous layer completely dry.

 5. Give It Time to Heal

 Do not walk on the floor for at least 24 to 48 hours.

 To give the finish time to completely solidify, don‘t move any furniture for a few days.

 How to Care for Bamboo Floors Following Refinishing

 After being refinished, your bamboo floor will continue to look fantastic for many years with the right upkeep:

In order to avoid scratches, use furniture pads.

 Use a damp, not wet, mop to sweep and mop on a regular basis.

 Bamboo is susceptible to water damage, so stay away from excessive dampness.

 To lessen wear, place carpets in high-traffic areas.

 Situations in Which Refinishing May Not Be the Best Choice

 Although worn bamboo flooring can be restored through refinishing, there are some situations in which this may not be the best option:

It might be necessary to repair severely damaged boards with extensive fractures or water damage.

 Engineered bamboo flooring with extremely thin wear layers may not be able to tolerate sanding.

 Because of its density, strand-woven bamboo can be difficult to refinish and calls for specialised knowledge.

 If refinishing isn‘t an option, think about polishing and recoating for light wear or replacing the flooring if there is significant damage.


Refinishing bamboo flooring is frequently possible, albeit the procedure varies depending on the kind of bamboo, how thick it is, and how worn it is. Careful sanding and finishing can prolong the life of your floor and restore its beauty if it is eligible for restoration. However, different methods could be needed for flooring that are badly damaged or engineered bamboo with a thin veneer.

You may appreciate your bamboo floor‘s inherent beauty for many years to come if you use the right methods and take good care of it. Knowing how to refinish it can guarantee the greatest results for your house, whether you decide to do it yourself or hire an expert.
