Important Associated with the Perfect Bamboo Flooring in Your Home

Posted on 2017-2-18 19:31:11

When people think of enhancing the beauty of their homes, the first thing that comes to their mind is bamboo flooring. With the help of this, you will not only be able to develop the looks of the home, but you will also get to add extra flooring. This will enhance the strength of the structure and will not have to worry about damaging the initial floor if you wish to add some structure later on. To ensure that the flooring in the proper manner, you need to know about all the aspects, which are associated with it.


How to care for bamboo flooring


There are no extra points for guessing that bamboo decking will involve a considerable amount of investment. If you are opting for this service, then make sure that you are opting for the best. If you are not careful about this, then all your investments might be in vain. As it has already been mentioned, you need not put too much effort in the maintenance of the bamboo floors. If you follow the points mentioned below then you will be able to increase the beauty of the flooring in a significant manner:


1. Basic cleaning


As bamboo is a natural material, which has rough edges and grooves on the surface, it has the tendency to attract dirt and dust. The shine can be marred if the dust enters and stays in the channels. Thus, it is important that you clean the bamboo flooring from time to time. This is not very complex. All you need to do is clean the surface with a damp piece of cloth that is dipped in a good cleaner. You can also make use of a vacuum cleaner that will be able to suck the dust particles bout of the furrows.


2. Keep it away from heat


As bamboo is composed of natural plant fibers, it will be damaged if it comes in contact with hot objects or liquids. When the surface of the bamboo floor is exposed to heat, the fibers will get charred. Due to this, a dark patch will be formed on the surface, and this spot cannot be removed. It will hamper the glossy look of the flooring.


3. Using wax is not a wise decision


Contrary to popular belief, the bamboo flooring will be damaged if you use any wax. The chemicals present in the waxing materials will react with the natural fibers and eventually spoil the appearance of the deck.


4. Use proper insecticides


There are no extra points for guessing that bamboo is susceptible to the attack by insects. The termites can easily borrow into the bamboo and make it weak from within. This will make the surface less resistant to pressure and will crumble when you place a heavy object on it. Thus, it is necessary that you spray insecticides to keep away termites and other insects, which can eat away into your investment.


5. Use cotton rugs


Many people use rugs made of rubber near the entrance. This is not a wise decision. The main reason behind this is rubber will clog the way and the moisture that gets accumulated on the floor will discolor the bamboo. Thus, it is better to place a cotton rug. This will not only absorb the water and prevent it from affecting the surface but will also trap any dust and dirt from the shoes.


6. Using humidifier is a good decision


Installing a humidifier will prevent the bamboo floor from swelling up or dwindling too much, due to the unprecedented changes in the atmospheric condition. It is better to set the humidity levels at around 40% for best results.


Importance of bamboo flooring


When discussing bamboo flooring, the first point of consideration is the rich yet earthy look that it imparts in the interior or exterior of the home. You will get a sense of staying close to nature. Maintaining the floors, done with bamboo is very easy. With a little bit of money investment, you will be able to increase its longevity, shine, and effectiveness. Another point of consideration is that bamboo flooring is the cheapest way of adding an elegant look to your home or garden.
