Bamboo Flooring: Your best choice to enhance the value of your house

Posted on 2020-11-11 8:00:51

What is Bamboo flooring? 


As compared to the other type of flooring options Bamboo flooring is one of the most justifiable floors for houses that not only offers you great excellence in quality but are hard also. Certain companies made a great quality of bamboo flooring to enhance the value of houses and hotels. If you are looking for sustainability under your budget then bamboo flooring is the finest options for you.


Bamboo flooring can enhance the value of your house great and can be installed by professionals or even by you. This sustainable floor is much easier to clean and take care of. If you are someone who has certain allergies, then choosing any other option rather than bamboo flooring would be waste. Unlike rugs and carpets that collect allergens and dust into it, wood attracts absolutely no dust or allergen in the form of dust. You can sweep and clean bamboo wood easily anytime. The extensive diversity of woods obtainable makes it the best product for your house under a low budget. 


Since they give a stunning and outstanding look to your house, Bamboo floors have a great name in the market of wood. This flooring style adapts any style of decoration. You can dye bamboo at home by yourself in colours. It looks like natural dye and makes your home graceful. You can add any type of furniture to add elegance as per the season or occasion. No matter what is your choice, bamboo flooring always clicks to your mind. You can find diverse varieties in bamboo and ranges that make it perfect for you under any budget from high to low. 


Why choose Bamboo flooring? 


Bamboo Flooring has been one of the topmost choices among people as it only comes under budget but makes the house look expensive and elegant. Bamboo floor manufacturers manufacture it in a way that it offers intrinsic constancy to humidity and insects, eco-friendliness, stability and strength. 


Bamboo is accessible in various types of surfaces, shades and styles. One of the best things about bamboo flooring is that it can glide over an underlay also worked with underfloor heat. It is a dimensionally sturdy flooring type that can be placed in areas where heat and compression change trivially. 


How bamboo floor manufactures? 


It is well-known that bamboo is not technically a wood, it is manufactured by experts into a product that appears like wood. Woven Bamboo is one of the most common methods of manufacturing bamboo flooring. The stubbles of the plant are carved into thin tiles that are ragged into fibres. Strong glue is added to the fibre that is then hard-pressed into dense blocks. The cut-out blocks then again cut into shrill boards. The boards are stained, sanded ended up looks like wood. 


Why Bamboo flooring is known as eco-friendly flooring? 


Bamboo is not exactly a wood but a type of grass that has more durability than wood. Consequently, making it a very renewable supply. Related to the twenty to thirty years, bamboo can be composed in every five years from the forest. Amidst so many qualities, it has one more quality that there is no need to re-plant as it continues to grow through roots and are spread into the soil deeply. 


What are the qualities of bamboo flooring? 


Bamboo flooring is known as the green choice by the people. The essential benefit of bamboo flooring is its naturally friendly nature. Alike another type of wood that fit the ground surface, it is reachable in vast supply. 


Bamboo flooring achieved great development essentially faster as compared to the other type of wood flooring. This infers when woodland is sliced down and does not take much time to reintroduce itself. This recommends bamboo is vicarious a speedily supportable timber or wood source. 


What are the options available in bamboo flooring in the market? 


Bamboo flooring is available in three different types i.e. Strand made, vertical and Horizontal. 


1. Strand Woven Bamboo 

Strands of bamboo are manufactured through intense heat, pressure and weight to make the board of flooring. 


2. Vertical Bamboo 


The layers of bamboo are wedged together plumb to make the board of flooring. 


3. Horizontal Bamboo 

The layers of bamboo are wedged horizontally to make the board of flooring.
