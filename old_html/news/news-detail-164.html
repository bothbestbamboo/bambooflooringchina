TERRACE BAMBOO FLOORING

Posted on 2018-4-23 9:45:17

Are you looking about your floorings and get confused between the bamboo flooring and other hardwoods. We are welcome you to the bamboo flooring facts, here we are providing you the advantage and drawbacks of the terrace bamboo flooring. 


Bamboo is the world fastest growing plants and a renewable resource, as they are not cut down through their roots. In addition to is renewable quality it is hard, strong, light weight, and water resistant, durable (longevity) and gives life time performance. And using bamboo shoot as flooring, paneling and furniture gives a new appearance to your room, living room, stairs, terrace etc. and also acting in the interest of environment. 


Bamboo flooring on terrace is the best option which gives a stylish, smooth, attractive and eco-friendly natural environment. Bamboo flooring on terrace is lifelong durable and since bamboo flooring are treading in market since 10 years but now people are aware towards environment and wants new stylish interior. 


Choosing the right flooring for your room, backyard, terrace, and balcony is very difficult and also so important and people spend thousands of money on other hardwoods, marbles and they forget about more stylish, cost effective, versatile option. The bamboo flooring is the best option to give your home a new look.


Bamboo flooring on your terrace gives your terrace a new adorable and beautiful appearance and a nature friendly environment. What mainly a terrace is used for? Are you spending your summer like a hot castle while walking on terrace in morning and evening for relaxing? Other hardwoods and marbles tiles and floors are not heat resistance and versatile, bamboo is heat resistance and gives you pleasure of nature. Bamboo flooring on terrace gives your garden a natural look if you are a plant lover and love terrace gardening. Bamboo flooring is the awesome idea for giving the full garden appearance. As bamboo flooring is so hard and strong which easily handle the weights of the flower pots and water resistance and give the pleasure of walking on woods.


Some benefits of terrace bamboo flooring:


1. Amazing looking: it gives your terrace a natural look and it makes it so attractive. It is different from other hardwood which is not whether resistant and gives the same shiny look for long time.


2. Life time performance: it is hard and light weight and an amazing whether resistant it is suitable and not damaged in any environment. bamboo woods are survived or resistant to all whether it is heat and water absorbent, and these properties makes it lifelong durable.


3. Nature friendly: as from all other hardwoods bamboo flooring is nature friendly, bamboo grow of its full maturity in 6years while on the other hand other hardwoods takes 20 years to grow up to the maturity. What is most reliable an endangered furniture or the long lasting easily available one. 


4. Hardness: bamboo woods are so harder and easy to handle heavy weights. It is 25% harder than Red Oak and 12% harder than North American Maple.


5. Renewable resources: bamboo woods are renewed and re-used. it is different from plastic coating tiles and rubber tiles which cannot  be used after once damaged.


6. Easy to maintain: bamboo flooring  are easy to maintain they are so tough , these are not so expensive and do not wants regular polishing, bamboo woods may get faded and scratched but they can easily be polished and renewed.


7.Different colors: now day’s people are so conscious about nature and prefer bamboo flooring. They are available in natural bamboo color that is pale yellow and darker wooden color.


Some demerits of bamboo flooring on terrace:


1. Scratches: bamboo floors are easy maintain but it is nearly impossible to maintain it always perfect, since there are certain nasty things which can harm your bamboo floor by giving it scratches on your terrace floorings. 


2. Moisture: as we know that bamboos are water resistant but when the water on floor for more time than it increases moisture since bamboos are also made of natural, organic elements.


3. Low grading system: there is grading system which can grade bamboos quality material for your terrace purposes, since grading is the most important factor before purchasing it.
