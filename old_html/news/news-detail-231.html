Several advantages of bamboo panels and characteristics of bamboo panels

Posted on 2022-6-30 8:22:19

Bamboo panels are usually spliced ??by bamboo strips and compounded with viscose at high temperature. This product has the characteristics of strong stability, non-stick and not easy to deform. Bamboo panels has super insect resistance and durability after removing sugar, acid value, starch and protein. Bamboo made of high-pressure composite materials is wear-resistant, mildew-proof, smooth and soft surface, stable quality, and can be well used in advanced materials such as furniture, crafts, toys, machinery and so on. As a new raw material, bamboo panels has the following advantages and characteristics:


1. Due to the influence of the specific soil, climate, photosynthetic conditions and hilly landforms of moso bamboo, the density of bamboo sheets determines the tensile and compressive properties of moso bamboo. The tensile strength and compressive strength of bamboo are much higher than those of ordinary wood. Compared with solid wood composite flooring, the tensile strength and compressive strength of bamboo panelss are particularly good, and the surface smoothness is softer and more comfortable.


2. the color is bright, the bamboo grains are clear, and each process link pays attention to the preservation of the original bamboo quality. The texture of the product is particularly bright, the natural bamboo particles are very clear, and the overall effect after paving is particularly obvious and different. The color difference of ordinary bamboo panels is smaller than that of wood. Compared with any solid wood panels, the uniform color of bamboo panels is difficult to compare.


3. Green and environmentally friendly, fresh and elegant bamboo is the green building material of nature. The bamboo panels completely maintains the original taste of natural bamboo, so that consumers can feel the simplicity, freshness and elegance of the home environment they create. This effect is difficult for any plank. Bamboo panelss are made of high-grade adhesive imported Taier glue, which conforms to the national E1 standard and is non-toxic and harmless.


4. Fast heat transfer, cool bamboo in winter and summer has the characteristics of fast heat absorption and heat release, and its temperature changes rapidly with the change of the environment. In the hot summer, the bamboo floor made of bamboo sheets makes people feel cool; in the cold winter, the bamboo floor heats up quickly with heating.


5. After high temperature and high pressure carbonation treatment, bamboo is treated with high temperature and high pressure to remove sugar and fat and destroy protein. The worms cannot survive and reproduce, do not produce quality problems of worm wort, and have good moisture-proof and mildew-proof properties.


6. Adopt advanced drying technology to process moso bamboo to produce non-deformed and cracked bamboo pieces, and the moisture content is evenly controlled between 8 and 12%. A combination of bamboo panelss was formed, and the plan was optimized through scientific pressure experiments. Under the condition of strict removal of green and yellowing, high-efficiency adhesive is used, and after high temperature and high pressure bonding, the internal stress balance of the slab can be achieved under different temperatures and humidity. Therefore, the bamboo panels will not deform and crack. Especially suitable for dry climate areas.
