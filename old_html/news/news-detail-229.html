How to choose household bamboo plywood

Posted on 2022-6-28 10:29:23

In the field of contemporary interior decoration, bamboo panels are more and more popular. This material can kill insects, sterilize, detoxify and resist corrosion. For contemporary people who advocate nature and pursue environmentally friendly consumption, furniture is the best choice.


Bamboo plywood is also called bamboo furniture plywood or bamboo laminate. The surface is beautiful, the bamboo pattern is fresh, elegant and pleasant, with a unique classical style. Due to its high fragmentation, elasticity and toughness, bamboo strips can be divided into side-pressed bamboo strips, industrial bamboo strips, vertical and horizontal bamboo strips, single-pressed bamboo strips, flat bamboo strips, and multi-layered bamboo strips. According to the production process, it can be divided into natural bamboo plywood, zebra bamboo plywood and charcoal bamboo plywood. What are the advantages and disadvantages of these natural, pure and popular materials in everyday use?


First, the advantages of bamboo plywood


1. Bamboo plywood has strong durability and wear resistance. Influenced by soil and geography, bamboo is denser than solid wood, and its fiber density is more resistant to tensile and compressive properties. It‘s also tough. The surface feel is also very smooth, soft and comfortable.


2. The bamboo plywood adopts high-quality special technology to prevent deformation and cracking. In the production process, the bamboo plywood adopts advanced drying technology, the moisture content is controlled at about 814%, and the high-temperature press adopts high-efficiency adhesive. Therefore, even if it is used for a long time, the bamboo plywood will not easily crack and deform at ambient temperature. Especially in the arid north.


3. Bamboo plywood optimizes the living environment, warm in winter and cool in summer. Bamboo plates dissipate heat quickly and have strong thermal conductivity. It may vary with ambient temperature. In the hot summer, the bamboo plywood can make people feel cool; in the cold winter, the bamboo plywood can lock the temperature perfectly. So it‘s warm in winter and cool in summer.


4. Bamboo plywood is healthy and green, natural and environmentally friendly. As a pure natural plant, bamboo is a green material, which can not only maintain the original characteristics of bamboo perfectly, but also create a simple, fresh and elegant home environment for consumers. In addition, its special adhesive is also an environmentally friendly material that meets the national e1 standard, and residents do not need to worry about being damaged by the toxic substances it contains.


5. The bamboo sheet has good aesthetic effect and clear texture. The processing and extraction process of bamboo plywoods pays attention to the preservation of the original quality. The finished product is bright and bright, the bamboo pattern is clear, the color is symmetrical, and the whole pavement effect is fresh and elegant.


2. Specifications of bamboo sheets


At present, there are no specific specifications for bamboo pieces, but the general length is controlled at 255 cm, the width is 125 cm, and the thickness is between 0.2 and 0.8 cm. The general bamboo pieces are 220 cm x 60 cm, 200 cm x 100 cm and 200 cm x 60 cm. Everyone can customize bamboo furniture according to their needs.
