What are the production process of outdoor bamboo and wood flooring

Posted on 2022-5-27 8:13:43

There are many manufacturing techniques for floors, but there are certain operating steps in the production. In fact, everyone should understand that outdoor bamboo flooring is a special wood-based panel, which has higher processing precision and aesthetics than other wood-based panels. Therefore, the technological process usually adopted in the production process is relatively strict. So, what is the production process of outdoor bamboo and wood flooring manufacturers?


What is the production process of outdoor bamboo and wood flooring manufacturers


1. Cut off the skewed end formed by the root of the original bamboo to form a flat mouth.


2. Using the port as the positioning surface, cut a certain length of bamboo tube from the root to the tip.


3. When encountering parts with large curvature, they should be twisted into short bamboo tubes. The crack part of the original bamboo should be cut according to the length of the crack, so as not to affect the use of bamboo in other parts.


4. The cutting allowance should be reasonably reserved when cutting. Generally, if the length of the finished flooring product is 910mm, the cut-off length should be 950mm. If the length of the finished floor is 610mm, the cut-off length should be 650mm.


In fact, before the production of outdoor bamboo and wood flooring manufacturers, for the selection of raw materials, the cylindrical bamboo materials are processed into bamboo pieces of equal width, etc., and then follow-up production is carried out according to the general wood processing method. This is completely different from bamboo flattening or bamboo curtain weaving of bamboo plywood. Select fresh, four-year-old or older original bamboo to be cut into a bamboo tube according to a preset length by a cutting machine, commonly known as cutting.


In addition, the original bamboo used for the production of outdoor bamboo floors is usually 7m to 9m long, the eyebrow circumference is more than 300mm, and the weight is 25kg?/root to 35kg?/root. The thickness of the bamboo wall of the original bamboo is . Sharp, curved and shipping. The degree of damage in the process varies. Therefore, when cutting, attention should be paid to the selection of materials to improve the utilization rate of bamboo and reduce production costs.


The bamboo tube cut by the cutter is sawed with a coaxial double saw blade opener to obtain bamboo pieces of equal length. The width of the bamboo is determined by the spacing between the saw blades, which can be adjusted as needed. Generally speaking, the wider the width of the bamboo, the greater the amount of cutting when shaving bamboo green and bamboo yellow. The utilization rate of bamboo is low. However, if the bamboo pieces are too narrow, the loss of the road will increase.


There are many aspects to pay attention to, for example, the bamboo pieces after sawing the bamboo tube are actually part of the circular arc of the bamboo tube. In order to facilitate subsequent processing, the outdoor bamboo floor must remove the bamboo yellow and bamboo yellow on both sides of the bamboo pieces, and shave it into rectangular section bamboo pieces. Because the green tissue of bamboo is dense and tough, the surface is also covered with a layer of wax. Bamboo yellow vascular bundles are sparse. If the bamboo green and bamboo yellow on the surface and middle layers are not removed, it is difficult for chemicals to penetrate into bleaching and bleaching.
