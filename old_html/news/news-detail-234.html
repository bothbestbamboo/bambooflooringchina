A Guide: Getting Bamboo Flooring For Your House

Posted on 2023-1-30 8:01:55

Why would one not want their house to be looking more attractive than any other‘s, right! You must use the appropriate material to make it look good. There are many certain ways that you can decorate your house. A properly designed house consists of many things such as Fancy ceilings, well-designed furniture, a suitable color combination, and good flooring.


As we are talking about flooring, there are many types of flooring done for making your home look attractive, and ‘Bamboo Flooring is one of the ways. There are many types of bamboo flooring as well. Below are the benefits of using bamboo flooring and their classification based on different things. So let us check them all.


Benefits Of Bamboo Flooring


Nowadays, interior design is a thing that is given the most value while building new houses. As we know, Wood is the main material cut down and shaped to make the furniture and give our house a modish look. On the other hand, bamboo flooring is very trendy and is used the most in the interior design of houses, offices, hotels, etc. They give our property a luxurious look.


Easier In Taking Care


You can maintain your bamboo flooring by normal sweeping, vacuuming, and moping. There is no such difference between bamboo floors and other types of floorings. If your bamboo flooring gets damaged, then the restoration process is very simple as it just includes being sanded down of the floor and refurbished, and the sellers can replace your old flooring with the new one.


Environment-Friendly


As we all know, where Bamboo comes from, Nature. Bamboo grows faster than the other types of trees, which are used for flooring properties and are a renewable resource. Bamboo takes around 2 - 5 years to grow fully, whereas the other trees used for flooring can take 10 - 20 to be fully grown. As it originates from Nature, it could barely harm Nature.


Types Of Bamboo Flooring


Bamboo flooring is available in many types and your decision making will surely depend on color, texture as well as grain type. Other factors that you need to keep in mind is cost and convenience.  Continue reading to learn more about bamboo flooring types.


Vertical Bamboo Flooring


This type of bamboo flooring produces vertical lines of the material and gives a modern look and feel. This type of flooring is made by using fusing stalks and in a vertical pattern.


Horizontal Bamboo Flooring


It is a type of bamboo flooring in which planks of Bamboo are placed next to one other horizontally. These are kept together with the help of adhesive before finishing them, and they randomly display their knuckles, growth of the rings, or the stalk.


Strand woven Bamboo


These bamboos are made up of the remaining strands of the stalk, which are trimmed from the long bamboo fillets. The manufacturers do compress them together with the help of any adhesive before finishing. Strand woven bamboos are two times harder than vertical or horizontal bamboo flooring.


Flooring Classification By Appearance


Bamboo flooring can be classified by appearance as they are of different colors. so here are some of the patterns, shapes, and colors from which you can easily choose to decorate your homes:


Natural Color


Natural color is the actual color of Bamboo that has got no added colors or stains. The natural bamboo flooring is less expensive than the others and is easy to find. Natural color is a very light or, we can say, a blonde color to look at. It goes with every type of decor. If you are interested in decorating your house eco-friendly, you can go for natural color bamboo flooring.


Tiger / Tigerized Pattern


It is made up of strand-woven Bamboo, which gives it a unique look. It has a pattern of different colors of strings and is named "The Tiger Pattern." Tiger bamboo flooring patterns are mostly used when the property owner wants to give their project a forest look or maybe a look that gives you the vibes of being surrounded by Nature.

Direct Paint

If you want to create your design or color, including the benefits of having bamboo flooring, you can go for direct painting on the floor. Many types of Textures are available to give your flooring a look according to you. You can also customize the paint and the shade of your floor with your choice. Printed bamboo flooring is treated with pressure and sealed to print the same designs seen on other hardwoods.
