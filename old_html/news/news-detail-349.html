Lumber Liquidators Flooring Reviews: Exploring Wood, Bamboo, and Engineered Options

Posted on 2024-12-26 7:53:51

One of the most well-known brands in the flooring solutions sector is Lumber Liquidators, which has since changed its name to LL Flooring. The company has established a solid name among interior designers, contractors, and homeowners thanks to its extensive range of reasonably priced and long-lasting flooring solutions. Focussing on wood, bamboo, and engineered flooring, LL Flooring provides a range of goods to suit different tastes, price ranges, and home remodelling requirements. The company‘s history, product line, and advantages of selecting their flooring solutions will all be covered in this article.

An Overview of Lumber Liquidators‘ Past


Tom Sullivan, a building contractor who spotted a chance to offer premium flooring at affordable costs, started Lumber Liquidators in 1993. At first, the business concentrated on purchasing excess hardwood flooring straight from mills and offering it to clients at a discount. Lumber Liquidators became one of the biggest speciality flooring merchants in North America by growing its product line and forming alliances with manufacturers over time.

The business changed its name to LL Flooring in 2020 to better represent its changing clientele and range of products. In addition to running more than 400 physical stores around the United States, LL Flooring provides a comprehensive online shopping experience. Millions of households are drawn to the company‘s pledge to offer "the highest quality flooring at the lowest possible prices."

Flooring Types That LL Flooring Offers

 The wide selection of flooring alternatives offered by LL Flooring is one of the main factors contributing to its popularity. LL Flooring offers a variety of flooring options to meet your needs, including eco-friendly bamboo, traditional hardwood, and adaptable engineered wood. Let‘s examine these three well-liked categories in more detail:

1. Flooring made of hardwood

 One of LL Flooring‘s main offerings is hardwood flooring. To fit their tastes in interior design, customers can select from a range of wood species, finishes, and plank sizes. Oak, maple, hickory, and exotic woods like Brazilian cherry are popular choices.

Advantages of Hardwood Flooring Options for LL:

large range of grains and colours to match any type of design.

 Strong and long-lasting, perfect for places with lots of traffic.

 Its longevity can be increased by refinishing it several times.

 enhances the elegance and worth of your house.

 Additionally, LL Flooring has prefinished and solid hardwood alternatives, which facilitate quicker and easier installation for both professional contractors and do-it-yourselfers.

2. Flooring made of bamboo

 The bamboo line from LL Flooring is a fantastic alternative for anyone looking for an environmentally responsible and sustainable flooring option. Though technically a grass, bamboo is a popular substitute for many traditional hardwoods in modern dwellings since it is tougher.

Bamboo flooring types that are available include:

Strand Bamboo: Extremely resilient and long-lasting.

 Horizontal Bamboo: Provides a distinctive look by showcasing the natural bamboo grain.

 Vertical Bamboo: Has a consistent, tidy look.

 Advantages of Bamboo Flooring Options for LL:

environmentally beneficial because bamboo grows quickly.

 Extremely strong and impervious to scratches and dents.

 For humid locations, there are choices that are resistant to moisture.

 less expensive than many hardwoods.

 Homeowners have more design options thanks to LL Flooring‘s bamboo products, which come in a variety of finishes and colours, from deep, dark stains to natural light tones.

3. Wood flooring that has been engineered

 Engineered wood flooring is a cutting-edge choice that adds structural stability while preserving the beauty of hardwood. Real hardwood veneer is laminated over a high-density plywood core in LL Flooring‘s engineered products, giving them the appearance and feel of solid wood but with increased resistance to moisture and temperature changes.

Advantages of Engineered Wood Flooring from LL:

Perfect for higher-humidity environments and basements.

 Because of click-and-lock mechanisms, it is simpler to install than conventional hardwood.

 available in a range of board widths, wood species, and finishes.

 less expensive than real hardwood floors.

 Where traditional hardwood might not be appropriate, engineered wood is a flexible option that can be laid over concrete, radiant heating systems, and other surfaces.

Why Opt for LL Flooring?

 LL Flooring has established a solid reputation for providing premium flooring at affordable costs. The following are some explanations for why contractors and homeowners select LL Flooring:

1. Reasonably priced
By purchasing goods straight from producers and mills, LL Flooring cuts out the intermediary and is able to provide competitive prices. This makes high-end flooring more affordable for those on a tight budget.

2. A Vast Range
LL Flooring offers hundreds of types, colours, and finishes to suit a wide variety of preferences and requirements. There are solutions that will work for you whether you‘re constructing a clean modern area or a rustic farmhouse.

3. Sustainability
LL Flooring is dedicated to ethical sourcing methods. Many of their items are environmentally beneficial choices that support sustainable living, such bamboo and other synthetic woods.

4. Customer Service
Customers may access a wealth of materials from the company, such as installation instructions, design ideas, and online or in-store consultations. Their customer service representatives are renowned for their expertise and helpfulness.

5. Sturdiness and Excellence
Because LL Flooring‘s products are made to resist normal wear and tear, families and pet owners can trust them. To give customers peace of mind, many of their products are covered by warranties.

Reviews and comments from customers

 Although LL Flooring has been commended for its selection, cost, and customer support, some reviews cite issues with product consistency and installation difficulties. Before making a purchase, it‘s crucial to carefully go over the product specifications and warranty information. Better outcomes can also be guaranteed by working with a skilled installation, particularly for intricate flooring choices like bamboo and engineered wood.

All things considered, LL Flooring receives great ratings for striking a balance between affordability and quality. The company is a popular choice for flooring projects of all sizes because of its openness and abundance of options, which are valued by many consumers.

Flooring is one of the most important design elements, regardless of whether you‘re building from scratch or remodelling your existing house. A wide range of wood, bamboo, and engineered flooring solutions are available from LL Flooring to improve the appearance and feel of any area. LL Flooring is still a well-known brand in the sector thanks to its solid reputation, affordable prices, and dedication to quality.

For your next project, LL Flooring can provide the ideal flooring option if you‘re searching for something strong, fashionable, and reasonably priced. Spend some time looking through their selections to find flooring that suits your demands and style.
