Fact about Bamboo Flooring for Consumers

Posted on 2020-11-2 8:38:46

Ever since the Bamboo flooring has come into existence, it has acquired a lot of attention as an alternative to the hardwood flooring. It is now been used in residential as well as in commercial properties. A bamboo flooring is a man-made creation with the use of a bamboo plant. This plant is a kind of a grass that originated from China. Bamboo flooring being a natural product can create a graceful as well as a recent appearance to your apartment or commercial space. 


Bamboo Flooring Constructions  


There are many ways to change bamboo grass plant into bamboo flooring planks that can be all set for an installation. Products that are created through engineering process has a detailed appearances. You will be able to choose the right type of bamboo for your venture if you are able to identify the options that are available in the market. 


In the Eastern Asia, the traditional flooring was prepared by carving the stems in the form of thin sheets, then fixing it to a hardwood beams. In certain rural zones, this type of flooring is still been used. The modified form of flooring mainly has two common categories – Solid bamboo flooring in which solid parts of bamboo are compressed and joined together to form floor board. While engineering bamboo flooring contains comparatively thinner external layer that are joined to a wood or a high-density fiberboard surface layer.


Types of profiles in Bamboo Flooring


A bamboo flooring has mainly two types of profiles or fitting method: 


Tongue and Groove – This is a Traditional bamboo flooring fitting method where the tongue is installed firmly into the groove in such a way that a slight opening is left among the flooring planks. 


Click Fitting Method – A click fitting method make use a uniclic lock-up system. Here the sheets can be easily installed together in a simple click and lock way.  


Colors and finishes of Bamboo Flooring. 


Bamboo flooring may be available in different finishes. Flooring finish is mainly the way the surface appears and displays an impression. The bamboo flooring colours is decided either by blemishing or carbonizing at the time of production. The bamboo colour is light yellow are the natural ones while a carbonized bamboo flooring is formed by burning in-order to make a light and dark brown colour. The method carried out in protecting the bamboo flooring from day to day damage is known as finish. Bamboo flooring of all kind come’s pre-finished thus can be used once it’s been installed.  


The Price Range of Bamboo Flooring


Bamboo flooring can be available at a cost that is much economy to that of hardwood flooring. The reason being the raw material is highly reusable, which makes the bamboo far economical than that of a hard wood. The value of the bamboo flooring may vary subject to the production of the planks. For instance, strand woven bamboo flooring is a shrill parting from the traditional horizontal as well as vertical bamboo flooring. This is a long-lasting bamboo flooring, thus its slightly pricier than the natural flooring. 


Bamboo Flooring Maintenance 


It is very simple to take care and maintain bamboo flooring. Here are few tips you can follow to have a vibrant looking bamboo flooring.


You must either broom or vacuum your bamboo floor on a daily basis to avoid marks.


It is recommended to make use of doormat at the main entrance of the door to avoid dirts, dusts from outside entering the bamboo floor.


Never make use of any acidic product to clean the bamboo floors; this may cause damage to the flooring. 


On a weekly basis you must clean your bamboo flooring by using a good wood floor cleaner. 


Conclusion


By now you might be aware about the benefits of bamboo flooring. There are various benefits of bamboo flooring and most importantly it is resistant to any form of dents and scratches. It is not going to mean that impervious as well. But be careful when you go on to choose one as there are a lot of companies in the market. Each of them might promise to give you the best of deals but opt for the best one.
