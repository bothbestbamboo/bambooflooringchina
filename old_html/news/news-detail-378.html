Brazilian Cherry Solid Wood Flooring: A Stunning Choice for Interior Spaces

Posted on 2025-2-6 7:41:01

One of the most popular hardwood species for solid wood flooring is Brazilian Cherry, sometimes referred to as Jatoba. This exotic hardwood is well-known for its vibrant colour, strength, and resilience to wear, making it a popular option for businesses and homeowners wishing to install high-end flooring in offices, rooms, and other interior areas.

Introducing the Jatoba, or Brazilian cherry.


Hymenaea courbaril, the scientific name for Brazilian cherry, is a hardwood species that is indigenous to Central and South America, having a notable concentration in Brazil. Since the tree is unrelated to actual cherry species, the term "Brazilian Cherry" is a little misleading. But it got its name because of its rich reddish-brown colour, which is similar to cherry wood.

The light orange-brown colour of freshly milled Brazilian cherry gradually darkens into a rich, deep reddish-brown, especially when exposed to natural light. The wood‘s appeal is increased by this colour change, which makes it a favourite among flooring experts and interior designers.

Brazilian cherry is known for its remarkable firmness in addition to its beautiful beauty. It is much harder than conventional domestic hardwoods like oak and maple, with a Janka hardness rating of about 2,350 lbf (pounds-force). It is the perfect flooring choice for high-traffic areas because of its remarkable hardness, which makes it extremely resistant to dents, scratches, and normal wear.

Origin Nation and Sustainability

 Brazil‘s tropical forests are the main source of Brazilian cherry harvests. For many years, it has been a mainstay in the hardwood flooring industry due to its natural abundance. However, in order to guarantee environmental sustainability, ethical procurement is essential.

Numerous trustworthy vendors source Brazilian Cherry hardwood from forests that follow certifications such as the Forest Stewardship Council (FSC) and are managed using sustainable procedures. These certifications aid in guaranteeing that the wood is collected with the least possible negative impact on the environment and in a manner that promotes forest regeneration. To encourage responsible forestry, buyers should always seek out Brazilian cherry flooring from sources that are FSC-certified or otherwise recognised.

Suggested Installation Techniques

 For Brazilian Cherry solid wood flooring to last and function well, proper installation is necessary. Professional installation is strongly advised due to the hardwood‘s density and relative stability. The following are the most effective techniques for interior installations:

1. Nail-Down Installation

 The most popular technique for solid hardwood flooring is this one. Using staples or nails, planks are firmly attached to a hardwood subfloor. Both residential and commercial areas can benefit from this technique‘s solid and long-lasting surface.

2. Installation via Glue-Down

 When installing over concrete subfloors, the wood planks are bonded directly to the surface using a premium glue. This approach is favoured for office environments and multi-story structures because it reduces mobility and offers sound insulation.

3. Adjustment Prior to Installation

 Brazilian cherries react strongly to variations in temperature and humidity. The wood needs to be acclimated to the indoor atmosphere for at least 7–14 days prior to installation in order to avoid excessive expansion or contraction. By taking this step, stability is guaranteed and problems like gapping or cupping are avoided.

Continual Upkeep for Durable Beauty

 Brazilian cherry flooring is very easy to maintain, but in order to maintain its durability and beauty, it is imperative that the right care instructions be followed. Here are some important upkeep pointers:

Frequent Cleaning

 Regularly vacuum or sweep to get rid of dust and debris that might scratch surfaces.

 To clean the surface, use a damp cloth (not wet) and a microfiber mop. Steer clear of too much dampness since it can harm the wood.

 Make use of a hardwood floor cleaner made specifically for solid wood floors that is pH-neutral. Steer clear of strong chemicals and cleaners that contain wax.

 Avoiding Dents and Scratches

 To avoid scratching, place felt pads underneath furniture legs.

 To prevent wear on the floor, place area rugs or mats in high-traffic areas.
Steer clear of heavy shoes or high heels that could scratch the floor.

 Controlling Climate and Humidity

 To avoid too much wood movement, keep interior humidity levels between 35% and 55%.

 To maintain stability, use a humidifier in arid climes and a dehumidifier in humid ones.

Brazilian cherry solid wood flooring is still a classic option for interior areas because it offers unparalleled character, beauty, and longevity. Its deep reddish-brown hues and durability make it a remarkable choice whether it is installed in a professional workplace, a comfortable living room, or any other indoor space. For many years to come, businesses and households can appreciate the beauty of Brazilian Cherry flooring by selecting wood that is obtained responsibly, installing the floor correctly, and taking good care of it.
