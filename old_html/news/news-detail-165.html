Kitchen Bamboo Flooring

Posted on 2018-8-14 14:47:14

Bamboo is a type of grass which is like strong wooden sticks. Bamboo is a natural binding substance that has many of the properties of hardwood floorings.  Bamboo is very useful for the flooring purposes but there are some drawbacks also. Bamboos are some of the fastest rising plants all around the world.  Bamboo floorings are very different from the other types of floorings. Bamboo floorings give you a different texture on your floors with the natural beauty.  It is one of the latest trends in interior designing. Bamboo flooring is a graceful and nature friendly substitute to classical hardwood floors. Bamboo flooring can easily change your interior from old, boring to stylish and attractive. 


Kitchen is the important hub of your home, where you cook food for your family and gather parties. And what gives your kitchen a new look. The hardwoods never go out of fashion and the trend of cutting bamboo and use it in your kitchen floorings is hot in market nowadays. Bamboo flooring gives your kitchen a different texture and a natural beauty to your kitchen floorings as bamboo are the fastest growing plant and has a hard strong wood which is suitable for the kitchen flooring and makes your kitchen a eco-friendly kitchen. 


Think what gives your kitchen more attractiveness, the bamboo flooring which gives you the feeling of nature like making delicious food around trees and   the bamboo flooring gives you that pleasure. So we are providing the information for your kitchen flooring. As kitchen bamboo flooring gives you a new look to your kitchen and it is cost effective ,long lasting, durable, moisture resistant, easy to clean, eco-friendly ,insect resistance, Rapid renewability, cost effective.


Benefits of bamboo flooring in kitchen:


Stylish: bamboo gives you a new natural look to your kitchen a hard wooden floor different from all the normal tiles or marble.


Durability: bamboos are quite very durable since there are some types of bamboos that can be tremendously strong, and hard. Bamboos which are properly harvested and un-carbonized can be as durable as red oak and strand woven bamboos are quite harder than other, so dining-room bamboo flooring can be very impressive by their hardness and finishing.


Easy clean: They are easy to handle, through a wet piece of cloth they can easily be cleaned and gives shine as new flooring.


Eco friendly: Bamboo flooring is made up of renewable resources, from bamboo plants which can be cut not from the root so they easily grow after cutting down there harder wood and can use long lasting.


Variety: Bamboo flooring for kitchen are available in different natural color which is pale yellow. 


Water resistant: Bamboos are used as water harvesting from the very ancient time as they are more resistance to water damages.


Renewability: As the bamboo floorings are the natural renewable resource they may be faded after some long time but that doesn‘t mean that they are useless they can be polished easily and renewed.


Price: bamboo floor can easily be found at any wooden store at the equal price of the most hardwood floors be found. You can found bamboo floorings from about $2-$8 per square feet and you can use it for you kitchen floorings. 


Some demerits of bamboo floorings in kitchen:


Scratch: As compare to marbles and other tiles bamboo are not so compatible after some use the bamboo floors are full of scratches. And if you have a pet in your home bamboo kitchen flooring is not a good option.


Production of VOC:  bamboo floor planks are created by wedging, cut into strips, of bamboo grass plants and then give them heat to adding the pieces together with pressure and resin-based adhesive which is made of some non organic chemicals which can harm air of interior place over the time.


No so tough or harder: As the bamboo stick is so harder but no good enough to put heavy utensils on it.


Moisture: As bamboos are natural they grow by using minerals in soil and water and on the same time they are water resistant but moisture is always present in it.
