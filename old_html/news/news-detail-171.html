5 Solid Reasons Why You Should Opt for Bamboo Flooring in Future

Posted on 2020-7-30 8:23:58

The floor is one of the many parts of the house we are in contact with every day: every time we choose how to make the floor of the house we expect to select something that is not only beautiful to look at but also pleasant to the touch. Bamboo flooring is not only a pleasant design floor, but it also has a natural luster superior to the classic wooden parquet. This is just one of the points in favor of this material: to find out more, keep reading this article.


Below we will talk about the main characteristics of bamboo flooring, and we will see what the main types compared are.

Bamboo parquet: characteristic features.


Before going into the different types of bamboo flooring offered by the market, let us see together what are the characteristics that have made this material so popular! First of all, you have to know that bamboo is an eco-sustainable material: the bamboo plant, in fact, has much faster growth and maturation time than the traditional wood used for parquet.


To be exact, it can be used already after 3 or 5 years, against the 20 needed for a normal tree to reach maturity. This, therefore, makes it possible to help the planet maintain healthy forests: clearly, this also depends on the behavior of the producing companies and unfortunately not all behave responsibly.


For this reason, we advise you always to contact only companies with certified products and materials. 


Another point in favor of bamboo flooring is its hardness and strength: compared to oak wood it is 30% harder.


Bamboo is also ideal for renovations since it is distributed already painted and ready for installation: in this way, you will avoid running risks associated with the evaporation of sanding and painting which instead still remain valid for classic parquet.


With all the flooring options available to us today, finding the right floor for you can be a challenge, so much so that it is easy to overlook options that would be perfect for us, such as bamboo floorings. If you‘ve ever wondered why you should also consider bamboo flooring for your home, here is a list of 5 good reasons. We hope they can convince you to choose bamboo!


1. Durability


You probably would never think of it, but bamboo is one of the most durable floorings and furniture options. It lasts as long as many types of parquet, sometimes even more. This depends on the quality of the bamboo used; the low-quality one will not last as long as the high-quality one. Therefore make sure that the bamboo used is of the best quality available.


2. Maintenance


A bamboo flooring is exceptionally easy to maintain. Daily cleaning will only require a simple rag without the need for special treatments, which is perfectly suited to busy people. Over time, if the floor shows scratches, nicks or bleaching, you can feel comfortable because the bamboo floor can be easily finished. Simply smooth the floor and reapply a top coat and voila! Like New!


The benefit of bamboo is that it is much more resistant to water damage, stains, and deformations than any other parquet.


3. Cost


Bamboo flooring is not as expensive as many might think. Since bamboo is delivered pre-finished and possibly pre-colored, you will not have to pay for sanding and polishing after the floor has been installed. This makes the bamboo a bit cheaper than other hardwood floors.


4. Visual impact


The essential thing to be considered while choosing a floor is undeniably the "how it will look". A bamboo floor is beautiful and elegant and is appreciated by many people, especially fans of a modern and clean aesthetic. It is very similar to a traditional parquet, but if you look closely, you will notice irrefutable differences.


5. Environmental impact


In today global environmentally conscious context, one of the things that often comes to mind when it comes to renovating a building or home is "how can we make our living space more eco-friendly?" If you are among these people, bamboo flooring is a solid option. While for normal hardwoods a minimum of 10 years is required to reach maturity, bamboo takes much less time, between 4 and 7 years, making it a sustainable choice for those who, like you, want to use natural material for their floor.


So the next time you or someone you know is thinking about buying a floor, we hope bamboo is the first thing that comes to mind. You will not regret it.
