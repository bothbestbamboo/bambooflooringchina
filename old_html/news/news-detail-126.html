China Produced Bamboo Flooring: New Way to Design Your House

Posted on 2017-4-24 8:29:30

Everyone wants to decorate their homes. There are many things in the market available to decorate homes. These items are the ones that have been made to beautify the home with the most elegant style. But in the recent day, the China bamboo flooring is the one that has been striding the market. The designing of the floorings are made in such a way that it attracts attention. The most beautiful part of this type of floorings is the creativity that has been made with style and dedication by the builders.


It is a fact that the China bamboo flooring is not the new type of flooring in the market. These types of floorings and designing of home materials have been introduced in the market of china through thorough research. Afterward, in the 1980s, the product has been launched for the people to design their home with this beautiful product. In the initial stage, the research could not take place in the way it has been considered too. There are several factors that have blocked the research to make its way. But all these problems at one point of time have been demolished and the most relevant product has been produced for the people.


The most beautiful things of the product are that it gives an ambiance to the room that has never been felt. Bamboo has been one of the beautiful products that have been used in the textile company. But in the recent day, it has been found that the designing of the China bamboo flooring perfectly matches the criteria of what it has been made for. The contemporary style, the most beautiful traditional finishing has made it one of the desires for many people to have it in their homes.


The rise of the bamboo floorings


The Chinese industrialists have marketed the product in such a way that it has now become one of the top most brands of house designing in a global way. From the year 2000, the product has been recognized by many global housing development brands. The main reason for this is the affordable costs that can be afforded by every single individual.


 China is rich in bamboo source and ranks top 1 in the world market. About a large portion of the china provinces have an abundance of the bamboo resource. The Mao bamboo is one of the most suitable bamboo materials for the purpose of flooring. Maximum of Mao bamboo resource is located within China. At present, there are nearly 200 China bamboo flooring developers that are mostly situated in the southern part of China. There is different kinds of flooring for example stained type, the strand is woven type etc. The flooring developed from there has been exported to various countries all over the world that include Brazil, USA, UK, France, Japan, Ireland and much more.


A look at the history


The history of China bamboo flooring dates back to a shorter period of time and can be recalled to about just 20 years ago. However, people must take into consideration that the materials and labor for the production of this type of flooring are very expensive. The profit is, however, lower than previous times. Various factors have affected the China bamboo flooring business in the past such as the financial crisis that had taken place in the year 2008.


There are various problems that are experienced by the market catering to this kind of product and they have affected their sale to a large extent. However, they serve as the perfect alternative for solid wood flooring owing to their environmental safety and lower price. It has a lower market share when compared to other kinds of flooring options. There is, however, a huge potential for the development of this kind of flooring option.


The constructed bamboo floors from the manufacturers are anticipated to be resistant to scratching and harsh weather conditions. This way, they will ensure that they run for a longer period of time and hence be used for a longer period of time without any sort of difficulty or problem. The processing techniques as well as a stain that are used in the whole construction process are of supreme quality and will ensure customers that they get the most suitable product that will change the look of their house.
