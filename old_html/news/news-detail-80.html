Four Strong Reasons To Buy Bamboo Flooring

Posted on 2016-6-12 20:12:06

Nowadays in the flooring market, bamboo flooring can be considered as the newly-developing flooring, in fact, more and more consumers are interested in it because of its unique features, however, lots of customers are still hesitating whether it is right to buy bamboo flooring. Don’t worry, in the following article, four strong reasons will be given to you to decorate your home or office with bamboo flooring.


Firstly, better stability than solid wooden flooring


In the past, solid wooden flooring would be people’s first choice when selecting flooring for decoration because of natural raw material. I think it will become history now because bamboo flooring has better stability than solid wooden flooring, for example, both the shrinking percentage and expansion rate of bamboo flooring is relatively limited.


Some people would have such standpoint that bamboo flooring is easy to be damaged by worms because bamboo grow under moist environment and lots of worms live in bamboo. This is the fact in the past, but please neglect this shortcoming now because advanced manufacturing technique is used during bamboo flooring manufacture to overcome such weakness.


Compared with solid wooden flooring, bamboo flooring has higher hardness, therefore it is durable.


Secondly, people will feel more comfortable when walking on bamboo flooring


When introducing the main advantage of bamboo flooring, I am sure salesman would tell you that it is warm in cold winter while cool in hot summer when using bamboo flooring for decoration at home.


As is know to you, bamboo itself has lower heat conductivity coefficient so that no matter the season, its temperature is constant. When walking on flooring without wearing shoes, people would feel more comfortable.


Thirdly, pure and fresh color of bamboo flooring


Just from the aesthetic feeling aspect, bamboo flooring is so distinctive on both its flower color and texture, at the same time, when looking at its texture, you will notice it is so unique.


As for laminate flooring, which is also mainly used for home decoration, there is a layer of paper on its surface, after a long time using, it is easy to be color fading. Compared with solid wooden flooring, there is only limited chromatic aberration, if you are people who pay more attention on flooring color, bamboo flooring would be your wise choice because of obvious advantage.


Fourthly, better performance of environmental protection


Believe it or not, bamboo flooring would be the flooring product development direction in the future due to its better environmental protection. As you know, bamboo is one of renewable resources because as usual its growth cycle is only a few years. In order to protect the environment, renewable resources such as bamboo will be mainly used.
