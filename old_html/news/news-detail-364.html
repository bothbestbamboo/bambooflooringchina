Armstrong Flooring: A Comprehensive Look at Their Bamboo, Hardwood, and Engineered Options

Posted on 2025-1-10 7:24:03

Armstrong Flooring is a well-known brand in the flooring industry that is associated with excellence, creativity, and longevity. With more than a century of experience, Armstrong is known for creating flooring solutions that satisfy the requirements of both homes and businesses. Bamboo, hardwood, and engineered flooring are all part of their wide range of products, and each has special qualities and benefits. Let‘s examine more closely what makes Armstrong Flooring a reliable option for both professionals and homeowners.

Armstrong Flooring‘s Story


Thomas Armstrong started the company in 1860 as a modest cork-cutting enterprise in Pennsylvania. Armstrong became one of the most well-known brands in the flooring industry over the years. Their dedication to innovation has greatly influenced flooring trends and technology. Even though the company has expanded into other product categories, its flooring segment continues to be a vital component of its success.

The core values of the brand are performance, aesthetic appeal, and environmental responsibility through the use of premium materials. Armstrong Flooring‘s sustained success in the market is evidence of its capacity to develop and adjust while keeping the demands of its clients in mind.

Examining the Product Types of Armstrong Flooring

 Although Armstrong has several flooring choices, its bamboo, hardwood, and engineered wood floors are especially well-liked. Because each variety meets various functional needs and design preferences, they are adaptable options for a range of applications.

Bamboo Floors

 Bamboo flooring has drawn a lot of interest due to its distinctive appearance and sustainability. Armstrong‘s bamboo flooring is an environmentally beneficial substitute for conventional hardwoods because it is made from bamboo plants that are renewable. Bamboo flooring is a great option for high-traffic areas because of its reputation for strength and resilience to wear.

From light natural colours to deep, rich hues, Armstrong bamboo flooring comes in a range of treatments and tones. Because of its strand-woven design, which guarantees increased strength, it is appropriate for homes with kids or dogs. Additionally, whether you‘re a professional contractor or a do-it-yourself enthusiast, Armstrong‘s simple locking mechanisms make the installation process easier.

Hardwood floors

 Armstrong‘s collection of hardwood floors is an ode to classic style. These flooring, which are made from high-quality wood species like oak, maple, hickory, and others, add warmth and natural beauty to any area. Armstrong hardwood products are versatile for a range of subfloor conditions since they come in both solid and engineered formats.

Armstrong‘s exclusive protective coatings are applied to the hardwood floors to improve their resilience to moisture, stains, and scratches. Customers can tailor the look to fit their interior design by selecting from a range of finishes, such as matte, semi-gloss, and high-gloss. A common option for homeowners wishing to give their houses more personality and value is Armstrong hardwood flooring.

Wood Flooring Engineered

 Engineered wood flooring offers improved stability and durability along with the beauty of real wood. Compared to conventional solid wood flooring, Armstrong‘s engineered wood floors are more resilient to moisture and temperature changes because they are made of layers of actual wood that have been glued together. They are therefore a great choice for spaces like kitchens and basements where humidity levels can fluctuate.

Armstrong provides engineered wood flooring in a variety of designs, ranging from exotic woods like acacia to traditional oak. Its adaptability is increased by the fact that engineered wood can be installed as a glue-down or floating floor. Armstrong‘s engineered wood floors are also made to last, featuring a wear layer that can be repeatedly sanded and refinished.

Armstrong Flooring‘s Standout Products

 Armstrong offers a wide range of products, but several standout choices have gained popularity among customers because of their performance, quality, and design.

1. The Hardwood Brushed Collection

 Customers who value the organic texture of wood are likely to choose the TimberBrushed collection. These floors have wire-brushed finishes that add a fashionable, rustic feel while showcasing the wood grain. TimberBrushed, which comes in a range of wood types and colours, is ideal for establishing a warm and welcoming ambience.

2. Hardwood Performance Plus

 This series is a fantastic choice for busy households because it is designed to last. Armstrong‘s acrylic-infusion technology is a component of Performance Plus hardwood that improves the wood‘s hardness and scratch and dent resistance. The floors will continue to look beautiful over time because to the additional layer of protection provided by the UV-cured finish.

3. The Rigid Core Luxe Plank

 Armstrong‘s Luxe Plank series is noteworthy for its gorgeous wood-look designs even though it isn‘t a real product. These high-end vinyl planks look like real wood and have a stiff core for extra stability. They are also very water-resistant. It‘s a great substitute for places where spills or dampness are common.

The Reasons Armstrong Flooring Is Unique

 Armstrong Flooring stands out from rivals thanks to its commitment to sustainability, innovation, and quality. From the eco-conscious homeowner seeking bamboo flooring to the design-savvy individual wanting engineered wood with a distinctive finish, their flooring solutions are made to satisfy the wide range of demands of contemporary consumers.

Armstrong stresses environmental responsibility in addition to the products. Organisations such as FloorScore have certified several of their flooring lines, guaranteeing minimal volatile organic compounds (VOCs) for better indoor air quality. Furthermore, the brand‘s emphasis on sustainable manufacturing methods and renewable materials shows its dedication to lessening its influence on the environment.

A Tradition of Artistry

 Armstrong Flooring is a unique option for anyone thinking about bamboo flooring, hardwood, or engineered flooring because of its heritage and wide range of products. The company has maintained its reputation as a reliable name in the flooring sector by successfully fusing innovation and tradition. Armstrong Flooring offers solutions to fit a variety of tastes, price ranges, and practical requirements, whether you‘re remodelling an existing room or creating a brand-new one.
