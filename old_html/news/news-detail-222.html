What is Bamboo Board? What are the characteristics of bamboo board?

Posted on 2022-6-21 8:24:56

Bamboo board, also known as moso bamboo plywood, bamboo board, bamboo furniture board, bamboo panels, bamboo plywood. The surface is similar to wood board and carbonized bamboo board, with beautiful surface, fresh bamboo pattern, natural color, pleasant bamboo fragrance, elegant texture, high cracking, strong elasticity and good toughness. According to the production process, it can be divided into natural bamboo board, carbonized bamboo board and zebra bamboo board; according to the bamboo belt structure, it can be divided into flat-pressed bamboo board, side-pressed bamboo board and I-shaped bamboo board. Bamboo board, vertical and horizontal bamboo board, bamboo veneer, multi-layer board


The invention relates to a bamboo board, which is a bamboo board and a square material formed by gluing and pressing a processed thin bamboo sheet. The bamboo plate of the present invention is usually produced by Nangang with a large diameter of 4-6 years, which is sawn, sliced, rough planed, high-temperature cooking, high-pressure carbonized, dried, finely planed, color-separated, and pressed into a blank. Good performance, low water absorption, no cracking, no deformation and so on.


In order to fully remove the sugar and fat in the bamboo and enhance the strength and stability of the bamboo board, the bamboo fiber is subjected to high temperature and high pressure steaming and carbonization treatment, so that the carbon fiber bamboo fiber tissue is extremely enlarged, thereby ensuring sufficient insecticidal sterilization. It greatly improves the hardness and strength of the bamboo board, and improves the mildew resistance of the bamboo board.


What are the characteristics of bamboo board


1. Characteristics of bamboo board: the diameter of bamboo is small, the wall board is hollow, and the sharpness is large. The structural composition of bamboo panels is very different from that of wood. The strength and density of bamboo boards are higher than those of ordinary wood, and the strength of bamboo products is higher than that of ordinary wood products. Bamboo products are smaller than wood products when used as a structural material. Bamboo board has straight texture, smooth texture, simple color, easy bleaching, dyeing and carbonization, which can be comparable to some broad-leaved trees. Bamboo wood composite floors and bamboo floors can replace precious hardwoods and have broad application prospects in furniture, decorations and interior decoration.


2. Good mechanical, physical and chemical properties: Bamboo laminated bamboo board, as a new type of furniture base material, maintains the physical and mechanical properties of bamboo board, and has the characteristics of low shrinkage rate and low expansion rate. It has the characteristics of large size, small deformation, stable size, high strength, good rigidity and good wear resistance. It has the characteristics of sawing, planing, sharpening, opening, drilling, grinding, assembling and surface decoration. Because the bamboo laminated bamboo board has certain hydrothermal, sugar removal, decarbonization and carbonization treatment, the finished product has good sealing performance, which can effectively prevent insect frogs and molds. Bamboo laminated bamboo board adopts modified UF resin glue, which is lower than the free nail of wood-based board, and has good environmental protection.
