Take an Advantage of Bamboo Flooring

Posted on 2021-2-28 14:49:08

Bamboo flooring is a kind of flooring which is mainly manufactured from the plant of Bamboo. It is a kind of species which is mostly used for flooring because it is having the same physical similarity of true hardwoods. This type of flooring indicates the strength along its durability part which supports eco-friendliness along with its natural resistance in terms of insects and its capacity to absorb moisture.


It is the grass which grows faster and highly renewable in nature. It can be harvested without the need to replant because the roots get intact while it is being harvested and grows itself when they get proper environment and conditions to grow. It is an eco-friendly source of material.


It is not wood but it is the grass native to China which is mainly cut into strands along with strips. It is available into different styles along with variety and colours.  This type of flooring are available in 2 colours which includes natural  that will include blonde along with tones of yellow colour and carbonized one which will offer light along with tones of dark brown.  The best part is that all the accessories of the bamboo flooring can be easily matched which can provide finish touch to the room.


There are the different types of bamboo flooring which will cover down


Strand woven bamboo- It is harder than wood flooring. It is woven together which is totally compressed under heat along with pressure in order to form the plank flooring. The beautiful designs and patterns can be created to make it appealing. It is durable and can be used for a longer period of time.


Horizontal bamboo- In this type, bamboo strips are glued together in horizontal form for plank flooring. In this type the grains of the bamboo are evident and it will show wider on the surface of the floor. It is cut into small, thin strips. The layers of lacquer are added to the surface to the surface to protect and finish the floor. The flooring is wider and the grain pattern is more evident.


Vertical bamboo- In this type, bamboo strips are glued together in vertical form for plank flooring. In this type of flooring the bamboo grain is thinner and it is less evident on the floor surface.


Some of the advantages of Bamboo flooring


It is referred to as one of the hardwood flooring which was mostly used by the people in earlier times. They are durable in nature and will give a contemporary look. It is easy to clean which can easily be cleaned with the mop along with mild soap. Apart from that bamboo can be refinished which can be easily depends on the planks thickness. It also takes you closer to nature and gives you an aesthetic appeal to the flooring. 


They are eco –friendly in nature and it will not be planted again. They are farmed in such a way as root remains intact meaning that will continue to grow in future. They are inexpensive in nature where people will not have to spend extra money to get their flooring done. They tend to be harder and it will prove more durable in nature which will benefit the person for a longer period. They can be looked after easily and it can be installed quickly.  Apart from that they are easy to fix and can be done over an underlay.


We can say that this type of flooring is best for residential one which is easy to clean and best option for allergy sufferers as they don’t face any kind of dust or dust mites. Due to its natural beauty, it is also used in restaurants and hotels to give you a better feel of connecting with nature. They are also used in resorts which are constructed in the outskirts of the city and create a difference in the feel. The bamboo flooring is a highly recommended concept and people use it intensively in their homes or offices to give a natural look to the space. It is soothing to the eyes and creates an aura to work better. It is used to feel closer with nature and feel its relevance in our life.
