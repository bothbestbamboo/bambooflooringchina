01 Who we are

Bothbest is a FSC certified bamboo factory based in China, manufacturing bamboo flooring, decking and plywood since 2001, selling bamboo worldwidely, gaining a good reputation, providing one-stop purchasing service of bamboo, from indoor to outdoor, from ground to ceiling.

Bothbest has been recognized as a reliable, trustable, preferred bamboo supplier and is pleased to work with importers, distributors, designers, architects, contractors and builders from all over the world.

02 What we have

Bothbest offers widest varieties, consistent quality, unbeatable pricing of bamboo, including flooring, decking, panels, plywood, veneer, lumber, mats, cabinets, countertop, fence, pole and related creative bamboo. All of our bamboo have been CE certified to be environmental and healthy.

Bamboo flooring includes solid, strand woven, click and engineered. Decking includes standard, xtra and black color. Panels thickness is from 3mm to 80mm. Mats include various dimensions.

03 Why choose us

Bamboo is considered as sustainable and renewable resource due to the plant‘s rapid growth. As a grass, bamboo is mature and ready to be harvested in 4-6 years compared to hardwood which requires 25 years to grow. Bamboo can grow as much as 24 inches in 24 hours.

Bothbest is an experienced and helpful flooring expert, providing quality guaranteed bamboo products and outstanding customer service. You save by buying direct from the manufacturer.
